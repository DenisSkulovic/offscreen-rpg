## Research worth reopening

- Presence, consequence and ownership of a lived history: Now and Journal as complementary experiences, contextual World/You, quiet scenes, truthful absence and blocked/uncertain states. A living stage when playing and a living edition when remembering; not a compulsory fantasy skin or humanoid dashboard.
- Compare Living Codex, Responsive Stage and Field Edition on identical narrative specimens, including abstract/nonhuman and mundane scenes. Preserve the recognizable reader-book hypothesis with adaptive surroundings/light; final art direction remains unselected.
- Published source, semantic narrative document and physical leaf are distinct. Measured layout is disposable; no lost/reordered text, fixed-character pagination or mandatory page-curl package. Compare continuous reading and measured pages; preserve exact source-based anchors during reflow, resize, late media and new publication. Rich narrative score with bounded exact quote anchors remains an unratified alternative.
- Current verified choice identity stays separate from history and narration; never hide long choice groups or infer action from decoration. Enlarged mobile text and six intentions challenge fixed leaf geometry. Test keyboard/focus, reduced motion, forced colors, zoom, selection, bidi/Unicode, narrow layouts and long history. Source authorization and history/fork boundaries remain independent.
- Optional presentation cannot change committed effects, offer IDs, command/failure/status semantics or accessibility. Unknown/malformed direction/media fails locally. Source-bound history, rights, stale/duplicate rejection and spend fences survive. Paid creative trial and rich score remain separately gated.
