Current authority: [documentation rule](../../.agents/rules/documentation.md), [navigation standard](../../.agents/references/documentation-navigation-standard.md), [workflow](../../WORKFLOW.md). No independent mandatory preload or second specification.

The original lean snapshot `f3e3bfe35490932ee363c00aad0fb239893d825e` contained 73 product/technical Markdown files, 70 substantive pages, about 129,339 words and 25 pages above 2,000 words. The audit inspected corpus headings/first screens and selected high-risk sections, not every paragraph or fresh runtime proof. Navigation success does not establish semantic truth. Findings: benchmark versus selected acceptance ambiguity; conflicting realization status; normative/realization/history mixing; competing ownership; missing claim-local dependencies; unclear modal language; unratified security/privacy/retention policy accidentally acquiring authority; weak adversarial examples. Reassess current source before treating any dated finding as open.

DOC05–DOC24 all have Done entries and archived packets in the [Change ledger](../../work/changes/ledger/2026-11.md). DOC07 explicitly did not adopt the proposed full vision rewrite; later solo-first and world-neutral decisions supersede its historical assumptions. DOC24 left the held-out comprehension set unexecuted. No blanket semantic certification follows. Historical dispatch is retired; no new Change commission.

## Retained evaluation method (not run)

For material editorial evaluation, freeze comparable before/after sources and independently key answers against ratified owners. Held-out reader questions cover authority classification, normal and adversarial outcome, proposal versus selected target, actual source realization, cross-page dependent impact and locating exact proof with bounded reads. Report critical omissions, wrong answers, unsupported certainty, headings/pages opened and reviewer-disputed interpretations. Do not combine these into a universal quality score or equate green link checks with semantic agreement. Reopen only for a concrete comprehension/drift problem; no mandatory recurring benchmark.
