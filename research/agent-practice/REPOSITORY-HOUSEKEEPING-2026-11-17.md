## Enduring lessons

Derive inventory from Git, not a competing manually maintained registry. Check reachability by lifecycle class, distinguish missing history from freshness, and separate structural integrity from semantic truth. Historical evidence may be reachable at collection level. Prefer actionable limited brief output, exact owner/review dates for exceptions and bounded dispositions rather than notification floods. Replace unique operational capabilities before deletion; preserve environment/authority/spending fences and negative tests when refactoring. Retain migration snapshots, active content/test consumers, thin adapters and unique evidence. Large code and stale-looking names require ownership investigation, not automatic deletion. No old dispatch sequence grants current execution authority.
