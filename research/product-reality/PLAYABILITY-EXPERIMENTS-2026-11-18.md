## Semantic correctness probes

Distinguish committed mechanics, published narrative evidence, proposed semantic knowledge, admitted versioned knowledge, selection and actual model input. A source-backed promise is not an automatically executable obligation; a failed transfer cannot earn a gift/favor; a rumor is attributed testimony, not established truth; incidental anonymous people need not become permanent entities. Reproduce these questions against the eventual Storyteller, Library and authority design, not old module names or schemas.

| Probe | Discriminating failure |
| --- | --- |
| Successful gift and promise | Committed holder change exactly once; narrative and eligible relationship source agree; later recall not inferred from storage |
| Omitted significant promise | Published passage survives but required semantic witness absent; distinguish missed proposal from failed admission or retrieval |
| Failed transfer | No holder change; no promotion of false gift/favor as an earned consequence |
| Rumor / incidental crowd | Attributed evidence remains separate from canon; no mandatory materialization of every mention |
| Restart and recall | Exact authorized version and body reaches final input after unrelated turns; handle/index hit insufficient |
| Stale, hidden, duplicate | No leak, duplicate effect, or mutation of newer branch truth |
| Exhausted / uncertain budget | Preserve committed mechanics; no fabricated narrative or hidden retry |

Record each causal arrow: accepted intention and receipt → committed time/effect → task source → proposed narration/knowledge → admission and publication → durable source → selection → actual input → response/hold. Label source-only, design-only, tested and blocked independently. Compare precise task guidance and bounded source selection before adding narrow semantic cues; a separately charged extraction pass remains an unratified alternative requiring measured benefit. No general scanner or extraction agent selected.

Empty proposed changes may omit a meaningful relationship. Database publication, admission restart and budget path were NOT RUN; no full semantic filter or live recall proven. The Harbor GS-01–07 and Mira Vale gift/promise fixtures were separate, not a demonstrated composite. Preserve this finding as dated evidence, not an assertion of a current defect. ## Human evaluation protocol (REAL03, NOT RUN)

Gate paid play on provider-free causal integrity and current authorization. A prospective small original solo world tests whether the player voluntarily continues, sees meaningful tradeoffs and consequences, and recalls source-backed social details. Record missing reasonable intentions and whether alternatives satisfy them; menus may be correct but uninteresting. Freeform creation remains available under current product owners; no new arbitrary-world reliability pledge or rights grant follows.

A: historical proposal 20–30 accepted turns, unbriefed player, quiet interval, meaningful decisions, declined/failed offer, interruption and return. Per turn record receipt and time, provider attempts/repair/discards, input/output tokens, cost, latency, publication, continuity errors, agency friction, novelty, enjoyment (historical 1–5 rubric), desire to continue, and manual intervention. Predeclare human tolerances, spend cap and stop conditions. No false effects or leaks; correct recall and worthwhile experience must both be assessed.

B: compare admitted closed-browser quiet work and a legitimate interruption against immediate montage; historical proposal 20–30 real minutes, accelerated diagnostic distinctly labeled. Record exact work identity, start/due, elapsed fictional time, hold/resume, cost during absence and return, duplicate/retry behavior, restart and subjective value. Do not force an event or authorize a new choice because the player is away. A waiting tax is a product failure even when timers work.

Economics: count all settled charges, known failures, repairs, discarded rounds, retrieval/embedding charges and explicit uncertain liabilities; distinguish accepted decisions, useful published turns and worthwhile returns. Measure context growth, exact reads/omissions, latency, quality per extra cost, and hard per-operation/session/round limits before spending. Zero accepted turns is failure with cost. Compare cheap one-shot and bounded exploration only where evidence warrants; no hidden retries. If integrity fails repair causal chain; if recall fails inspect creation through final input; if dull redesign choices; if cost fails reduce work rather than trust; if absence lacks value reconsider pacing. Historical sample $0.023294 not current pricing. Publish dated source-linked evidence and negatives under existing evidence owner.
