# Product reality | concluded audits and open research questions

Historical research, not product authority. Two November 18 audits led to completed PVAL01–06 governance. Current owners: [product](../../docs/product/README.md), [vision](../../docs/product/foundations/vision.md), [technical](../../docs/technical/README.md), [STATUS](../../STATUS.md). Original audited baselines `ec8c652c5b0a401fb90a40b015ea4b612ae0a7ba` and `43d5ddfc068aced3825e25692ec1b2bb14259e2a`; static snapshot audits, not fresh gameplay or paid evaluations.

- 31 product pages and 46 high-impact claims reviewed. Authority, desirability, coherence, feasibility and economics are distinct. PVAL promoted solo-first, retained multiplayer as later aspiration, separated world-neutral invariants from speculative mechanisms, labeled scenario stages and proof limits, and added optional claim-impact guard. Current canonical text and guard source inspected; no guard rerun or gameplay proof.
- **Semantic middle:** creative systems decide which enduring identities, objects, promises, relationships, spatial facts and changes deserve materialization; deterministic systems only enforce declared admitted effects. Evaluate missed materialization, false promotion (especially failed transfers), identity confusion, contradictions, recall and exact branch history. Distinguish proposed, admitted, published and retained evidence. No assumption that an agent framework, more context, schema validation or vector search solves this. Reopen with Storyteller, document/Library and game authority owners when target boundaries converge.
- **Coherence:** narrow authored worlds can coexist with world-neutral architecture; avoid universal humanoid stats. Bounded menus can be correct but uninteresting: measure omitted reasonable intentions and consequential agency. Accepted offscreen work may progress without model polling, not unconsented choices. Place, route, journey and presentation differ. Compare minimal representations against failure cases; avoid premature multiplayer/admin/cinematic gates. [Full coherence analysis](https://github.com/DenisSkulovic/offscreen-rpg/blob/c51119c505fcb3dddd87119a734594277840ee17/work/designs/open/product-reality-reconciliation/COHERENCE.md) retains alternatives, not requirements.
- **Economics:** historic single settled Sol attempt $0.023294, not a distribution or current price. Measure total cost per accepted worthwhile turn including failed/discarded work, repairs, retrieval, repeated input, reasoning and attributable maintenance; cost during absence and return, latency, quality and evidence coverage. Zero accepted turns is a failed run with cost. Separate provider-free custody/causal proof from explicitly funded human quality evaluation. No historical price or threshold is a release promise.
- **Governance:** authority and evidence maturity are independent. Preserve binding consent, source truth and financial safeguards. A staged benchmark is not a logged run. Bounded falsifiable experiments, not automatic claim approval by static analysis.
