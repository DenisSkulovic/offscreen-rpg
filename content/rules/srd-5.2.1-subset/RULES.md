# SRD 5.2.1 Offscreen subset

This package is the compact reference for mechanics currently implemented by the `srd-5.2.1-subset` adapter. It is not a complete D&D edition implementation.

Use the task's mechanic tags to load the matching page. Do not load every page by default.

- Ability checks and difficulty: `checks/ability-checks.md`
- Advantage and disadvantage: `checks/advantage-and-disadvantage.md`
- Hidden event occurrence: `checks/event-occurrence.md`
- Long-running contribution: `activities/contribution.md`
- Fictional seconds, committed duration and optional calendars: `time/committed-time.md`
- Storyteller and engine authority: `authority/storyteller-and-engine.md`

Rules derived from SRD 5.2.1 are used under CC BY 4.0. Project-specific time, event and activity policies are identified on their pages.
