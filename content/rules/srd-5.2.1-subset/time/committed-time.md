# Committed time

Every campaign has a monotonic integer fictional-second coordinate. A finite action or activity boundary commits a positive fictional-second duration before resolution. Narration cannot retroactively change that duration or make the same resolution consume time twice.

A calendar is optional. When present, it is a projection from fictional seconds using the campaign's admitted calendar definition. Worlds without days, months, daylight or conventional chronology still use the same monotonic coordinate; no calendar fields are fabricated for them.

Schedules, deadlines, daylight restrictions and timed opportunities are content or rules expressed against fictional seconds and, when available, calendar projections. They are not hard-coded assumptions about employment, humanoid sleep or Earth-like days.

The scheduler's real-time heartbeat is not a fictional unit. When content supplies an exact duration, preserve it. Otherwise estimate each action independently from its fictional effort and the world's scale; do not copy a nearby action's duration merely because it is the only visible number. This rule supplies no universal human, planetary or biological timing defaults. A world or scenario may provide its own bounded calibration examples.

This page describes Offscreen time policy rather than an SRD rule.
