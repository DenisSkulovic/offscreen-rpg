# Committed time

Every campaign has monotonic integer ticks. A finite action or activity boundary commits a positive tick duration before resolution. Narration cannot retroactively change that duration or make the same resolution consume time twice.

A calendar is optional. When present, it is a projection from ticks using the campaign's admitted calendar definition. Worlds without days, months, daylight or conventional chronology still use ticks; no calendar fields are fabricated for them.

Schedules, deadlines, daylight restrictions and timed opportunities are content or rules expressed against ticks and, when available, calendar projections. They are not hard-coded assumptions about employment, humanoid sleep or Earth-like days.

This page describes Offscreen time policy rather than an SRD rule.
