# Contribution activities

A contribution activity represents durable progress toward a content-defined target. Its definition names the target contribution, productive cadence, one validated ability check, and bounded contribution for success and failure.

At each eligible boundary, the engine resolves the captured check and adds the selected bounded contribution. Elapsed ticks and earned contribution are separate values. Time passing alone does not complete the activity, and failure may consume time without adding contribution when the definition says so.

Pausing, interruption or switching work preserves an unfinished commitment unless an admitted rule explicitly abandons, invalidates or reduces it. Resumption refers to the same activity identity and earned progress.

This contribution lifecycle is an Offscreen extension. Its embedded ability check uses the SRD-derived check rules in this package.
