# Advantage and disadvantage

When a validated ability check has advantage, roll two d20s and use the higher result. With disadvantage, roll two d20s and use the lower result.

If both apply, they cancel and the engine rolls one d20. Multiple sources of advantage or disadvantage do not create additional d20 rolls. Sources must be represented by the admitted plan or current state; narration alone cannot add them after the roll.

This page adapts advantage and disadvantage from SRD 5.2.1.
