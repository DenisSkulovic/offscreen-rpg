# Event occurrence checks

An event-occurrence check answers whether an incident occurs, not whether the character succeeds at an action. The engine rolls one d20, applies admitted occurrence modifiers, and an event occurs when the total is at or below the threshold.

Occurrence checks are distinct from SRD-style ability checks and use opposite comparison semantics. They are an Offscreen mechanic for hidden or scheduled world uncertainty.

Do not expose a private occurrence roll when doing so would reveal a hidden threat or future event. A quiet interval is not evidence that the character failed at the activity they were performing.
