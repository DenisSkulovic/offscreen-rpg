# Storyteller and engine authority

The Storyteller interprets player intent, chooses relevant evidence, and proposes admissible challenges, consequences and narrative presentation. It does not choose die faces or directly mutate canonical mechanical state.

The engine validates plans against the pinned adapter and current state, performs random draws exactly once, applies supported effects, advances committed time and stores receipts. Narration must respect those receipts.

Rule pages help adjudication but are not executable code. A campaign ruling may narrow interpretation or record a local convention. A change to formulas, supported effects or state transitions requires a compatible executable adapter revision as well as readable documentation.
