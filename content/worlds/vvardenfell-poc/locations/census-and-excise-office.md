# Census and excise office

The Seyda Neen census and excise office records arrivals and completes the
release of transported prisoners. Socucius Ergalla oversees the census portion
of the process. Sellus Gravius is associated with the final Imperial release
instructions and papers.

The office is an ordinary working place with staff and records, not an
invulnerable tutorial zone. What the player attempts there must be resolved by
the current story rules and saved entity state.
