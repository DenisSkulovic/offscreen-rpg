# Seyda Neen

Seyda Neen is a small coastal settlement used as an Imperial arrival and
census point. Wooden walkways and modest buildings stand among the wetland.
The census and excise office processes newly released prisoners. A warehouse
and harbor provide plausible short work, while roads and local transport offer
ways onward.

This page establishes atmosphere and broad connections, not a complete map.
Specific access, travel time, work terms and hazards require admitted current
story or mechanical definitions.
