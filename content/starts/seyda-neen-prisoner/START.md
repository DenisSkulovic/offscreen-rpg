# Released at Seyda Neen

The player character is a transported prisoner arriving by ship and being
released through the Seyda Neen census and excise process. Begin aboard the
arrival vessel or during the escorted walk into the office. The immediate
purpose is to establish identity, complete release and let the player decide
how to meet the settlement rather than forcing a single quest.

Use the pinned Vvardenfell pages for setting facts. Preserve player freedom:
cooperation, curiosity, rudeness, delay and reckless action are all possible
intentions when supported. Do not assume a grand narrative is active merely
because the setting contains famous conflicts.
