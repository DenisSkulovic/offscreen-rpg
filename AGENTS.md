# Offscreen RPG | agent entry

**Start:** identify actual repository, integration branch, HEAD, scope, permissions and existing work. Read current applicable instructions; memory is not evidence. Product docs own intended behavior, technical docs own guarantees, code/tests/evidence show realization.

## Delivery over procedure (owner decision 2026-11-27)

**Design, planning, documentation and review exist to accelerate the delivery of correct, safe and maintainable working software. They are supporting engineering activities, not independent deliverables to be optimized at the expense of development throughput.**

A design or Change packet must be sufficiently correct, safe, coherent and actionable for its purpose. It does not need to be perfect. Planning quality is measured by successful execution with minimal avoidable rework, not by document count, review count, procedural complexity, formatting completeness or the number of concerns eliminated before implementation.

Development throughput and continuous flow are first-class objectives, alongside correctness, safety, maintainability and cost. Preserve authentication, authorization, privacy, financial and provider-spend controls, durable-data integrity, concurrency, idempotency, migration, publication and destructive-operation safeguards. Do not lower those standards. Remove unnecessary procedure, not necessary engineering reasoning.

[Finding severity](#finding-severity-and-blocking) is the blocking policy for architecture self-defense, independent committees, design reviews, Change readiness, planning-artifact quality and implementation review within each review's acceptance boundary. [Investigation depth](#investigation-depth) chooses the process. [Continuous flow](.agents/references/planner-investigation-standard.md#continuous-development-flow) owns planner batch completion. Specialist procedures apply these rules and do not invent competing gates.

## Engineering roles

Roles name responsibilities. One agent may combine them. Independence is required when a review must not reuse the author's context: the independent reviewer is not the author of the reviewed candidate. A fresh chat is one valid independent review context.

| Responsibility | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| Designer | Product or technical target, alternatives and selected meaning |
| Planner | Source investigation, packets, batches and proof plans |
| Independent reviewer | Verdict on a candidate the reviewer did not author |
| Implementation agent or coordinator | Commissioned edits, focused checks and receipts; sequences bounded tasks when the tool has them |
| Acceptance lead | Required heavy or designated acceptance of an exact revision |
| Owner or operator | Commission, ratification and live-operation permission |
| Integration custodian | Shared joins and authorized persistence of delivered artifacts |

Access and explicit authorization determine permissible mutations. A role name or client brand does not. A read-only agent delivers artifacts. A write-authorized local custodian persists them. Never attempt an unavailable or disabled write as a ritual. The [artifact handoff](.agents/references/planner-investigation-standard.md#artifact-handoff) is the ordinary route when the assigned role cannot write.

## Finding severity and blocking

Report every substantive finding, including Medium and Low findings. Do not suppress a finding because it does not block. Classify by material consequence. A violated guideline, without a material consequence, does not justify blocking. Do not raise a Medium or Low finding because a reviewer prefers a design, document structure or degree of completeness. Do not lower a genuine safety or correctness defect to increase speed. Several minor deficiencies may together be High when their combined effect prevents reliable execution; explain that combined consequence. A finding blocks only the scope it affects, including material dependencies.

| Severity | Meaning | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CRITICAL** | Could invalidate the selected architecture, or cause serious security exposure, unauthorized operations, corruption or loss of durable data, substantial financial-integrity failure, or a similarly severe consequence. | Blocks the affected scope and materially dependent work until corrected, validly resolved or explicitly excluded. |
| **HIGH** | Prevents safe, correct or sufficiently determined implementation. Examples: contradictory selected architecture; an unresolved material decision; missing essential source, interface, caller or migration mapping; unsafe concurrency, resource ownership or dependency ordering; missing essential correctness or safety proof; a credible failure that violates required behavior; instructions that force the executor to invent architecture or a consequential contract; an incoherent packet that cannot reliably guide implementation. | Blocks the affected scope until resolved. |
| **MEDIUM** | A worthwhile improvement that is unnecessary for safe and correct execution. Examples: bounded maintainability; nonessential extra tests; a suboptimal but correct and safe mechanism; avoidable complexity that does not prevent reliable execution; additional diagnostics; documentation that would help comprehension but is not required to implement correctly. | Record and defer. Does not block architecture acceptance, Change readiness, commission, dispatch or integration. |
| **LOW** | Minor imperfections, editorial and presentation issues, naming, optional examples, small redundancies and other nonessential polish. | Record and defer. Does not start another planning or review iteration. |

Only Critical and High findings require correction before the affected scope proceeds. Record deferred findings compactly in the existing review receipt or ledger. Do not create a document, Design or Change for each finding, and do not repair Medium or Low findings during the current correction cycle merely to improve the review result. A later explicitly selected activity may address them.

Missing indispensable evidence remains blocking or evidence-gated for the affected scope. Distinguish that unverified scope from a confirmed defect. Missing optional assurance is not automatically blocking. Missing authorization and required live-operation permission remain separate gates. A favorable review does not grant implementation, deployment, spending or mutation authority.

## Investigation depth

Choose depth from actual risk, novelty, uncertainty, blast radius and cost of failure. Do not choose it from the subsystem name, document type, file count or the mere presence of infrastructure, persistence or Kubernetes. Prefer the least expensive process that establishes the required confidence. Keep planning effort proportionate to implementation complexity and risk. Do not introduce time-tracking, planning-budget files or reporting machinery. [Design-led engineering](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md) owns the procedure.

| Depth | When | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Focused work under settled contracts | Local bug fixes, fixture corrections, verification repairs and small improvements whose interfaces and behavior are already accepted. | Focused source investigation, a concise execution plan where needed, and proportionate review. Do not manufacture a Design, formal defense or independent committee. A compact Change or direct commissioned implementation is enough. |
| Evolutionary subsystem development | Improving existing observability, reorganizing Helm artifacts, extending established developer tooling, or refining a package without replacing its architecture or authority model. | Investigate the affected subsystem, set the outcome, preserve relevant behavior, resolve material decisions and prepare the implementation batch. Use a compact design artifact where it adds value. Review consequential architecture, interfaces, integration boundaries, safety guarantees and acceptance obligations. Reuse accepted architecture and established evidence. Do not apply new-architecture ceremony merely because the subject is technically sophisticated. |
| Material new architecture or substantial redesign | New durable state authority, financial or provider-spend control, a new authorization boundary, a major Agent Core redesign, complex migration or distributed consistency, or a new externally exposed trust boundary. | Deep source investigation, meaningful alternatives, concrete contracts, warranted experiments, self-defense, independent committee and owner ratification. Corrections and later review follow severity and impact. |

## Submission shape

[Artifact quality](.agents/references/planning-artifact-quality.md) owns file roles and scale signals. Designs resolve coherent problems. A Change prefers one coherent measurable outcome. Delegated work normally targets 5–10 minutes of active implementation and 1–5 tasks. Seven actual tasks, including nested assignments, is a strong default boundary. A narrowly justified exception is allowed when splitting would add coordination, break an atomic correctness boundary, or increase implementation and integration risk, while child context, ownership, dispatch and acceptance stay bounded. Do not restore a monolithic packet, and do not split a coherent bounded Change merely to satisfy a count. Document counts, byte counts, word counts, time estimates and task counts are signals. Classify their consequences with the severity policy. Edit current statements in place, not amendment logs.

## Design before packets (owner decision 2026-11-26)

Material new architecture and substantial redesign follow [design-led engineering](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md): choose posture, research alternatives and pinned upstream/source evidence, make specimens and discriminating experiments, settle safety, integration and acceptance, and defend a coherent target. A redesign request authorizes scoped non-destructive investigation; start substantive work in the same turn. Respect selected clean-slate exclusions until the target is coherent; preserve adjacent contracts and data. No implied implementation, live mutation or spend. Evolutionary and settled-contract work use the depths above and do not manufacture that ceremony.

For material new architecture, after self-defense require an [independent architecture review](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#mandatory-independent-design-committee) in a context that did not author the candidate, before owner acceptance and promotion of that scope. A fresh chat is one valid realization. The reviewer delivers a complete verdict bound to the candidate SHA, integration baseline, scope, findings, evidence limits and verdict. Persistence is either an authorized direct write or delivery for the integration custodian. Distinguish analysis completed, verdict delivered, verdict persisted and verified, owner ratification, and implementation commission. A complete verdict awaiting persistence is not an incomplete analysis. Retain the verdict in the repository before relying on it for promotion or integration. The custodian verifies that the imported content matches the reviewed candidate. Do not attach a verdict to changed content. No force-push, destructive overwrite, silent candidate substitution or loss of review history. Promote accepted meaning into canonical owners before implementation commission. Choose direct planner implementation, delegated Changes or a hybrid. No artificial Design for a settled-contract fix.

## Dependency readiness is not a planner blocker

Fully settled work may be Ready (dependency-gated) before predecessors land. Explicitly commissioned tasks may dispatch after verified prerequisite receipts and source postconditions; mechanical reconciliation is allowed, architecture invention is not. PLANNER-BLOCKED means a Critical or High defect: a named material decision, authority, safety, source or semantic contradiction, or a submission whose consequences meet that severity. It does not mean ordinary dependency order, separately owned heavy proof, or deferred Medium or Low findings. Verify commission, source and the relevant branch before dispatch.

The planner's delivery unit is the complete authorized assignment. Continue through internal checkpoints until that assignment is complete or a genuine stop applies: a consequential unresolved product or architecture decision, a necessary permission boundary, unavailable indispensable source, runtime or evidence, a blocker that prevents further useful work, a material contradiction that needs owner judgment, or completion. When one part is blocked, continue unaffected authorized work. Batch owner questions and owner-operated experiments. Do not ask the owner to reauthorize work already commissioned, and do not treat a routine checkpoint, a single edit or an intermediate commit as a new decision. Do not claim background progress the current environment cannot perform.

A Ready Change is not commissioned. An explicit owner commission may name a batch. Within that batch, eligible Changes proceed on the established dependency and ownership schedule without repeated approval of each task or routine checkpoint. Dependencies are individual edges. Release an eligible commissioned Change when its own prerequisites hold; do not hold it for an unrelated Change or a whole wave. A blocked Change does not stop unrelated ready or commissioned work. Preserve separate worktrees, branch ownership, shared-file serialization, resource isolation and one serialized join at a time for each target. Architectural authority, planning readiness, dependency readiness, commission, execution, live permission, integration and final acceptance remain separate.

A sufficiently designed system, an actionable packet, implemented source, passing focused tests, live operational evidence, required integration acceptance and full certification are different claims. A future precisely specified harness is not a planning blocker. Unrun tests stay unrun. Required acceptance still has an owner and must be satisfied before the corresponding claim or integration boundary. A Medium or Low assurance recommendation does not become an indefinite implementation prerequisite. Do not waive an accepted mandatory safety or correctness proof by reclassifying it. An explicitly bounded intermediate integration is allowed when its guarantees, limitations and deferred certification are stated. Integrating individual Changes does not certify the whole system.

## Mandatory implementation boundary

**Git:** main is the current integration trunk. Design dossiers, reviews and Change packets are normally prepared on main. Material candidate review stays bound to its exact revision; a change to reviewed content invalidates the affected evidence. Storyteller Workshop is a dossier, not a second trunk. No new joins to the old branch; preserve in-flight work and do not force-push or delete an old branch before a verified migration.

When a Design is commissioned for implementation, create one `implementation/<design-slug>` branch from the verified main revision that contains the accepted design and packets. Record its target, base revision, scope and dependency schedule in the existing design implementation plan or catalog. That commission authorizes the branch creation. Do not ask for a separate confirmation only to create it. An existing explicitly authorized integration branch may stay under its current name. Do not rename or recreate it. This topology does not require an Undertaking.

Every Change that realizes that Design branches from the latest verified design implementation branch and joins back into it, on its own `change/<id>-<slug>` branch and linked worktree. No constituent Change merges into main. A standalone Change that does not realize a commissioned Design keeps the direct route to main, or to another explicitly recorded target. Never switch the primary checkout or discard existing work. The implementation coordinator runs `node scripts/repo-tools/change-branch-guard.mjs --change <ID> --target <reference-branch>` in the Change worktree before edits, adding `--design <slug>` and `--grandfather <ref>` when those apply. No per-T-ID branch. Shared-checkout children do not stage or commit. [Branch policy](.agents/references/bounded-parent-orchestration.md#mandatory-change-branch-policy).

**Planner:** [product](docs/product/README.md), [technical](docs/technical/README.md), [evidence](evidence/README.md). Verify current source, owners, callers, schemas, permissions, failure/replay, tests and migration map. Follow [Change standard](.agents/references/change-work-unit-standard.md), [packet design](.agents/references/packet-design-manual.md), [submission standard](.agents/references/planning-artifact-quality.md) and [readiness review](.agents/references/packet-readiness-review.md) before Ready. Unresolved architecture stays with the planner; implementation discovery is not a substitute for a missing design. Prepare the complete authorized batch and release independently ready work. Reconcile docs at closure; no pointless pre-code rewrite for settled details.

**Execution/acceptance:** The implementation agent implements the selected Change, constructs specified deterministic proof tools, runs focused unit/contract, changed-file quality and cheap local integration checks. The acceptance lead and owner own broad builds/suites, infrastructure lifecycle/fault recovery, multi-service/browser/end-to-end, release upgrades and long/costly probes. The planner specifies exact target, commands, prerequisites, positive/negative predicates, deadlines, cleanup/stop and evidence; the owner runs the authorized batch and the planner interprets. No heavy proof by the implementation agent unless explicitly delegated by name. Essential cheap negative tests remain required.

An unambiguous commission such as "Do OBS-D02" authorizes the routine lifecycle of that one Change: resolve its packet, target, baseline and prerequisites; establish or resume its linked worktree; run the branch guard; implement the authorized tasks; run the assigned focused, negative and quality checks; archive the completed packet and reconcile its catalog, references and transferred Design obligations; commit that closure; join when the Change-level gate passes; verify the target contains the archived packet and no longer contains the open packet; stop only owned temporary resources; retire the clean integrated worktree; and return a short receipt. Do not stop after a child task, an intermediate commit, a passing test, or a finished diff while the remaining steps are safe. Do not ask the owner to confirm those steps again. Do not start an uncommissioned successor.

At source completion the coordinator commits the exact SHA, paths, tests, known failures, remaining acceptance and resource state. If indispensable Change-level acceptance is pending or failed, stop **IMPLEMENTED — ACCEPTANCE PENDING**, keep the worktree, and do not join. A Design may allow the source join after the Change-level gate while joint acceptance is still pending. That join is not runtime certification, live activation, or a waiver of the joint proof. The Change packet is archived because its own obligations passed. Before that move, record the remaining joint obligations on the Design and do not mark them passed. The coordinator then verifies the archived packet is on the implementation branch and the open packet is absent there, records the target SHA, and retires the clean worktree. Do not report completion while the open packet remains on that target. Main may keep the open packet until the design branch lands. Commission lookup reads the implementation branch, so an archived Change is not started again. The exact source, join, tests and unresolved joint obligations stay on the implementation branch. Final integration into main belongs to the design-level closure after every constituent Change is integrated and the required joint acceptance has passed. No constituent Change publishes an incomplete Design to main. The acceptance lead and owner certify that joint proof. Cheap-only standalone Changes close on their own when their required proof passes. [Parent procedure](.agents/references/bounded-parent-orchestration.md) · [verification](WORKFLOW.md#9-verification-review-and-the-next-loop).

**Quality and authority:** [Code craftsmanship](.agents/rules/code-quality.md), changed-file formatting/lint, focused positive/negative tests, actual diff. Preserve authentication, permissions, data integrity, idempotency, financial/provider-spend safeguards. Read-only and authorized disposable checks are not permission for paid calls, protected database writes, deployments, destructive operations or secrets. Escalate architectural contradictions with file/symbol/reproducer. PASS, NOT RUN, source-inspected and live-observed are distinct. When the Cursor profile is selected, [its model budget](.agents/references/cursor-model-budget-policy.md) applies. [Documentation](.agents/rules/documentation.md) · [spending](.agents/rules/spending.md). Source health: `pnpm repo:code-health`, compare reference with --base, newly crossed severe bands --strict-new; numeric hot spots trigger review, not automatic refactor.

**Dev resources:** Change/worktree does not require running stack. Verify current commands/prerequisites in [owned dev stacks](docs/runbooks/dev-worktree-stacks.md) and [environment contract](docs/technical/platform/developer-environments.md). Source-only first; acquire only needed capacity, never bypass admission/seize foreign environment. On partial startup/pressure/OOM park only own attempted workloads; preserve reservations and SQL/S3/Temporal tenants; confirm own replicas/processes/ports parked, report contention/back off. Never kill foreign processes/remove release or infer tenant destruction authority from stopped process. Registry/lease state machine-local, not Git evidence. Tilt sole active dev Deployment image writer. No invented commands or stale status.

**Parallel work:** before splitting a Design into tasks, map its Change dependency graph in the existing implementation plan or catalog. Each Change names its real predecessors and source postconditions, its own useful outcome, the files, symbols, contracts and resources it owns, shared-file and runtime collisions, the proof required before its join, and the design-level acceptance that remains after that join. Prefer independent Changes. Serialize only for a real dependency, a safety boundary, or a shared write. Do not split an atomic transaction, authority, migration or safety boundary merely to create parallelism. The commissioned Change's coordinator carries it through the join. Concurrent joins to the same target take the repository lock in `scripts/repo-tools/change-lifecycle.mjs`. [Parallel policy](.agents/references/change-work-unit-standard.md#batch-execution-and-parallel-change-orchestration-2026-11-19).

**Lifecycle:** [Design catalog](work/designs/README.md) and [Change catalog](work/changes/README.md) own open/backlog/archive routing. Active decisions/outcomes open; deferred worthwhile work backlog with trigger; terminal archive provenance, not authority or selection. **Change placement:** Open is the current sprint/execution horizon, including every owner-intended current Change (proposed, planner-blocked, dependency-gated, Ready, commissioned, in progress or acceptance-pending). It is not limited to the next dispatch, and there is no arbitrary sprint-size cap. Backlog is genuinely deferred work outside that horizon, potentially revisited in weeks or months. Move work to open when it becomes current, before readiness or commission if necessary; do not move current blockers to backlog. Medium and Low findings stay in the existing findings record until a follow-up is explicitly selected; they do not automatically become backlog Changes. Placement, readiness, selection, commission and live permissions are separate. See [Change catalog](work/changes/README.md). Promote accepted meaning, preserve unique evidence, repair links and ledger with full SHA/path. Seven-day archive review is not deletion authorization. Never retire or mark a Change Done while its own mandatory proof is pending. A constituent whose own gate has passed is archived on the implementation branch in the same commission; joint obligations move to the Design and are not marked passed. Main may keep the open packet until that design branch lands. [Commit format](.agents/rules/git-commits.md) · [maintenance](REPOSITORY-STRUCTURE.md).
