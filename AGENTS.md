**Owner decision 2026-11-26: implementation and heavy acceptance have different owners.** Cursor implements the selected design, constructs specified deterministic proof tools, runs focused unit/contract tests, changed-file quality checks and proportionate cheap local integration checks, and commits a coherent implementation receipt. The design/planning ChatGPT and human owner own heavy acceptance: broad builds/suites, infrastructure lifecycle and fault/recovery runs, multi-service/browser/end-to-end exercises, release upgrades, long-running or costly probes and other substantial operational proof. ChatGPT selects exact commands, target, prerequisites, expected outcomes, timeouts, stop/cleanup and evidence; the human runs the bounded batch in their terminal and returns results for ChatGPT to interpret and record. Prefer one reproducible scripted batch over repeated paid-agent terminal orchestration. No heavy acceptance by Cursor unless the owner explicitly delegates that named proof; elapsed time alone is not a hard cutoff, and essential focused negative tests are not skipped to save tokens. The planner partitions the existing acceptance matrix by executor before commission (or at the next checkpoint of an in-flight Change). This changes who runs proof, not its required tier, safety, or integration gate.

When Cursor completes source work and its assigned cheap checks, it commits on the Change branch, reports exact SHA, owned paths, tests/results, known failures, remaining acceptance commands and resource/collision state, and stops at **IMPLEMENTED — ACCEPTANCE PENDING** (an open Change checkpoint, not a new lifecycle category or Done). Preserve the linked worktree and do not merge, archive or retire while mandatory heavy acceptance is pending. ChatGPT/owner take over proof without re-dispatching Cursor merely to run commands; fix ordinary source defects through a bounded implementation follow-up, and return architecture contradictions to the planner. Record acceptance against the exact tested SHA, then the named integration custodian reconciles current main, checks affected proof on any changed seams, integrates, reconciles documentation/evidence and retires clean work. Cursor may perform mechanical join/retirement after receiving the accepted proof receipt, or close cheap-only Changes autonomously when every required gate is met. No extra ceremonial approval or duplicate test sweep. This explicit responsibility split supersedes older unconditional whole-Change Cursor acceptance/closure language; never infer live mutation permission, waive essential gates, or claim unrun proof.

**Owner decision 2026-11-24: `main` is the integration trunk.** Storyteller Workshop remains an active outcome/acceptance dossier, not a separate integration branch. New Changes branch from current `main` and join `main` after their own proof, using dedicated Change branches and linked worktrees. The former `undertaking/storyteller-workshop` reference is a historical transition checkpoint; no new joins there. Reconcile in-flight branches against main without resetting or discarding user work. Do not force-push or delete the old branch until local migration is verified. This explicitly overrides older Storyteller Workshop target instructions, not safety, release, live acceptance or Change gates. The dossier closes on accepted outcome, not branch promotion.

## Design before packets (owner decision 2026-11-26)

A direct request to redesign an area starts the authorized [design workshop](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#adaptive-design-workshop-choose-the-investigation-not-a-universal-sequence), not merely its kickoff plan. Begin substantive research and scoped design-dossier work in the same turn; report findings or a meaningful decision/experiment/blocker rather than waiting for another generic instruction to proceed. Design authorization is not implementation or live-mutation authorization.

For substantial new architecture, infrastructure, domain redesign or renewal, the [design-led renewal rule](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md) is mandatory **before Change planning**. Design is a real, iterative engineering phase in `work/designs/open/`, not a quick preface to TASKS.md. Invest in understanding, alternatives, first-party research, concrete specimens, discriminating experiments and repeated critical review. Do not assume existing implementation is valuable or use its internal structure as the target. For explicitly selected clean-slate work, defer reading the replaceable implementation until a coherent target is established; inspect adjacent contracts and constraints, then audit integration, compatibility, data and retirement before implementation. A user-directed exclusion of legacy source must be respected; unresolved seams remain explicit.

**Independent design committee:** a fresh chat assigned this role must follow the [committee protocol](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#mandatory-independent-design-committee), including committing every completed verdict to the reviewed design branch before returning its final response. Formal submission follows passed self-defense and a feasible proof matrix; early owner-requested consultation is non-accepting. Negative and preliminary reviews are not exempt; a chat-only verdict is incomplete. Read the current integration-branch rules even if the design branch predates them.

After design convergence and necessary owner acceptance, choose the cheapest appropriate execution route: direct planner implementation with owner-operated terminal experiments, a bounded Change handed to Cursor, or a hybrid. Cursor is optional, not the inevitable next step. Design verification alongside the architecture and freeze integration-critical versus extended proof before commission. All implementation routes retain Git ownership, documentation, safety and proof requirements. No automatic permission for destructive actions, live mutations or paid calls. Small fixes to settled contracts remain narrow.

## Dependency readiness is not a planner blocker

Owner direction (2026-11-24): distinguish design readiness, execution dependency order, commission, and live-resource permission. A settled packet with frozen interfaces, selected algorithm, scope and proof is **Ready (dependency-gated)** even when predecessors have not landed. An explicitly commissioned batch may dispatch each named packet as soon as its recorded predecessor receipts and source postconditions hold, without further planner promotion, certification, or owner approval. A parent may verify those postconditions and mechanically reconcile import paths, manifests, symbols and test paths under a frozen mapping; this is execution, not delegated architecture discovery. Do not require a pre-existing test harness when creating it is the selected task. Source implementation and deterministic tests proceed independently of separately gated live proof. Use **PLANNER-BLOCKED** only for a named material unresolved design, algorithm, authority, safety, or semantic contradiction that prevents correct execution; state exact missing decision/evidence and affected task, not a generic readiness disclaimer. Unfinished predecessors, normal integration sequencing, or absent future source implementing an already frozen contract are not planner blockers. Preserve all actual safety, correctness and resource permission gates. Before recommending `do ID`, ChatGPT must ensure the repository packet is actually commissioned and executable now or explicitly dependency-gated, and verify the push. No repeated approval for an unchanged commissioned contract. Every authorized execution route continues through the agreed scope, including direct ChatGPT work: an acknowledgment or intermediate checkpoint is not a deliverable. Stop only at completion, a material decision/permission boundary, a necessary owner-operated check, or a concrete blocker. See [verification and integration](WORKFLOW.md#9-verification-review-and-the-next-loop). This rule clarifies all readiness/review language below; it does not waive source-grounded planning or permit uncommissioned sibling work.

**Identify the actual repository, branch, HEAD and scope before work.** Follow this file first; its links identify the owners, not optional competing rulebooks. Do not assume `main`, infer permission from a proposal, or claim to have inspected inaccessible material.

## Mandatory implementation boundary

**Git:** One short-lived `change/<id>-<slug>` branch per commissioned implementation Change, created from the **actual recorded integration branch** before edits, not automatically `main`. A **separate linked worktree is mandatory for each commissioned Cursor Change**, even if it is the only chat. Never switch the user's primary checkout to a Change branch. No branch per T-ID. Parent integrates accepted work into that same reference branch and retires the temporary branch. Run the read-only pre-edit check `node scripts/repo-tools/change-branch-guard.mjs --change <ID> --target <reference-branch>` before dispatch; see [branch and integration owner policy](.agents/references/bounded-parent-orchestration.md#mandatory-change-branch-policy).

**Plan before paid execution:** Product docs ([docs/product](docs/product/README.md)) own selected behavior; technical docs ([docs/technical](docs/technical/README.md)) own guarantees; code/tests and [evidence](evidence/README.md) show what exists. Accepted new product/architecture contracts must be in their permanent docs **before** implementation commission. Planned implementation follows [Change work-unit standard](.agents/references/change-work-unit-standard.md) and [Composer task contract](.agents/references/composer-first-execution.md); unresolved architecture stays with ChatGPT. For ordinary implementation details and fixes to already accepted behavior, reconcile docs by closure, not via pointless pre-code rewrites.

**Recurring source health:** The [code-craft remediation program](work/designs/open/code-architecture-health/README.md) tracks source-architecture candidates. `pnpm repo:code-health` is the read-only AST growth report. A Change parent compares its source against the reference branch at closure with `--base` and, for newly crossed severe bands, `--strict-new`. Numeric hot spots trigger review, not automatic refactor.

**Code craftsmanship:** [One canonical quality standard](.agents/rules/code-quality.md) governs readable control flow, meaningful invariant comments, explicit boundary types, runtime validation, typed results, narrow responsibility and focused changed-file Prettier/quality-lint checks. Passing TypeScript or tests does **not** establish readability or security. Do not make Cursor produce opaque mixed-responsibility controllers merely because a packet specified working behavior.

**Execution readiness:** A packet is not Composer-ready merely because it has TASKS.md. Each selected T-ID must name exact write paths/symbols, a fully chosen edit, concrete positive/negative proofs and one contradiction stop; a multi-module move additionally needs an old→new ownership/export/caller/test migration map. Never delegate caller discovery, authorization composition, test-harness design or package-interface selection to Cursor under a `Ready` label. If those are missing, ChatGPT repairs the packet before dispatch. Read [Composer-first execution](.agents/references/composer-first-execution.md), [packet design](.agents/references/packet-design-manual.md), and the [adversarial readiness audit](.agents/references/packet-readiness-review.md) before declaring Ready.

**Cost and proof:** Cursor parent/children default to Composer Standard, use small assignments, focused tests and concise receipts; deep investigations/reviews belong to ChatGPT. Preserve real authentication, permissions, data integrity, idempotency, financial and provider-spend safeguards. `PASS`, `NOT RUN`, `source-inspected` and `live-observed` are distinct; never manufacture evidence. A Change is Done only when the accepted outcome and affected-claim reconciliation ledger are complete, with appropriate negative checks. [Budget policy](.agents/references/cursor-model-budget-policy.md) · [documentation rule](.agents/rules/documentation.md).

**Permissions:** Read-only investigation and authorized disposable local proofs are distinct from paid model calls, protected database mutation, deploys, destructive operations or secrets. The latter require explicit task-specific approval. Inspect existing work before changing files; do not reset, force-push or discard work for cosmetic Git cleanup. On an actual architectural contradiction, escalate its file/symbol and reproducer to ChatGPT rather than letting Cursor redesign.

**Parallel Changes:** Design independent Changes where practical; owner authorizes each. One integration owner serializes shared catalogs, status, technical headings, database migrations and final joins. Independent parents own their implementation branches; they do not concurrently rewrite the shared integration branch. [Parallel standard](.agents/references/change-work-unit-standard.md#batch-execution-and-parallel-change-orchestration-2026-11-19).

**Repository history:** Product/design decisions are retained in their owners, proof in evidence, terminal packets in short-lived archive/ledgers and Git. Do not keep historic design folders indefinitely. Commits follow [type(scope), Why and truthful Evidence](.agents/rules/git-commits.md). Use `pnpm repo:brief`, `pnpm wiki:find` / `wiki:read`, and targeted guards only as needed. [Architecture atlas](docs/architecture/README.md) is explanatory, not binding; [Engineering System Tour](ENGINEERING.md) is a human overview.

**Design lifecycle (owner decision 2026-11-27):** `work/designs/open/` holds active investigations with a named owner, unresolved decision and review trigger; `work/designs/backlog/` holds deferred worthwhile investigations with a reason and reactivation trigger; `work/designs/archive/` holds terminal accepted, completed, rejected, cancelled or superseded dossiers as a browsable review cache. Placement never authorizes implementation, live mutation or spending. Accepted decisions must be promoted to canonical owners; implementation and acceptance remain with their Changes. Record disposition, exact original full SHA/path, canonical successor, unique evidence and repaired references in the design ledger. Review archive retention after seven days by default; preserve unique evidence and require explicit authorized pruning, never age-only removal. Git remains permanent recovery; archive is not canon or a shadow queue.
