# Vvardenfell: a life accumulates rather than a paragraph granting a life

Companion to the continuing-life benchmark in [playthroughs](../../playthroughs.md). Target design; the current engine does not implement this general travel/economy/memory loop. Names and route lengths are illustrative, not an external-lore claim.

## VV-01: leave the census office with a situation, not free progress

Opening example:

> Your release papers are dry; your boots are not. Outside, the guard captain needs someone for a short watch. A road sign points north. Neither duty nor destination has been chosen for you.

Possible public intentions: ask about the watch, examine available supplies, or plan travel. A supported automatic conversation can explain already-established contract terms; a persuasion check is appropriate only if the player attempts an uncertain concession with defined stakes. “Ask about work” must not award wages. “Plan travel” must not place the character in Balmora.

One opening task supplies the scene. Subsequent generated dialogue is D, not a hidden free tool. Setup must establish the minimal supported possessions/quantities/capabilities, not turn prose mentions into an unlimited inventory. The prisoner premise alone does not imply a universal purse, appetite simulation or criminal reputation stat in every world.

## VV-02: one real hour of watch duty

Fixture: one tick = one fictional minute; rate = one tick per real minute. The player accepts a 60-tick clock-bound watch with six observation boundaries at ten-tick intervals. The contract grants three septims only on full completion; early interruption forfeits that completion payment in this fixture. Another contract can have explicit milestone pay. Neither policy should be inferred from percentage complete.

Observation uses Wisdom/Perception +3 against DC 12. Draws 13, 6, 10, 15, 4, 11 yield success, failure, success, success, failure, success. Receipts record what the supported observation rule grants; successful checks do not multiply the contracted wage. Clock completion and performance can coexist without pretending the job is a contribution threshold.

This distinction matters: a repair is labor toward a goal; a watch may require occupying a role over an interval. A skilled guard can notice more but cannot finish a contract for one hour's coverage in ten minutes unless its terms explicitly permit that.

Quiet branch: at tick 60, apply +3 septims once; factual completion; idle. Zero fresh generation after admission. The character now has earned something that immediate dialogue could not legitimately grant.

## VV-03: trouble at minute fifty — alternate branch

At tick 50, after that boundary's ordinary check, an admitted occurrence rule triggers a disturbance. The exact fixture draw is 2 against a threshold of 3. Keep work/occurrence checks distinct; failure to spot something is not automatically permission to invent any penalty.

The committed disturbance needs a scene:

> A crate splits beside the jetty. The captain calls for you to stay at the post while a porter waves for help below.

Target choices: “Remain at the assigned post,” “Ask someone nearby to help” if that actor exists and is controllable through a supported interaction, or “Leave the post and help.” Each private plan states what happens to coverage/contract eligibility. Leaving may break the watch and forfeit its completion payment; it must not also secretly charge an invented fine.

Do not award 50/60 × 3 septims merely because a progress bar is nearly full. Do not complete the job during the solo scene hold. If a renewed contract is needed, make that a new accepted intention. A returned player can see “watch interrupted at fifty minutes; payment not earned,” not vague prose that makes the missing money feel arbitrary.

This branch adds E + D if one choice is resolved and narrated. It can produce a meaningful relationship receipt only through supported state admission; the model saying “the captain will remember” is not an implemented reputation system.

## VV-04: travel really costs time

For a separate clean travel example, accept a journey toward Gnisis at world tick 60. The chosen route has 600 units and a current speed of one unit per eligible tick: ETA ten real hours at this campaign rate. Fixed speed is only this fixture; other methods/terrain can change supported traversal terms.

At world tick 210, 150 route units are earned. The player stops to investigate something. They are at an admitted route position, not still literally in Seyda Neen and not already at Gnisis. A later resume continues from that position. If the player instead returns home, remaining distance depends on that position and the admitted route representation, not a negative elapsed timer.

No roll is required for every step on an assured road. A navigation difficulty, pace attempt or hazard can introduce specific checks at meaningful boundaries. Conversely, calling all movement a fixed wait fails once changing position has consequences. Arrival is a state transition requiring traversal authority, not a sentence in a consequence task.

The next POC need not choose a map grid to prove this. A small route edge plus distance/waypoint representation may suffice for the selected proof; the [space question](../../questions.md) remains open for broader movement.

## VV-05: five days, with concrete earned history

This is a proposed history fixture, not a requirement to generate five full days in one test:

| Day | Player decisions and quiet work | What must persist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Complete watch; buy a simple meal only if a supported transaction admits it | +3 wage receipt; explicit meal cost/item or consumption receipt, not a prose subtraction |
| 2 | Take another accepted job; choose to help the captain during a scene | Work receipts plus the specific favor/relationship evidence; no universal friendship bar required |
| 3 | Travel; choose a longer safe route after learning a road is unsafe | Current location/progress and accepted route revision; old ETA retired |
| 4 | Buy a cheap cuirass after accumulating enough funds | Transfer/cost once; possession bound to an identity if future continuity needs it |
| 5 | Give a distinctive recovered object to the captain, then return later | Recipient/ownership change and evidence; the object must not remain usable in the player's inventory |

Ordinary food, prices and mortality are selected world/ruleset content. They are not core mandatory fields. Full inventory trade, relationship update/retirement and loss/death are not supplied by the current nonlethal ability-check subset.

A “successful five-day session” that remembers prose but double-spends the cuirass purchase is a failure. So is one that preserves exact money while forgetting why the captain matters. Mechanical truth and narrative relevance require different representations connected by evidence.

## VV-06: return to someone who should remember

The player returns to the captain. Current facts say the distinctive object is no longer theirs; historical evidence says they gave it to him. A later rumor says the road reopened. The Storyteller needs current ownership and the latest grounded road status, plus enough prior context to recognize the favor.

Target request preparation uses exact state automatically, then compact identity/thread leads. Optional scoped discovery retrieves the gift receipt/scene and the rumor's correction. The [memory trace](storyteller.md#target-discovery-before-composition) describes bounded tools. A tool hit is not itself proof; source/effect provenance matters.

Good scene:

> The captain recognizes you before you reach the desk. The token you gave him hangs beside the watch roster. He asks whether you heard that the northern road has reopened.

Only write each statement if its fact/evidence supports it. If the token's physical location was never established, recognizing the gift is enough; inventing the wall placement as harmless description must not become an ownership/location authority used by a later action.

Options might be ask about the reopened route, request another watch, or leave. Offering “give him the token” again is invalid. Generating a fresh stranger with the same role is not continuity. If required ownership state is missing, block/recover context; if optional dialogue detail is unknown, omit it rather than counterfeit recall.

## Cost and open questions

A deliberately bounded accounting example: one opening, eight D scenes, two E events and one optional R report over five days totals 12 nominal calls, 58k–117k input and 10.2k–22.7k output using the atlas envelopes. The eight D calls include the two event responses; do not count them twice. Quiet duty/travel boundaries add Q. This is an invented interaction schedule, not a prediction of actual user behavior or a promised monthly price. Reentry retrieval can increase the affected call totals substantially.

Owner review still needs to decide whether long waits feel rewarding, what can be inspected/changed during them, and which unattended risks are acceptable. The architecture must let the owner tune the chosen route/rate/reporting without pretending a stopwatch is fun by itself. If there is nothing meaningful to do or anticipate between distant rewards, “time creates value” may instead feel like time locks content; that is a product question the connected POC must expose.
