# Microbe and abstract consciousness: no hidden human-shaped engine

Companion to the small/abstract benchmark in [playthroughs](../../playthroughs.md). The first microbe check is grounded in the current fixture; extended activities and the consciousness example are target behavior. This is an architectural contrast, not a biological simulation or proposed game of microbe economics.

## NH-01: current microbe opening

The current seed has Constitution 14 and Wisdom 12 applicable; other D&D scores exist but are not currently applicable. `environment-sensing` is a declared proficient skill, proficiency +2; HP 5; facts `exposed=true`, `gradient-disrupted=false`. There is no required currency quantity or inventory of human equipment.

The scripted opening offers:

- “Follow the weaker gradient”: Wisdom/environment-sensing DC 12, success sets `exposed=false`; failure remains exposed.
- “Contract away from the disturbance”: assured protective response text, no current mechanical effects.

Fixed check draw 8 + 1 Wisdom + 2 proficiency = 11 fails. The receipt records failure without inventing lost limbs or a dropped wallet. A D task must describe the environmental situation and propose applicable options. It may not switch to a Strength/Persuasion plan simply because those abilities exist in the generic ruleset.

The current first action is immediate: no travel cursor is created. If an extended migration is desired, it needs traversal terms rather than a retrospective claim that this check silently consumed hours. Conversely, an immediate microscopic reaction should not require a job row just to fit the activity architecture.

## NH-03: interruption, switch and return

Alternate branch: at tick 8 a scheduled environmental occurrence draw 1 against threshold 2 commits nutrient-access lost. Further absorption becomes blocked at 2/6. An E scene may help the player choose, but the condition changes before narration. A failed generation cannot continue feeding the organism as though nothing changed.

The new situation must explicitly offer migration B and authorize the later relevant A resumption; neither is inferred merely from environmental eligibility. B completes after four eligible ticks at world 12, establishing access to another nutrient pocket. Resume exact A only with that current authorization and valid target/retention terms. If A represented a place-bound colony structure, its work would stay at the old target, not travel with the organism. The definition must distinguish these cases, not infer them from the word “growth.”

With retained personal growth, A still has 2/6, and its next full four-tick contribution attempt occurs at world 16. No world-clock rewind to A's old start-plus-local-cursor. Same authority proof as [BC-04](beacon.md#bc-04-b-actually-advances-before-a-resumes--partly-current-target-chronology), without repair tools, harbor credit or a human body.

The proposed occurrence/context packet describes gradients, access, progress and applicable capabilities. It should not demand one agent per nearby organism or full fluid dynamics. Only continuity-relevant entities/conditions need explicit representation.

## NH-04: a consciousness weaving a connection — target

A bodiless entity maintains one fragile connection and wants to establish another. “Place” here is an admitted relation between domains, not necessarily a 3D coordinate. It has applicable Wisdom and an authored `attunement` skill; Wisdom 16 gives +3 and declared proficiency +2. No Strength-based physical labor is assumed.

Scene:

> The old link still carries a faint answer. A second pattern is close enough to reach, but holding both would stretch your attention.

Options: reinforce the existing link, begin attuning to the new one, or release the old link. Private plans identify which relation is targeted and what capacity is required. A content-defined resonance capacity of two units permits a one-unit ongoing maintenance participation and one unit of attunement; it is not automatically two hands or two action slots for every character.

New-link work requires four points, cadence three ticks, Wisdom/attunement DC 14, success +2 and failure 0. Fixed draws 6 + 5 = 11, 13 + 5 = 18, 9 + 5 = 14 complete at tick 9. Each tick is an authored “phase”; two real seconds per tick means eighteen active seconds. Completion admits the new relation only if its target is still valid. An estimate does not create the link early.

If maintenance of the old link consumes the whole capacity after a supported disturbance, new-link participation blocks rather than producing free parallel labor. Releasing the old link is an explicit supported decision with persistent consequences. An LLM cannot repair the conflict by narrating that the entity suddenly has infinite attention.

An optional milestone at the second successful contribution can generate a reflective report. It need not introduce an enemy or an interactive menu. If a supported event exposes a meaningful choice, a different follow-up can hold. Abstract tone changes the prose, not the authority boundaries.

## What this contrast must rule out

Reject shared code that assumes every worker has hands, uses food, receives money, travels between map tiles, rests eight hours, or can perform every D&D skill. Also reject a supposedly generic design that strips D&D of all useful capabilities because microbes cannot use swords.

Applicability is explicit; supported rules retain their depth where appropriate. Identity, exact time, participation, typed progress/effects, admitted choices and evidence are reusable. Physiology, presentation scale, quantities and metaphors belong to content/rulesets. No per-species subclass hierarchy or arbitrary model-authored executable rule language is needed.

Cost: the current opening plus failed check is O + D (9k–18k input, 1.7k–3.6k output under hypothetical live envelopes). The admitted quiet growth branch is Q; the interruption/response branch adds E + D. The abstract quiet work is also Q, with R only if its selected report policy requests it.
