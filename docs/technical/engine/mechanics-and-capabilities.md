# Rules, admitted actions and storyteller consequences

**World-neutral target:** Game Authority can admit narrative-only choices in worlds with no humanoid sheet, while typed mechanics remain authoritative wherever a prepared experience enables them. Capability packages are optional domain law, not a universal D&D substrate.

## Supported mechanisms and the future kernel

### Selected target: optional capability packages

Mechanical authority is world-neutral and optional. The engine admits narrative-only choices with no rule package. A world that enables typed mechanics validates every proposed effect against its pinned capability package, and a capability that is absent is not granted. The rule path is pure and deterministic: availability, admission, one captured check, one branch, effect identity and the dice stored with the receipt. The Storyteller proposes and the engine admits; prose never grants a mechanical effect, and a lucky or unlucky roll never turns an inadmissible action into an admitted one. The [world-neutral capability kernel](selected-architecture.md#world-neutral-capability-kernel) is a selected conceptual target and this page does not treat that kernel as live. Authority split, product side: [Game rules](../../product/gameplay/rules.md#authority-split).

### Legacy realization: the offline authored slice

The implemented slice is the typed roll and effect path in [Content and authority](#content-and-authority) and [Admitted action and time](#admitted-action-and-time). It keeps one captured check, one branch, effect identity, and dice stored with the receipt. The effect vocabulary is `fact.set.v1`, `item.create.v1`, and `item.transfer.v1`. `item.create.v1` introduces one bounded keyed item. `item.transfer.v1` moves an item only when the declared prior holder still matches. Ordered effects and the passage commit in one transaction (the existing technical owner for atomic publication is [Admitted action and time](#admitted-action-and-time) and [Consequence narration](#consequence-narration)). **Prose and descriptive canonical-document changes alone never create or transfer possessions.**

**Scoped D&D subset (fixture rules, not a general rules engine).** Six abilities (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA), modifier `floor((score - 10) / 2)` (`packages/game/src/checks.ts`), named skill proficiencies, proficiency bonus and hit points. An ability check is d20 + ability modifier + proficiency when applicable + supported situational modifiers against a DC. Advantage and disadvantage are the standard pair (they cancel when both are set; `resolveCheck`). Natural 1 and natural 20 do **not** automatically fail or succeed on these ability checks. No mandatory currency, wage or profession. Event occurrence is a separate d20 at-or-below contract, distinct from the action-success check.

**Named fixture worlds.** Opening mechanical rehearsal is proven with the deterministic pineapple, microbe and beacon fixtures. They prove the boundary, not general model quality. Existing narrative stories are not silently assigned character sheets.

**Immediate actions and processes.** Immediate actions and ability checks share one receipt path (see [Admitted action and time](#admitted-action-and-time)). A literal wait is the supported activity process `clock-wait.v1`. A traversal family is a different process meaning and is not `clock-wait.v1`. Long-running work contributes rule-defined progress at meaningful tick boundaries through a captured contribution contract (target, cadence, ability check); failed or check-only boundaries can consume fictional time without contributing.

**Exclusions (not delivered, not claimed).** Explicit abandonment, full combat, study and level progression, live-model evaluation, private encounter disclosure and the bounded planning agent. Provenance for the facts in this subsection: product [Gameplay rules](../../product/gameplay/rules.md) headings Authority split; First rules subset (POC); Implemented entry and honest limits (PD02 frozen transfer; the product text stays until the product integration verifies this successor). Source anchors read for this page: `resolveCheck` (`packages/game/src/checks.ts`), `applyOutcomeEffects` (`packages/game/src/effects.ts`), `settleProcessBoundary` (`packages/game/src/activities.ts`), `selectOfferAction` (`packages/game/src/offers.ts`). These constants and effect kinds are not inferable from `effects.ts` alone: the formula, advantage and natural-roll rules are in `checks.ts` and the `clock-wait.v1` process in `activities.ts`.

**No applicable body capability.** `resolveCheck` in `packages/game/src/checks.ts` throws before drawing a die when the ability is absent from `applicableAbilities`. Plan admission in `packages/game/src/immediate-actions.ts` records `unavailable-ability` for that case. `packages/game/test/actor.test.mjs` reads a microbe subject and expects a Strength attempt to fail with "not applicable". That test was read at `88d685aa` and was not re-run. A model suggestion does not apply the rejected effect.

**Valid mechanical consequence.** The same test resolves a Wisdom check that the microbe's sheet allows, and expects one successful roll. Immediate settlement then commits that receipt once, as [Admitted action and time](#admitted-action-and-time) describes. Later narration repeats the saved outcome. It does not roll again.

The implemented mechanical slice (legacy realization) uses offer-local private immediate-action plans and the existing storyteller execution/publication lifecycle. It remains an authored, offline slice. Opening and consequence tasks now return freshly validated plans, but their scripted source recognizes only controlled benchmark state; this is not general model adjudication or world understanding. Combat, private encounter disclosure and the bounded planning agent are not implemented. Those limits do not change the product requirements in [Game rules](../../product/gameplay/rules.md).

## Content and authority

Content can define lore, typed capabilities, rules packages, opportunities and presentation guidance, but each role keeps distinct authority. A Storyteller may establish descriptive original-world facts when the creative policy permits. It may not turn prose into an executable grant.

A world with typed mechanics validates proposed effects against its pinned adapter/capability package. A narrative-only world may admit choices whose consequences are fictional state rather than dice/inventory operations. The engine must not fabricate a fantasy character schema merely to represent that interaction.

Fork portability applies to **fictional commitments** and state, not execution machinery. If a scene says a caravan is halfway to a destination or an offer remains open, that semantic state must be representable at the scene boundary. A provider attempt or worker lease that happened to advance it is not inherited by a child branch.

## Admitted action and time

The player submits an offer identity and path, never replacement mechanics. A public leaf exposes a label, attempted intention, optional apparent risk and only `{ kind: "attempt" }` as its action payload; its ID is an opaque reference within that offer. `game_offer` stores the private plans under the same offer identity, story and narrative revision. The campaign separately persists versioned situation authorization tied to that exact offer: either no extended activity access or the exact process/resume action keys the Storyteller selected. Under the story lock, admission checks command deduplication, current revision, absence of unresolved narration, exact current offer and authorization, private-plan fence, prerequisites and active commitments. A fabricated, unauthorized or stale public selection cannot recover a plan or roll.

Immediate settlement directly commits a durable action receipt, optional roll, effects, updated character, consumed offer, command receipt and outbox follow-up atomically. It creates no zero-duration activity and advances no tick. Storyteller context preparation happens afterward from the saved receipt. A preparation failure therefore leaves the admitted result visible and retryable without another roll; the consumed offer prevents a later action from overtaking it.

An immediate plan resolves at selection time. Automatic actions apply their admitted outcome; checked actions roll once and select the admitted success or failure branch. An explicitly authorized process plan instead creates a version-6 activity at zero effort and typed rule progress, captures the current settings revision, consumes the offer and authorization, records command idempotency and schedules the activity in the same transaction. It does not create an immediate receipt, narrate completion or grant completion effects. DCs, branches and effects never enter the public offer.

The clock and process progress are distinct persisted values. Strict rule dispatch currently supports contribution and clock wait. At each productive cadence, contribution resolves one captured D&D ability check and records it under the reserved `process-contribution` identity. Its branch awards bounded work toward the content-defined requirement; elapsed time alone cannot complete it. Clock wait instead advances eligible ticks toward one positive target and completes without a compulsory roll or fictional work points. Both can share separately scheduled environmental checks. Completion effects apply only when the selected rule's predicate is satisfied and the boundary is not interrupted.

The conditional completion estimate is derived from current progress, cadence, the exact d20 success distribution and the captured success/failure contribution. It is presentation only. Rate-mode clock settlement is not capped at that estimate, and instant mode advances only to the next meaningful boundary; a run of failures can legitimately move the estimate later without stalling the process clock. A controlling world obligation is one of those boundaries: instant settlement moves the campaign clock to its due game second and leaves the later check unapplied.

Each schedule has its own cadence and check identity. Ability checks succeed at or above DC; event occurrence uses a separate d20 at-or-below threshold contract, with captured modifiers. Event occurrence is not character failure. A boundary can run both contribution and a separate environmental check. Due schedules run in authored order; the first interrupt stops further schedules and future progress. An interruption retains already committed progress. Immediate encounter responses can change the facts without abandoning that progress. A later private resume plan matches one eligible retained row by action-definition identity and rechecks character prerequisites; ambiguous matches reject. Admission reanchors that row rather than creating a replacement. Starting a different process suspends an encounter/paused activity; selection while one is running rejects. Explicit abandonment and instance-specific resume offers remain unimplemented.

The worker settles at most 24 boundaries in a transaction, then schedules remaining work. Each roll stores raw dice, modifiers, captured inputs, result, effects and tick position under `(operationId, segment, checkKey)`. Story locking and the activity cursor fence duplicate workers. Pause/speed changes settle due work using the old rate before applying the new rate. Earned whole ticks and an exact rational remainder survive reanchoring. If more than one batch is due, the first batch commits and the control returns conflict/refresh while catch-up continues; it does not claim to have applied. No model call occurs in a timing transaction or per routine tick.

## Consequence narration

At a completion or interruption, the transaction saves mechanical consequences, a plain outcome passage, the new offer and a `consequence` storyteller task. Its bounded context includes current character/facts, committed receipts, available actions, continuity evidence and the current creative settings. Intermediate routine boundaries update authoritative activity progress, roll history and the public campaign projection without appending a story passage or advancing narrative revision. A completion, interruption or block still establishes a meaningful factual passage before any configured report or controlling scene.

That unconditional completion handoff lacks the proposed configurability. The [historical activity foundation](../../../work/designs/archive/legacy-initiatives-index/PARKED-2026-11-15.md#activities-participation-and-progress) separates mechanical settlement from accepted reporting/continuation policy. The [historical bounded-autonomy proposal](../../../work/designs/archive/legacy-initiatives-index/PARKED-2026-11-15.md#bounded-autonomy-and-re-entry) records chain and follow-up admission options (quiet transition, completion or milestone narration with continuation, interactive scene preparation); those remain unratified configurations, not current authority. Consequence narration remains valid; it must be selectable rather than mandatory. Count generation admissions separately from provider charges and test the zero-call configuration explicitly.

The new task uses the existing generation, outbox, scripted/provider execution, budget accounting, output validation, publication and explicit retry facilities. It has a version-3 scene contract: prose plus `available` or `held` opportunities. It cannot return an interval or reroll. Zero opportunities means held, never an ended life. It proposes fresh plans, and publication independently revalidates every plan against the captured character, facts, quantities and evidence before saving the new public/private offer pair.

Publication is fenced by story revision and generation identity. It appends a passage with generation provenance and publishes continuity notes. Until publication succeeds, action admission holds. Mechanical settlement commits first; later context preparation or execution failure leaves that outcome visible and retryable. Once a task is admitted, explicit retry reuses the same task and receipts. It cannot re-enter mechanical resolution. Profile changes apply to subsequently admitted narrative tasks, not to already captured requests.

The offline consequence source repeats the deterministic outcome summary and selects authored state-responsive plans for the pineapple and microbe benchmarks. Unknown mechanical state holds rather than receiving a generic filler action. These fixture branches live outside generic application policy and do not pretend to understand arbitrary tags or worlds. The provider adapter can consume the same task contract, but live calls remain disabled and unverified.

## Opportunity boundary

`@offscreen/game/opportunities` filters captured private plans against authoritative prerequisite facts and returns separate public offer and private-plan collections. Persistence saves the private collection before making the public offer current. Consequence publication performs the same proposal validation and availability checks before creating a new immutable offer identity. This is explicit constraint filtering, not a semantic proof of tactical plausibility. A real Storyteller still needs human quality evidence after producing valid packages from scene evidence.

Contrasting authored examples enter through the same preview, private-plan lookup, resolver, receipts, narrator and recovery boundaries. They prove that an example can omit quantities, currency, human calendars, anatomy and geography without changing runtime logic. Their mechanics remain authored content until the planning agent is connected.

**Scene frame candidate (unratified).** From the product page [Continuity — Places, scenes and cinematic presentation](../../product/gameplay/continuity.md): the initial scene representation is a bounded `scene-frame.v1`, not a map or universal entity system. Candidate content is a durable place reference or temporary place description; fictional time and temporary environmental conditions; present durable participants and salient durable objects; a small set of meaningful spatial relations; and current focus and atmosphere for generation. The Storyteller may propose an updated frame with a DM turn, and application code validates references to durable identities and authoritative relations. Prose may add disposable texture. This is a candidate representation at the opportunity boundary, **not** a chosen map schema, not an implemented record and not a new authority: a frame or camera instruction is never a prerequisite, an effect or evidence that an event occurred. Transfer provenance: PD02 frozen row for continuity.md (the product text stays in place).

## Prepared local opportunities

**Selected contract.** Every interactive scene explicitly authorizes its activity access: either no extended activity, or the exact process/resume keys it selects. That authorization is versioned, tied to the exact offer, and replaced only by a controlling scene. Reusable definitions never carry their old menu forward. Repeat and limit terms are explicit rules, never universal penalties. The product promise of prepared local life includes the later limits named below.

**Legacy realization (bounded solo path, source-observed).**

- Mechanical opening and consequence scenes authorize no extended activity or select every process/resume key in the current offer. Versioned situation authorization privately retains unconsumed extended plans while work runs; a one-shot selection is removed, and switching work atomically rebinds a retained exact-instance resume to the suspended revision.
- Quiet completion rechecks prerequisites and retained-instance state, then saves a fresh public/private offer without a generation intent.
- Explicitly repeatable definitions create fresh instances. A limited definition records terminal completions in durable story-scoped occurrence history; reaching its limit removes later projections, and an unfinished limited instance reserves the scope so switching cannot duplicate it. Starts, suspension and abandonment do not spend a completion. A controlling scene replaces authorization but cannot reset occurrence history.
- Strict Storyteller process plans may propose supported contribution or clock-wait definitions and exact retained resumes; validation and publication remain authoritative.
- Story scope is the only supported occurrence scope; actor/site scope waits for durable identities rather than accepting unverified string keys.
- Not delivered: irrecoverable invalidation and broader target/resource conditions (owned by the activity foundation), and queues/follow-ups (owned by autonomy).

Activity prerequisites declare whether they are checked only at admission or again before every authoritative process boundary. Boundary-rechecked conditions are the first supported continuous policy: if one becomes false, the instance becomes blocked before another attempt or terminal reward, retains its exact identity/progress and opens the configured scene follow-up. Restoring the fact does not silently restart it; a later current situation must explicitly authorize its exact resume. This is intentionally narrower than arbitrary event subscriptions: admitted commands settle due work before changing relevant facts, and scheduled activity effects are rechecked in the same settlement transaction.

### Definitions, availability and offers are different

- **Activity definition:** captured supported rule/version, parameters, requirements, outcomes and follow-up hooks. Reuse the terms, not an old successful outcome or old dice.
- **Local opportunity:** a story-scoped, versioned grant to propose that definition with specified targets/bindings in a place or situation. It includes declared start/scope conditions, repeatability, known limits and evidence. It does not grant the character new capabilities or control rights.
- **Current offer:** a short-lived public projection of Storyteller-authored choices authorized for the current situation, filtered by mechanical eligibility. Its private plans retain the usual opacity and stale-command protection. A consumed offer is never made reusable to avoid a model call; the application can project another offer without inference only within the still-valid authored authorization. Inventory/capability matching must never synthesize additional choices.
- **Work instance / participation:** the admitted attempt at extended work. Repeating fishing can create a new instance; returning to unfinished repair references the exact retained instance. A reusable definition is neither one immortal job nor permission to reset meaningful work.
- **Accepted chain:** permission to start particular future work without another player click. Availability in a local menu alone authorizes no unattended execution.

These are separate responsibilities, not a requirement for five new tables or abstractions. Start with a small bounded scene/place scope and existing typed rules. No global action taxonomy, generic expression interpreter or arbitrary generated code.

### Preparing and refreshing a situation

An opening, arrival or event task may propose a bounded local opportunity package together with relevant supported world declarations. Admission validates references, rules, capability applicability, costs/results, scope conditions, repeat terms, wake hooks and evidence. Publish the package and its required state changes atomically against the captured source revision. The Storyteller cannot refer to a tavern or tool it merely invented in prose as though an authoritative target already existed.

Revisiting a known place can reuse its definitions as preparation input, not automatically expose its old menu. Each new interactive situation's publication explicitly selects its offered opportunities, carries forward specific authorized references, or permits no activity starts. Omitted references are not implicitly inherited. A prepared continuation can capture this decision in advance, but it remains Storyteller/authored content, not an engine inference from place identity or mechanical feasibility. Record the situation authorization and originating task/content revision. An opportunity revision does not silently rewrite an in-progress instance's captured mechanics.

Bound preparation to what is useful now. Fishing rules plus a small prepared observation set are reusable content; dozens of prewritten branching futures are not required. Future general world/definition generation still needs its own validated result contract; the existing opening/consequence schema must not be advertised as already producing these packages.

### Contextual curation with deterministic enforcement

Availability is the intersection of the current Storyteller-authored selection/authorization, the admitted opportunity's scope/conditions, and mechanical capabilities/resources/control. Re-evaluate on projection and under the start transaction lock, including the situation authorization revision. A stale browser button fails before any roll, debit or work start. Mechanical feasibility can remove an option; it cannot add one absent from that authored selection. Storyteller authority over the menu cannot override unsupported effects, missing resources or another actor's control rights.

Represent only bounded supported conditions over declared state: presence/access, enabled opportunity scope, required capabilities/resources, admitted time window, and relevant scene/phase restrictions. Do not evaluate arbitrary prose such as “when narratively appropriate” at every click. An unknown condition either needs supported preparation or cannot authorize that opportunity. Local context need not be a geographic place: a microbe's nutrient pocket or an abstract connection phase can supply the same boundary.

Temporal conditions use the same rule discipline. Keep absolute availability, recurring phase windows, deadlines and minimum process cadence as distinct typed predicates because they have different boundary behavior. A predicate references the campaign's admitted tick/calendar/cycle definition; it never assumes a terrestrial day or derives executable arithmetic from labels such as “day shift.” Capture whether the condition is checked only at start or remains continuously required, plus its supported closure behavior. The Storyteller can propose “warehouse work is available during the admitted day phase,” but validation must reject it unless that phase and the chosen behavior are representable. Once admitted, projection and the locked start/boundary operations—not repeated model judgment—enforce it.

For the locked-confrontation fixture, the Storyteller may offer only the current scene's intentions and authorize no routine starts. It need not enumerate everything the character could physically do or invent locked doors to justify a focused menu. Where closed routes or unsafe rest are actually established, those also constrain admitted mechanics. Opening a route restores departure only if the current authored selection already includes that conditional option; it cannot by itself reopen activity access. There is no `if location == Red Mountain` rule. Narrative selection still must respect established facts, accepted risk and meaningful player choice.

### Scene cadence and explicit return to activities

A scene publication must state its activity authorization rather than rely on absence of a blocker. The conceptual choices are no routine starts, or an explicit bounded selection of referenced opportunities with validity terms; this is a responsibility, not a mandated new mode hierarchy. Quiet factual receipts and historical report-only publication are not new interactive situations and do not reset a continuing authorization. Its declared expiry/invalidation still applies.

The Storyteller may continue scene → response → scene for several turns, each with freshly authored intentions and no activity access. Resolving a decision clears only that decision's hold; it does not implicitly reinstate the old local selection, automatically resume suspended work or run a queued successor. Continued work requires both the player's accepted permission and current situation authorization. Any intended resume/chain handoff must be explicit in the admitted transition; invalidating access preserves earned work and history under the activity lifecycle.

The same Storyteller can later explicitly reopen selected activities, reusing validated definitions where appropriate. The engine never balances scene/routine frequency by inserting gameplay options. It enforces configured spending, deadlines and simulation time; it does not add artificial delays to instantaneous actions or accelerate genuine travel to make a scene feel faster. If the next required scene cannot be prepared, retain a legible system hold rather than expose an unauthorized routine menu as a fallback.

Explain known blockers without leaking hidden facts. Incompatible routines cannot bypass a controlling decision, but pause/stop controls follow their own authorization and are not removed by narrative curation. If nothing is eligible, distinguish a deliberate hold, an understood temporary block and missing preparation; do not silently charge generation for every empty-menu refresh.

### Start eligibility is not ongoing validity

An opportunity that was available when it was shown is not guaranteed to be startable now. Availability is re-evaluated at projection and again under the start transaction lock, so a stale button, a changed fact, a consumed offer or a revoked authorization fails before any roll, debit or work start.

Each relevant condition declares whether it is start-only or continuously required. Settle work through the admitted change boundary before blocking, interrupting or invalidating incompatible participation. A lockdown can therefore prevent a new sleep and interrupt existing incompatible work without retroactively undoing earned results. Merely hiding its button is not enforcement. A changed package can revoke future starts; cancelling existing work additionally needs an explicit lifecycle cause/policy.

Repeat cycles obtain new receipts/draws at their meaningful boundaries. Resource consumption, finite prepared findings, quotas and any cooldown/diminishing-return semantics are explicit content/rules, not mandatory universal penalties. Persistent occurrence/consumption state belongs to its declared actor/site/story scope; stopping and restarting cannot reset it to farm draws or reveal the same supposedly unique rumor repeatedly. Completed rewards are never reissued from cached generation output.

### Dormancy and wake admission

Reading/projecting local opportunities, starting/repeating admitted work, supported switching/resumption, deterministic availability changes and quiet settlement admit no generation task unless a selected hook requires one. Scripted execution of an unnecessary generation task does not satisfy this property.

Supported wake sources include occurrence results, milestones, admitted deadlines and relevant state-change events. “At any moment” means the corresponding authoritative boundary can trigger a wake, not continuous LLM polling or one task per world mutation. Register explicit relevant triggers; deduplicate by cause/hook identity, apply captured budgets/cooldowns, and coalesce compatible pending causes without losing committed hazards or allowing one busy source to starve a required interaction. Initial implementation needs only a small selected subset.

The wake packet includes the trigger receipt or candidate, current scoped opportunities and revisions, active/retained work, material outcomes since preparation, character/world state, unresolved interaction and relevant memory. Resolve through the existing budget/recovery fence. Candidate-versus-committed hazards and optional-report-versus-required-scene behavior follow the autonomy contract. No budget or provider availability does not invalidate unrelated prepared routines; however, an already-admitted controlling hazard cannot be bypassed just because inference is unavailable.

Returning generated output is validated, then published against current state. It may narrate and propose explicit revisions, but cannot replay routine rewards, rewrite past dice or resurrect a retired opportunity from a stale snapshot. Once no unresolved interaction needs generation, the system can return to dormant local play without a mandatory farewell scene or new task.

## Persistence and known limits

**Resumption, hidden rolls and private fact transitions (transferred from product [Gameplay rules](../../product/gameplay/rules.md#resumption-hidden-rolls-and-private-fact-transitions), PD02 frozen row).** Later resumption of unfinished work references the same commitment, preserves earned progress, rechecks current prerequisites and reacquires its named capacity; the current runtime retains work but does not yet enforce capacity or the complete activity lifecycle, and the generic lifecycle must not invent abandonment, decay or lost progress. The public roll log shows permitted completed checks and redacts secret DCs or modifiers where they would disclose unknown facts; the full record stays private; repeated quiet checks may be grouped for presentation with individual records retained; completed activities stay inspectable after leaving the active view. On an opening preview, review exposes public labels while the generated result retains private intentions, prerequisites, checks, DCs, process rules and outcome branches, and Start copies those exact reviewed plans into the story; the browser later submits only the offer identity and selected path. **`fact.set.v1` declaration:** newly generated finite plans identify every intended write to an existing typed character fact by outcome branch; admission requires the declaration and the `fact.set.v1` effect to agree in both directions and rejects targets absent from the captured character state. Empty declarations are valid and do not make a state change compulsory. This private offer and path metadata is mechanical coherence, not a parser that derives rules from narrative prose. **Negatives:** prose that says an item changed hands neither creates nor transfers it; a reload, a narration retry or a pace change does not roll again; an approaching interruption may offer resumption of the same saved repair and must not restart it at zero progress.

The single baseline migration creates the current database directly, including immutable `game_offer` private plans, situation authorization, captured activities, rolls, effects and settings. There are no prototype conversions or compatibility DTOs. Local databases must be reset when the pre-POC schema changes. Multiple unfinished rows are retained, but the campaign pointer still authorizes only one advancing instance. Named capacity is captured without reservation enforcement. The campaign now owns one exact clock/frontier while each activity owns only eligible effort and rule progress, so resuming an old instance cannot rewind world chronology. The [historical activity foundation proposal](../../../work/designs/archive/legacy-initiatives-index/PARKED-2026-11-15.md#activities-participation-and-progress) still records participants, capacity enforcement, additional rule families and full real B-settlement evidence as unratified research.

Snapshot reads use their supplied repeatable-read transaction. Settings history is read-only. Start retry compares the original profile snapshot, not the subsequently edited story profile. Narration retries use existing generation/publication recovery. Broad typed rule domains, generated process planning, semantically grounded process modifiers and dynamic continuation of interrupted activities remain open work.

## Activity history and diagnostics

The durable activity ledger records player-meaningful lifecycle facts under the same transaction that changes activity/campaign authority. Each event has a stable identity, story and activity identity, activity revision, world tick, event kind, safe summary, structured public details and links to relevant roll/effect or command identities. It is append-only. A uniqueness fence derived from the causing command or settled boundary makes replay a no-op. The ledger is the source for activity history; current activity rows remain the source for current state, and story passages remain narrative chronology. Neither prose nor process logs reconstructs missing mechanics.

Public events distinguish `started`, `paused`, `resumed`, `suspended`, `blocked`, `interrupted`, `completion-pending`, `completed`, `abandoned`, `failed`, `expired`, `invalidated` and explicit progress-loss/reset where supported. Do not emit events for scheduler polling or a boundary that made no authoritative change. Repeated quiet checks may be grouped in presentation but their roll records remain individually linked. Private plans, undiscovered blockers, secret DCs and hidden event candidates are redacted from player projections.

Operational diagnostics are a separate structured stream with stable event names, severity, timestamp and correlation fields such as story, activity, operation, outbox notice, workflow and generation identity. Expected domain rejection is not logged as a runtime error. A retryable infrastructure failure is an error with the retained retry identity; suspicious stale/duplicate delivery is normally informational or warning according to whether authority was threatened. Logs never contain credentials, full provider bodies or private gameplay payloads. Durable Chamber traces may retain bounded redacted artifacts, while ordinary process logs remain diagnostic and non-authoritative.
