# D&D baseline and mechanical coverage

D021 confirms D&D as the actual rules foundation. D033 requires mechanical flexibility across worlds, bodies and scales. This document owns adoption and coverage; edition and exact source subset remain unselected.

## Source decision

RULES-F01: Recommend evaluating SRD 5.2.1 as the initial baseline; retain SRD 5.1 as the older-rules alternative. The official source lists both. Selection must consider the intended character, required abilities and extension needs, rather than treating recency as proof of suitability. No edition-specific rule has been adopted by this recommendation. [Official SRD source](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd), checked 2026-09-16 during product definition.

An SRD is a bounded source; choosing it is not a claim to cover every D&D book or arbitrary fictional setting. Exact source text and version must be checked when a rule is adopted.

## Coverage contract

For each capability needed by the selected campaign, record the source/version and rule reference, supported behavior, exclusions, any changed behavior and its reason, and a worked normal/interruption case. Classify it as adopted unchanged, parameterized content, ordinary bounded adjudication, uncovered addition, or deliberate adaptation. These categories must not silently substitute for one another.

| Area | Required definition before claiming support | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actor preparation | Legal capabilities and resources for the selected embodied character | Source/coverage decision open |
| Checks and uncertain actions | Eligibility, applicable check, difficulty authority, costs and result | Edition-dependent rules unadopted |
| Combat and recovery | Ordering, legal actions, position/range, effects, defeat and recovery | Draft encounter contract in DOC-COMBAT; source rules open |
| Equipment and abilities | Access, limits, expenditure and effect interactions for included content | Coverage open; prose alone is insufficient |
| Travel | Adopted movement/needs rules plus explicit route and interruption additions | Draft in DOC-TRAVEL |
| Employment and production | Check for applicable source rules; identify uncovered agreement/progress behavior | Draft additions in DOC-EMPLOYMENT |
| Autonomy, continuous time and cross-scale worlds | State where adopted rules apply and where explicit extensions are necessary | Product contracts drafted; no universal replacement ruleset selected |

The first release needs a coherent supported subset, not a promise of complete edition coverage. A listed creature, spell, vehicle or body form is unsupported until the relevant behavior exists. Open scope is visible to creation rather than filled by confident narration.

## Extension boundary

Shared product concepts must remain general. A world/body profile can select appropriate supported behavior; it cannot make an undefined capability executable by assigning it a name. New behavior needs prerequisites, target/reach, cost, time, effects, interaction/counter rules and examples before being treated as reliable mechanical support. Source departures are explicit decisions, not hidden fixes to make an example work.

Release clarification (D040–D041): the initial livelihood slice adopts only mechanics needed for its supported actions. Full combat coverage is a later candidate; this document must not be interpreted as a combat prerequisite for first delivery. DOC-RELEASE-SCOPE owns release placement.
