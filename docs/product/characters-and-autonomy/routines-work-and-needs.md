# Ongoing intentions and ordinary life

Recurring character life remains central. D064 reopens whether it needs an activity engine. Recommend representing a routine as an ongoing intention and permissions that guide the same [story progression](../experience/story-progression.md) used everywhere else. This is a proposal, not an accepted routine language or schedule schema.

## Routine is ongoing behavior, not guaranteed repetition

“Live here for a while and help at the mill” can guide successive story segments. A routine need not be an ordered list of implemented work, eating and sleep functions. The storyteller can summarize repetition, notice a meaningful change and offer or select a new intention. Personality and circumstances can alter what the character does under the established control policy.

## Proposed behavior

- ROUTINE-001: Distinguish a one-off intention from permission for continued life under a general plan.
- ROUTINE-002: Reconsider meaningful changed facts before continuing; do not blindly replay a prepared day after the workplace burned down.
- ROUTINE-003: At interruption, preserve already-established elapsed time and consequences; the future remains revisable.
- ROUTINE-004: Remember explicit promises, payment and possessions where consequential without requiring payroll.
- ROUTINE-005: Do not tell incompatible completed histories for the same interval merely because two future plans were prepared.
- ROUTINE-006: Use relevant current facts to judge the next segment; no requirement to recalculate every need or location every tick.
- ROUTINE-007: Edits change future intentions, not completed history. Personality cannot grant itself authority to exceed campaign limits.
- ROUTINE-008: Continue through valid prepared/default behavior when unattended. If no legitimate continuation can be produced within constraints, disclose the exceptional block; do not claim indefinite no-model autonomy without a mechanism.
- ROUTINE-009: Reports summarize actual elapsed life, meaningful changes and exceptions. Quiet periods are valid; no obligation to log every meal.

## Needs and desires

Hunger, fatigue or comfort can be relevant narrative facts. No need meter, decay curve or recurring expense timer is required unless the player wants predictable quantitative simulation. Preserve consequential conditions and judge their effects consistently; do not invent recovery merely to resume an old plan. Different bodies have different relevant pressures without needing an engine per species.

## Options and forks

ROUTINE-F01/F02: Recommend a plain ongoing intention plus compact permissions and variable narrative granularity. Detailed schedules are later candidates. ROUTINE-F03: Prefer qualitative relevant conditions before need meters. ROUTINE-F04/F05: Replan after interruption and adjudicate compatibility when relevant rather than requiring a catalogue of every possible activity combination. Exact defaults remain open.

## Open work

Test whether the character feels recognizably alive and autonomous across a few story segments and a long quiet interval. Measure calls and costs, including re-planning. Do not make a month's perfectly balanced livelihood economy the acceptance gate for narrative continuity.
