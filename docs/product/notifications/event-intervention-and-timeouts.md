# Notifications, player decisions and timeouts

User direction now places phone notifications and simple player choices in the initial web-platform loop, not merely a later possibility. This topic owns what an invitation means. Channel/OS details, exact frequency and deadline defaults remain unselected.

## Initial experience

Activity completion can create a plain factual update: arrived at mine, work began, shift ended, payment received. Distinguish information-only updates from choices awaiting the player. Narration may be generated by code; no model is required for each notification.

A decision identifies the actual current situation, allowed choices and applicable fallback. The interaction can open the mobile web app at that decision or, under D053, accept a supported action directly in a connected messaging interface. Neither route is selected universally. Do not require operating-system notification action buttons for initial support. The in-app event/decision list remains usable if push is unavailable, muted or delayed.

Selecting a choice invokes the same eligibility, resource, location and control rules as an ordinary in-app command. Old notifications can display resolved history but cannot replay a payment, travel departure or completed decision. Show what happened and which actions are still available. Concurrent or repeated submissions have one authoritative resolution.

## Proposed policies

D057 selects continuing delegated life as the defining direction: routine transitions and ordinary unanswered invitations resolve through permitted autonomy rather than routinely pausing at each decision. Arrival and completion may be informational. Explicit player pause remains available; exceptional missing-permission/invalid-state stops are not the normal interaction loop. Optional intervention holds require a deliberately selected policy, not an assumption. Notification preferences remain distinct; muting a phone does not increase autonomy.

D050 brings timed choices into the proposed initial experience; the concrete contract below replaces categorical deferral. Manual pause stays indefinite. A device acknowledging delivery is not proof of attention. Do not turn a delayed notification into a retroactive choice.

Coalesce repetitive updates, respect contact preferences, and keep important pending decisions discoverable. Exact quiet hours and frequency defaults are open. Do not create an LLM-generated message for every tick or every NPC event.

## Availability requirement and validation

The requested initial experience requires progression and event delivery opportunities while the browser and player's computer are closed, when the campaign is intentionally running and the service is available. A web service must not rely on a foreground browser tab to count ticks. This states the experience, not a selected hosting architecture. Service interruption, global pause and user absence remain distinguishable; recovery must avoid duplicate progress, wages and messages.

Test: a player closes the app during a journey, receives an arrival/shift update, opens the current decision on a phone and selects a new destination. Repeat with push denied, delivery delayed, a duplicate click, no response and a service restart. Record actual progression and delivery behavior; do not promise instant or guaranteed push delivery.

Web push is a candidate, not a chosen integration. Apple's documentation describes Home Screen web-app push support on iOS; device/permission requirements must be verified on the user's actual phone during technical work. [Apple web-push documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/usernotifications/sending-web-push-notifications-in-web-apps-and-browsers), checked 2026-09-16. No phone OS has been assumed and no notification permission has been requested.

## Routine digests

D041 adds recurring-life reports as a central use case. Recommend configurable digest cadence and optional arrival/work notifications, with exception alerts for unresolved decisions. A 30-fictional-day report need not generate 30 pushes or 30 model calls. Its totals derive from recorded outcomes, including spending categories and failed steps. Indicate report interval and current campaign time; delayed delivery must not imply the campaign is still at the notification's old state. Notification frequency and autonomy remain independent controls.

## Draft timed intervention contract

The player can configure permitted autonomous responses and whether eligible incidents offer a response window. The invitation shows the situation, currently valid options, response deadline and what nonresponse permits. Fifteen real minutes is an example. Storyteller style, contact preferences and game speed do not silently change that allowance or authorize otherwise forbidden choices.

INTERVENTION-001: Recommend starting the window when the authoritative decision becomes available, not when a device receives or opens a push. Show the actual deadline in the app. Delayed, denied or muted delivery may mean the player misses the window; disclose that consequence when enabling timed autonomy. Expiry must not depend on a push receipt or an open browser tab.

INTERVENTION-002: Support a defined intervention timing policy. A timed hold freezes fictional progression during the real-time response window; continuous progression lets circumstances change while awaiting a response. D051 requires continued incident progression between interventions and makes a universal hold assumption inappropriate. D057 makes continued running during ordinary unanswered invitations the default design direction. A timed hold remains an optional explicitly selected alternative, not the recommended base experience. In running mode, state whether options remain available until the deadline or may be superseded by world events; an advertised guaranteed window must not silently expire early. Game speed does not change the real-time deadline, though it affects fictional exposure. Manual pause remains authoritative.

INTERVENTION-003: Manual pause suspends a pending window with its remaining time; it cannot expire and apply consequences behind an explicitly paused world. Resuming restores the remaining window. Speed changes do not shorten a real-time response window. This preserves the confirmed ability to pause indefinitely.

INTERVENTION-004: On expiry, choose only among currently valid, authorized responses. Apply a defined personality/dice policy; exact weighting and dice rules remain open and must respect the adopted rules/profile. Separate selection of an action from resolution of its success or failure. Record the selected action and relevant roll/trait influence so the result is explainable. Do not reroll on retries. If no permitted response exists, pause or retain the world pause and request attention instead of inventing permission.

INTERVENTION-005: A player response racing the deadline and automatic fallback can produce only one committed resolution. Late arrivals see what resolved and any new available actions. Do not apply the rejected choice, double-spend resources or narrate two histories. Restart recovery preserves the deadline, selected outcome and pause status.

INTERVENTION-006: Proposed outage behavior: if an unpaused deadline expires while the service is unavailable, reconcile pending events and its fallback once on recovery under the chosen timing policy; use the held situation in hold mode, or respect event order and any superseded choice in running mode, then resume only where permitted; do not fabricate uninterrupted play or a timely delivered push. Record the interruption. Manual suspension remains binding. Technical design must distinguish late background processing from service failure and implement recovery without rerolls; exact availability guarantees are unselected.

Personality can produce a foolish allowed response; it cannot override hard spending or autonomy limits. Ordinary fallback selection can use logic and dice without inference. A richer model contribution must fit the budget and remaining time; model failure cannot prevent the supported fallback. Timed interventions should be selective, with limits on interruption frequency and clear contact settings, so autonomous life does not become a stream of phone demands.

## Incident stages and honest decision windows

INTERVENTION-007: Resolving one choice may start an activity or advance an incident, not complete it. After an instruction such as hiding, resume authorized progression; later supported developments can interrupt the action, alter the situation and offer another decision. Each invitation references its own current stage. An old choice cannot act on a later stage, and overlapping invitations cannot resolve the same action twice.

INTERVENTION-008: Separate committed outcomes, current threats and remaining choices. If someone has already died or the character is injured, state that as established; the new countdown concerns the response or remaining danger. Do not advertise a chance to prevent an event that is already resolved. Conversely, a genuinely preventable outcome must respect the offered intervention contract. Presentation cannot impose unsupported injury, possession changes or relationship loss by description alone.

Repeated decision windows are permitted but must not force endless phone attendance. Let the player delegate further permitted stages or explicitly pause; personality/dice fallback continues within existing permissions. Coalesce stale notices and show current state on opening. Exact episode limits and contact frequency are open. Lack of response need not protect the character in a permissive-risk campaign; muting notifications does not itself authorize new risk.

## Messaging as a game surface

Slack, WhatsApp and similar channels are integration candidates, not interchangeable chosen transports. Channel selection must validate unsolicited updates after long inactivity, interactive responses, onboarding, identity binding, costs, message restrictions and failure behavior. Start with one supported channel if selected; no account setup or message sending is authorized by this design discussion.

A channel action and browser action share campaign identity, permission checks, deadline and a single committed outcome. Bind the sender to the correct player/campaign; forwarded messages or another workspace member must not acquire control. Repeated callbacks and cross-channel races cannot execute twice. If messages cannot be edited, later clicks must still return current status rather than perform stale actions. Channel history is not the authoritative save or the sole long-term recap.

Capability check, 2026-09-16: Slack's official [Block Kit reference](https://docs.slack.dev/reference/block-kit/) and [button documentation](https://docs.slack.dev/reference/block-kit/block-elements/button-element/) support interactive message controls, making in-channel choices a concrete candidate. This does not settle app installation, audience fit or operating costs. WhatsApp's current onboarding, outbound messaging/template rules and costs remain to be verified against current Meta documentation before selecting it; no equivalence or price claim is made.

## Office-day Slack reference

D056 illustrates an occasional direct-message experience: a small routine update, a later meaningful incident, a few relevant actions and a clear response window/default. Recommend a private player conversation rather than a shared work channel; actual installation and workspace suitability must be established before implementation. This product idea does not authorize posting to the user's employer workspace.

Let contact settings select a discreet preview or a more expressive message, especially for distressing content visible at work. Preview detail changes presentation, not the underlying event or autonomy. The player can inspect details, respond quickly, delegate subsequent stages or pause. Avoid mandatory rapid check-ins: missed invitations use the configured fallback and later messages show the current situation. Character identity and a short relevant connection can make a notification recognizable without exposing a full biography or spending an LLM call to rewrite every routine update.

## Intensity and attention are independent

D057–D058 permit sustained episodes with successive reports, followed by quiet stretches. Storyteller pacing controls developments; contact preferences control how frequently the player hears about them. Ten-minute battle reports are illustrative, not a scheduler default. A quiet contact mode must not silently suppress world events, and a busy story must not demand a player answer at every stage.

Recommend coherent incident updates that summarize intervening outcomes, highlight any current choice and identify the autonomous fallback. Reports may be frequent during an opted-in episode and sparse during travel, within contact limits. A player can stop responding without ending the episode. Before a running invitation is offered, establish how autonomous behavior continues while waiting, what happens on expiry and what can supersede its options. Reaching a deadline does not require holding all progression until then. Any promised protected response window must be an explicit exception with consistent timing.

If no legal fallback exists within hard permissions, explain the exceptional blocked state rather than silently inventing authority. Content/action design should make such dead ends uncommon in the intended autonomous loop. Explicit manual pause freezes progression and pending windows under INTERVENTION-003.
