# Everyday action examples, not dedicated systems

This topic preserves employment/exchange examples while removing the earlier requirement to implement specialized wage, production or merchant machinery. Under the proposed [story-progression contract](../experience/story-progression.md), these are narrative situations handled through the same intention, duration, adjudication and consequence flow. Exact product choices remain open under D064.

## Working for lodging

“Work here this week and sleep in the barn” establishes an agreement. Preserve what the parties actually agreed, completed relevant work and any dispute that matters. The storyteller can judge an interrupted arrangement in context; no hourly payroll formula, attendance system or lodging billing engine is required merely to tell this story.

If an explicit wage and completed paid duration were established, keep the arithmetic consistent. Otherwise do not retrospectively turn a narrative arrangement into precise debt or wages the player never agreed to. Dinner, conversation and rest can be chronology entries with relevant consequences, without food consumption or sleep-meter subsystems.

## Exchanging something for flour

The storyteller may decide whether the miller will sell, barter, offer credit, refuse or change an offer in light of the situation. A market price simulation is unnecessary. Once the player agrees to an actual exchange, record what changed once. A promise, possession and paid consideration are different facts. [Inventory and value](../rules/equipment-and-resources.md) owns the small proposed transfer/quantity contract.

WORK-F01: No longer a required pre-build payroll decision. Revisit explicit rates, partial completion and recurring charges only if predictable accounting materially improves the selected play. They are optional later mechanics, not implementation gates.

WORK-001: Preserve explicit received/spent quantities consistently if the campaign tracks them; this does not require accounting for every background meal or every NPC's cash flow. Detailed wage formulas, production yields, restocking, crafting recipes, scheduled rent and hunger economies are removed from initial requirements. Earlier examples remain in Git history and optional historical scenarios, not an active feature backlog.
