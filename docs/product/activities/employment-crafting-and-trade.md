# Employment, production and exchange

This document owns activity-specific progress and compensation. It proposes a concrete employment contract for SCN-001; it does not make paid work or human needs universal, select wages, or replace applicable adopted D&D rules. Recurrence belongs to DOC-ROUTINES.

## Proposed time-based employment contract

Before starting, establish participants, location/access, required capability/equipment, work window and duration, compensation basis, payment due time, resource costs, interruption terms and termination conditions. A vague promise to work is not a completed agreement.

Credit only eligible time actually worked. For a time-paid agreement, earned compensation is its agreed rate multiplied by eligible performed duration, subject to explicit partial-work and rounding terms. Completion-paid agreements require their stated deliverable; they cannot silently use the same formula. Distinguish earned, due and received compensation. Currency changes only on actual payment, not on planning or narrative description.

Advance to the next relevant boundary: completion, resource exhaustion, eligibility loss, external interruption or a new autonomous choice. Resolve intervening consequences before crediting later progress. Pause credits no time. Replacing a plan does not remove earned compensation or automatically pay an incomplete deliverable. Resume requires fresh eligibility and uses the retained progress allowed by the agreement.

If an employer cannot pay, retain the actual obligation and available responses; do not invent funds or assume the debt disappears. Workplace closure ends available work only where circumstances make it unavailable, and payment follows the agreement rather than a universal forfeiture rule.

SCN-001 checks a partial shift and delayed payment. An incompatible simultaneous activity cannot also consume the same actor's work time. Travel plus work is allowed only when the particular job explicitly permits it.

## Production and exchange boundaries

Crafting/gathering require their own inputs, tools, capability, work/progress measure, output specification, losses and interruption rule. Performing work, producing an item and receiving payment are separate outcomes. Do not generalize the wage formula to combat, travel, crafting or animal foraging. An item description does not establish yield or executable properties.

Exchange must state participants, accessible goods, quantity, consideration and completion conditions. A dialogue promise does not complete an exchange. Transfer cannot duplicate a unique item or spend unavailable resources; ownership and custody remain distinguishable. Broader pricing and economic processes remain with their world-system owner.

WORK-F01: Select the first concrete agreement and its interruption/payment terms. Retain time-paid work with explicit partial payment for SCN-001/009. The production-and-sale example below illustrates an alternative; D042 confirms that fishing is not core or selected content. Broader crafting and markets remain candidates. Exact rates and source adaptations are open.

## Generic production, exchange and recurring expenses

Production and paid employment have different outcomes. A proposed production activity specifies place/access, required capabilities/resources, duration, an output rule, capacity limits and interruption outcome. Rules determine outputs; narration cannot mint resources. Fishing is merely one possible content instance, not a required subsystem. Generated content selects validated parameters from the supported profile, not arbitrary executable formulas.

An exchange transfers an actual quantity out of inventory and adds the agreed payment exactly once. State the merchant's opening window, accepted goods, prices, stock and ability to pay. Recommend simple configured daily restocking/funds with a visible rule for this slice; do not imply a closed simulated economy. Catch and unsold inventory are not cash income. Food purchase and consumption are distinct; lodging charges have explicit due dates and eligibility. Failed payment follows the selected consequence and autonomy policy rather than silently allowing negative balances or guaranteed survival.

WORK-001: Every economic outcome can be explained by an activity, exchange or due obligation. At any reporting boundary, closing cash equals opening cash plus received income minus paid expenses plus explicitly recorded other adjustments. Unpaid obligations and unsold goods are separate. Retry/recovery must not repeat a transfer, production outcome or charge.

The illustrative month starts with zero cash, receives 100 and pays 90, leaving 10 only if those are actual transactions and no other cash changes occur. Starting with 20 would leave 30. Neither example guarantees the generated economy remains solvent; scenario validation must check routine feasibility and expose deficits. Full balancing, spoilage, supply chains, dynamic pricing and intoxication mechanics can be added later.
