# Return and journal

**Owns:** coming back after absence and reading a long life.  
**Does not own:** staff forensic investigation, or a finished replay feature.  
**Companions:** [POC Journal](living-book-poc.md#poc-journal-intentionally-modest), [concepts](../foundations/concepts.md).

## After absence

Return shows the **changed situation**: what is true now, and how the player got there.

- Offer a **focused recap** and then make any current decision clear. The reader should not need to read every quiet interval.
- A recap that needs inference must not delay a decision that is already available. If the story moves while the recap is prepared, label what the recap covers and show the newer entries.
- A push notification is not proof the new passage was read, and opening the story list does not mark every story read.
- The chronology contains admitted results. An autonomous choice appears there only when that decision was specifically pre-authorized. A prepared narration or pending event is not yet a committed outcome.

The recap helps someone understand how they reached the current situation; the original published passages remain the record.

## Reading a long life

A month of ordinary life should remain enjoyable to revisit without becoming hundreds of equally prominent cards. Offer the detailed chronology underneath a shorter narrative overview. Quiet accomplishments, relationships and routines can matter as much as dramatic danger; significance is not a measure of violence or surprise.

Use a focused recap and searchable history linked to people, places, commitments and original passages. Later relevance can change what a recap includes **without rewriting what happened**. A generated recap or synopsis does not replace the saved original passage. The first POC keeps that limit in [POC Journal](living-book-poc.md#poc-journal-intentionally-modest). Do not discard ordinary entries because they initially seem unimportant, or require every mechanical update to become prose.

## Scene checkpoints, review and forks | long-term target

### Selected eventual experience (2026-11-19)

The Living Book must allow the player to navigate back to **any committed scene**, inspect the actual recorded scene and its historical state, then fork a new independently evolving story **from that exact scene**. A scene checkpoint includes enough authoritative information to recover character capabilities, consequential world facts, relationships, applicable inventory/equipment/resources, fictional time and commitments without importing later parent-story outcomes: a fork of an earlier scene does not see the parent's later events, and a copy of the current head is not a historical fork. World-neutrality remains binding: a microbe's relevant facts are not a medieval adventurer's gold ledger. Historical reading and forking are separate actions; reading is side-effect free, and a fork may not reroll the old scene or repeat provider charges.

### Narrow current proof and limits

The journal remains source-faithful passage reading. Opening a saved scene may load that scene's checkpoint on a disjoint query key; it does not reuse the live snapshot and does not make a historic offer commandable. A quiescent checkpoint can start an independent fork. Scenes without a checkpoint still cannot be forked. Replay of dice, provider spend, and changing live time from the journal stay forbidden. Specific scene boundary semantics belong to the technical [lifecycle and compatibility](../../technical/engine/lifecycle-and-compatibility.md#forked-continuities) contracts, which also state the exact conditions under which a fork is supported or refused. This page does not claim the long-term target is built or proved beyond that narrow boundary.

## No chapters

There are no chapters or chapter boundaries in the experience. A continuous conversation can contain many rapid decisions; quiet life can continue without a literary transition. Internal summary segments serve storage and retrieval only. Vocabulary: [concepts](../foundations/concepts.md).

## Separation from staff forensics and replay product

Player journal/return is not staff forensic investigation. Proposed Story Replay remains **deferred and unratified**—not a finished player feature—and belongs under [player replay](../../../work/designs/backlog/player-replay/README.md), not as a silent claim here.
