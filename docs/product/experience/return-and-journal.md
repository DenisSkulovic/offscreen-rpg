# Return and journal

**Owner (DOC01 P2):** coming back after absence and reading a long life. From player-experience return/chronology sections + experience return-and-memory flows.

## After absence

Returning after a long absence should offer a **focused recap** and then make any current decision clear. It should not require reading every quiet interval. A push notification is not proof the new passage was read, and opening the story list does not mark every story read. A recap that needs inference must not delay a decision that is already available. If the story moves while the recap is prepared, label what the recap covers and show the newer entries. The chronology contains admitted results. An autonomous choice appears there only when that decision was specifically pre-authorized. A prepared narration or pending event is not yet a committed outcome. The recap helps someone understand how they reached the current situation.

## Reading a long life

A month of ordinary life should remain enjoyable to revisit without becoming hundreds of equally prominent cards. Offer the detailed chronology underneath a shorter narrative overview. Quiet accomplishments, relationships and routines can matter as much as dramatic danger; significance is not a measure of violence or surprise.

Use a focused recap and searchable history linked to people, places, commitments and original passages. Later relevance can change what a recap includes **without rewriting what happened**. A generated recap or synopsis does not replace the saved original passage. The first POC keeps that limit in [POC Journal](living-book-poc.md#poc-journal-intentionally-modest). Do not discard ordinary entries because they initially seem unimportant, or require every mechanical update to become prose.

## No chapters

There are no chapters or chapter boundaries in the experience. A continuous conversation can contain many rapid decisions; quiet life can continue without a literary transition. Internal summary segments serve storage and retrieval only. Vocabulary: [concepts](../foundations/concepts.md).

## Separation from staff forensics and replay product

Player journal/return is not staff forensic investigation. Proposed Story Replay V1 (observability program) remains **proposed**—not a finished player feature—and belongs under [replay](../../../work/designs/open/player-replay/README.md) when filled, not as a silent claim here.
