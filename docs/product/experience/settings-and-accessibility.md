# Settings and accessibility

**Owns:** player-facing controls that are not Storyteller creative presets, plus accessibility obligations.  
**Does not own:** Storyteller creative presets ([profiles and settings](../storyteller/profiles-and-settings.md)).  
**Companions:** engineering acceptance for operable controls, motion, and client cost is [client accessibility and performance](../../technical/client/accessibility-and-performance.md).

## Player settings (product)

Settings change supported play preferences with captured impact visible before confirm. They must not rewrite past facts, secretly enlarge spending authority, or silently reinterpret creative fields as invent/risk authorities.

Personal preferences (accessibility, sound, private notifications) remain personal even when a campaign locks creative/speed settings. A locked creative or speed campaign setting never removes an accessibility control: accessibility and personal settings are independent of locked creative policy.

## Accessibility obligations

Selected design target (not a certified result): WCAG 2.2 AA for player-facing web where applicable, with motion-sensitivity guidance (SC 2.3.3). Game fiction is **not** an exception to accessible controls. The 320px, enlarged-text, and reduced-motion cases stay in [Reader jobs](living-book-poc.md#reader-jobs-and-non-negotiable-outcomes) and [Visual hierarchy and ownership](design-system-and-expression.md#visual-hierarchy-and-ownership).

- Every color, animation, icon or spatial cue that conveys “what is happening / committed / actionable” has a text equivalent.
- Passages are semantic narrative text with sensible heading hierarchy—not canvas-only bitmaps.
- All offered intentions and controls work by keyboard with visible focus.
- New passages should not steal focus or force auto-scroll while someone is reading; offer a discreet “New passage available.”
- Live updates use modest polite announcements; avoid announcing every timer tick.
- Distinguish HTTP acknowledgment from completed world consequence in assistive messaging.
