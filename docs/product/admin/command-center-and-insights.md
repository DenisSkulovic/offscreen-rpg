# Command center and insights

**Owns:** the shift-start surface for a multi-role employee, and the analytical vocabulary for arrival, activation, value and experiments.

**Status:** proposed, 2026-11-14. These are not live dashboards, and the page does not claim exhaustive current analytics. Portfolio demos are not this product. No widget framework, search index, realtime transport or numerical target is selected.

The home and the insights views are scoped jobs. The home answers what needs this employee's attention, composed only from the sections their grants allow. Insights answer how people arrive, activate and stay. Neither is a monitoring wall over staff or players, and neither treats a chart as a cause.

## A home for my work, not a panopticon

Command Center is a work queue, not a surveillance surface and not an exhaustive analytics page. It answers what needs this employee's attention now, and it composes only the sections their grants allow. It does not watch staff or players, and it does not claim to show everything the business knows.

| Role on this shift | The home may show | It does not show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Support | Assigned cases, escalations, overdue responses, known incidents that affect customers | Another team's raw charts |
| Engineering | Live incidents, relevant execution failures, recent releases | Customer payment credentials |
| LiveOps | Active configuration, experiments, quality signals, scheduled interventions | An edit button that skips review |
| Finance | Billing exceptions, unusual grants and refunds, cost anomalies | Private narrative |
| Growth | Activation, cohort movement, campaign outcomes the operator may see | A tiny segment that identifies a person |
| Leadership | Business health, reliability, retention, spend, exceptional risk | An implicit approve-anything control |

Overlapping grants add sections. They do not recombine into a stronger action. A card cannot skip approval because it is on the home screen. One incident with many affected players stays one incident, with customer work linked underneath. Unassigned urgent work stays on a responsible queue. A calm period may be an empty home.

### Freshness and search

Freshness and search are scoped jobs too. A delayed aggregate is labeled delayed, not live. An unknown or missing metric is unknown, not zero, and no inference is drawn from its absence. Saved searches, recent items and pins re-check access after revocation. A link from a coworker does not reveal a protected record through a search suggestion. Global search is specified on [the charter](charter-and-personas.md).

**Normal.** The morning after a provider incident, Engineering opens one incident from the home. Support opens the customer cases tied to that same incident. Each person sees the fields their grant allows.

**Negative.** A second role widens the home and does not mint a new power. A restricted Trust case appears only as a safe item, without the complainant. A prominent status card does not invent a healthy number from a missing upstream.

## Growth and insights

Growth & Insights is the analytical job: explaining how people arrive, activate, convert and stay. It is scoped to aggregate evidence. A chart is not a cause and not permission to read a story.

Person, account, campaign and activity are different units. Paying-customer acquisition, active paid relationships, renewal, cancellation, behavioral absence, refund rate and a quality-adjusted view are different metrics. Churn from a cancelled subscription is not churn from a quiet week. No 7-day or 30-day policy and no success target is accepted here. An illustrative retention number does not become product policy. Every chart states population, basis, source, window, freshness and gaps.

An uplift after a release may be seasonality, mix, a promotion, or a quality change. Show assignment, windows, comparability and sample size. An underpowered experiment is not "zero impact." A correlation is not a cause. There is no universal enjoyment score.

| Set | Means | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analytics cohort | Who is in a reporting population | They may be emailed or discounted |
| Experiment cohort | Who was assigned and actually exposed | Intended percentage equals exposure |
| Promotion audience | Who may receive a commercial offer | They are in the analytics cohort |
| Contact audience | Who may be messaged on a named channel | They may be targeted from a Trust file |

A shared campaign is not a set of independent per-account rule experiments. Two participants in one world do not follow conflicting mechanics because a growth test assigned them differently. Growth proposes and analyzes. LiveOps activates a game version. Commercial owns the offer and the grant economics. Trust constrains targeting and contact.

Tiny segments can identify someone. Use a minimum group size, protected dimensions and a separate export grant. Private text and another participant's identity do not leak through a filter.

**Normal.** New players abandon during first story setup. The chart is an activation population with a defined window. A shorter Storyteller recipe that saves money is reported beside continuity and complaint signals, not as an automatic win. A promotional trial names eligibility, exclusions and a funding ceiling before anyone is awarded.

**Negative.** A dormant player's return is not proof the last message caused it. Mixed payer and participants are not one "account value." A player in an analytics cohort is not eligible for a discount or a risky mechanic. Growth cannot read Trust allegations as a targeting list.
