**Owner (DOC01 P2):** what staff authority means to operators.  
**Status:** Core direction accepted with AD access/audit slices; deeper operating policy still **proposed** in places ([authority-operating-policy](../../../evidence/inspections/admin-program-essays-2026-11/authority-operating-policy.md)).  
**Sources:** [authority-and-delegation](../../../evidence/inspections/admin-program-essays-2026-11/authority-and-delegation.md), [access-and-governance-product](../../../evidence/inspections/admin-program-essays-2026-11/access-and-governance-product.md).

## Product promise

A legitimate employee can understand **what they may do, to which subject, for which reason, under which constraints, and with which additional approvals**. A second employee or auditor can reconstruct why the decision was made.

Access is task-appropriate, scoped, preferably time-limited for exceptional work, and explainable at the affected record. Unnecessary universal access is a design defect.

## Grants are coherent, not shopping carts

An effective permission combines staff identity/source, permitted actions, resource set/environment, contextual limits (purpose, case, quantity, expiry, sensitivity), and required additional authorization.

**Composition = union of intact applicable grants subject to global prohibitions**—not independently unioning every action from one package with every scope from another. “Can see A” + “can edit B” never becomes “can edit A.”

## Hard boundaries

- An operator does not become a more powerful player. Acting on behalf of a player is an explicit, attributable mode.
- Viewing a record does not imply bulk export or unrestricted story-content access.
- Production Support `support-investigator` case/campaign grant issuance for live entitlement remains **absent** — OBS06/07 Situation UI is synthetic/disposable-grant proof only.
