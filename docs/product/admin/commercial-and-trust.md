# Commercial operations and trust

**Owns:** how staff explain money, allowances and privacy, and which of those actions stay conditional.  
**Status: proposed, 2026-11-14.** Owner review is still required. Legal review is still required before a privacy or enforcement workflow is operational. Nothing on this page is a production grant to refund, export, delete, or read private narrative. Illustrative Support, impersonation, raw-prompt, refund, export, and LiveOps examples stay Proposed or Blocked.

Companion: [Player 360](player-360-and-support.md), [authority](authority-and-governance.md).

## Commercial objects that must stay distinct

Commercial Operations answers who was promised what, whether it is available now, what was consumed, who is responsible, and what it cost the business. Settlement and payment credentials may stay with a provider. Offscreen owns what a player may do in the game.

| Concept | Meaning | Do not confuse it with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offering and version | The promise at a point in time | Today's catalog rewriting an old contract |
| Payer | Who is commercially responsible | The player, the character, or every participant |
| Subscription status | Provider billing lifecycle | Effective game access |
| Entitlement | A named capability for a beneficiary | Remaining allowance |
| Grant / allowance | A bounded consumption source, window and expiry | Fictional gold, a wallet, or a provider invoice |
| Usage | Reservation and consumption of an admitted activity | A queued request, a model attempt, or a published turn |
| Adjustment | A new compensation or correction record | Overwriting the original history |
| Refund | A money movement with external confirmation | "Request accepted" |
| Provider expense | Model or infrastructure cost | A successful player-facing result |
| Internal budget | Company exposure for trials, promotions or diagnostics | A contractual entitlement or a game rule |
| Restriction | A separate stop on account, campaign or dispatch | Proof the customer was never paid |

Customer allowance, company provider spend and fictional resources have different histories. A screen may show them together. It must not treat them as one balance. Who pays for another participant is an open business rule. A one-payer, several-beneficiary layout is a stress test, not approval of a family plan.

### Actions that stay conditional

| Proposed action | Required shape | Still not granted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain access | Read effective capability and blockers; payer detail may be omitted | — |
| Goodwill allowance | A new bounded record: beneficiary, expiry, reason, case, cap | A rewrite of the purchase |
| Provisional access | A visible exceptional source with start and end | A mutated contract |
| Reconcile a mismatch | Compare authoritative sources | Inventing a payment success |
| Change subscription | An explicit commercial lifecycle operation | A local badge flip |
| Refund | A separate money request plus a verified result, with a ceiling | Support's allowance grant |
| Bulk promotion | Eligibility, funding ceiling, exclusions, preview, separate approval | A drafted campaign that is already live |
| Pause sponsored spend | Stop new admission; do not pretend in-flight charges vanished | A kill switch over committed play |
| Export | Its own purpose and authority | Inferred from per-account view |

No direct ledger surgery. Staff cannot delete usage, rewrite a provider charge, or paint an invoice green. Unknown provider data is unknown, not zero revenue and not a successful refund. A paid plan does not erase a restriction. A platform spending stop can leave history readable while blocking new charged generation. A timed-out compensation must not double-grant on retry. A provider charge without a publication does not automatically bill the player. A catalog change that would touch existing contracts shows the affected versioned population and waits for a customer-policy decision.

Prices, SLA numbers, exact monetary thresholds and the ledger schema are not decided here.

**Normal.** Support sees valid payment evidence, a stale entitlement, and a separate platform stop. Revenue Operations reconciles or issues a narrowly bounded provisional remedy, then checks effective gameplay permission. If the provider is late, the case stays pending.

**Negative.** A goodwill allowance is not a refund, and a refund is not an allowance. Retrying a lost acknowledgement does not create a second grant. Fictional gold is not an editable commercial balance.

## Trust and privacy are a different mandate

Trust & Safety investigates evidenced harm, policy abuse or security-relevant behavior and applies proportionate restrictions. Privacy handles verified requests for access, correction, portability, deletion or objection. The same person may do both jobs on different days. The jobs do not share an evidence audience. A Support agent does not inherit abuse-report inspection. A privacy operator does not inherit enforcement.

Private fiction is not misconduct. Tragic, violent or unusual story text is not, by itself, evidence against the player. Separate fictional content from harassment of another participant, unauthorized disclosure, fraud, and abuse of company-funded generation.

| Information class | Boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary metadata | Need-to-know status, not the whole file |
| Protected account data | Its own sensitive read and purpose |
| Private narrative | Default private; a case-scoped reason to inspect |
| Raw model context | Separate from trace metadata. See [AI investigation](ai-engineering-and-investigation.md) |
| Trust evidence | Specialist audience; a safe customer summary may omit the complainant |
| Commercial evidence | Not a moderator entitlement |
| Staff audit | Governance review; the subject cannot clean their own log |
| Data-rights package | Restricted privacy operations; purpose-bound export |

A removed login is not erased data. A processor copy or a lawful audit record may remain, and the workflow must say so. Deletion must not drop another participant's shared history or falsify a ledger. Exact legal deadlines and retention windows are not set on this page. An illustrative retention number does not become product policy. Request verification, export audience, and deletion across stored classes stay unratified in [Player data lifecycle](../../../work/designs/open/player-data-lifecycle/README.md#rights-matrix).

**Normal.** A participant reports unwanted contact. Trust checks the real participant relationship, reads only permitted evidence, and applies a scoped restriction with an appeal. Support sees the safe customer-facing status, not the complainant file. An appeal is a new review. It does not let the original reviewer erase the record.

**Negative.** A villain's generated dialogue is not the player's harassment. A spend spike is not a fraud finding. "No data affected" is not a valid close when the read was not recorded. Deleting the login while a processor has not confirmed its copy is partial, not complete. Restricting one participant does not open another participant's private text or payer record. Account restriction, content restriction, campaign pause and commercial hold stay separate switches.
