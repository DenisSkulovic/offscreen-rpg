# Storyteller behavior and quality

The Storyteller is a creatively free writer inside a hard authority limit. This page selects what players are owed: the freedoms the writer has, the limits it never crosses, and the capabilities (research, a Workdesk, writing skills, bounded delegation) it may use when a turn merits them. Those capabilities are not mandatory round counts or ritual steps, and none is claimed as already working behavior. Game Authority stays separate and decides what becomes authoritative.

**Owns:** narrative behavior the product owes players. **Does not own:** proof that sustained multi-turn creative quality works. That quality is unproved in [STATUS](../../../STATUS.md); the dated offline rehearsals named below show bounded behavior only, and constitution rows that exceed shipped capability are selected targets, not live proof.

**Technical:** [selected controller](../../technical/storyteller/operation-and-controller.md) and [current publication](../../technical/storyteller/generation-and-publication.md).

## Role

Offscreen RPG is not a chatbot sending a chapter on Next. A player lives as a particular character in a consequential world. The application is a **neutral and strict world authority**; the Storyteller is a creative participant in that authority, not its replacement. The engine is creatively unopinionated: tone, dramatic intensity and unpredictability come from the selected profile, campaign direction, world content and player configuration, not a universal house style. Profile, temperature or model tier changes voice and investigative depth, not the limits below.

## What it may do

Each freedom is exercised through admitted publication rather than by assertion.

- Shape scenes, choices, judgments and developments from premise, preferences, situation, history, capabilities and intentions, and propose currently feasible intentions and presentation.
- Introduce developments that would not arise from the character's immediate routine, and keep private revisable narrative intent, when campaign direction permits; see [Narrative initiative](#narrative-initiative).
- Promote consequential people and places into concise canonical identity or lore records in the same turn when continuity value warrants it, and attach bounded active-scene cues deliberately, not by name inference; see [promotion and attachment](memory-and-continuity.md#promotion-and-active-scene-attachment).
- Investigate before writing: see [Investigative writing, revision and delegation](#investigative-writing-revision-and-delegation).

## What it must not do

- Decide that the character heroically stops a threat, invalidate an accepted action without a supported interruption, or preserve a preferred finale after play makes it impossible.
- Retroactively make rewards earned, reroll checks, spend beyond policy, reveal private future plans as fact, or take actions for a human without an admitted decision or delegation rule.
- Treat model memory of a recognizable franchise setting as supplied canon. Canon invention follows [profiles and settings](profiles-and-settings.md#canon-invention-policy), not temperature or price tier.

The prompt admission that has to stay inside these prohibitions is [What the prompt must not grant](../../technical/storyteller/prompts-and-tools.md#what-the-prompt-must-not-grant).

## Investigative writing, revision and delegation

A mission starts from a small pinned orientation rather than the whole campaign stuffed into one prompt. The writer may browse the Library, search for likely evidence, open exact source revisions, compare contradictory records, load a relevant writing skill, stage notes or prose in its Workdesk, reread and revise before submission. A simple continuation may need one short inference; a difficult 200-scene callback may justify deeper investigation. These are available capabilities, not required stages.

Delegation is purposeful. A research assistant receives its own bounded context and source scope for a separable question, such as reconciling an old promise or critiquing a draft for continuity, and returns claims, uncertainty and source handles or an isolated draft proposal. The parent Storyteller decides what to use. A child never becomes a permanent NPC mind and never publishes world state directly.

Quality is judged partly by whether the writer **finds and uses the right evidence**. The number of tools called, rounds run or agents spawned is never itself a quality measure. Injecting the entire Library, performing arbitrary fixed rounds, or spawning agents that merely paraphrase the parent are failures of the intended product.

## Ordinary life and absence

Caring for a character over several real days can be the main experience. Quiet idle may remain quiet. Supported accepted routines can progress without model calls where prepared opportunities allow. Frequency of unusual incidents and severity when an incident occurs are separate preferences—low incident frequency must not freeze character progress, and routine repetition must not force escalating danger merely to keep the plot busy.

## Falsifiable principles (adopted)

| Principle | Positive | Discriminating failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agency | Meaningful intent through authorized choice | Model paraphrases choice into a different act or forces unrelated climax |
| Earned time | Work/travel take admitted time; completion linked to source | “I begin working” instantly yields earnings |
| Durable memory | Gift, promise, exact wording can matter later from valid evidence | Vague or wrong recall invents owner/recipient |
| Creative neutrality | Quiet/chaotic/severe profiles realize *their* commitments | Shared prompt always enforces fixed drama density |
| World neutrality | Abstract forms get relevant perception; omit inapplicable humanoid defaults | Every story assumes tavern/hands/inventory |
| Rules as reality | Typed abilities, checks, receipts constrain claims | Prose grants item/success rules did not commit |
| Ordinary life and absence | Routines without compulsory model; quiet stays quiet | Every minute tick runs a model |
| Privacy and visibility | Character knowledge ≠ private DM possibilities | Retrieval exposes secret future intent as fact |
| Cost and reliability | Explicit policy bounds every model round | Secret double spend or incoherent depleted-tier fiction |

Lethal risk expansion and free-intent paths remain **policy-gated**; current `nonlethal` (where present) is a scoped capability limitation of that realization, not a universal moral rule and not an eternal “never kill” prompt dogma.

## Multi-day evaluation family

This family is Proposed/Blocked. It is an illustrative evaluation, not ratified acceptance. It sets no drama percentage, no fixed drama density and no required incident rate. Its two named rehearsals, the remembered promise and the quiet breakfast, are dated offline rehearsals, not live play.

Creative temperament, limited initiative, private possibilities, canon invention, and funding or tool permissions stay separate. Temperament is the profile's voice. Limited initiative is whether the campaign allows cultivated pressure, including none. A private possibility is not a public event, as [Six kinds of record](../gameplay/continuity.md#six-kinds-of-record) requires. Canon invention is the domain permission in [Canon invention policy](profiles-and-settings.md#canon-invention-policy). Funding and tools stay with [Provider and budget boundary](../../technical/storyteller/operation-and-controller.md#provider-and-budget-boundary). A future controller design is not a live feature.

The family has three readings, each bounded by source that already exists:

1. **Continuity.** A gift or promise that was admitted can matter in a later scene. The offline rehearsal includes a remembered promise. That fixture does not prove sustained multi-day play.
2. **Quiet work.** An accepted prepared interval may finish once, and inactivity admits no new generation. The same rehearsal includes a quiet breakfast. A quiet day does not require a dramatic incident.
3. **Player redirection.** A conditional encounter planned along a path the player abandons is reconsidered, not played as if nothing changed; the exact negative is under [Selected solo proof boundary](#selected-solo-proof-boundary).

Negative: an agent must not force a dramatic incident every quiet day, treat a discarded narrative plan as committed truth, or charge a provider for every idle minute. [Supported behavior and evidence limits](../../technical/storyteller/operation-and-controller.md#supported-behavior-and-evidence-limits) records that these rehearsals do not certify live narrative quality.

## Narrative initiative

Stored passages and a private narrative intent do not prove that a long life stays coherent or enjoyable. This heading selects permission to author developments inside admitted world changes. It does not make multi-step memory exploration the live default, and it does not claim the sustained creative proof [STATUS](../../../STATUS.md) still lists as unproved. A turn that treats a discarded plan as truth, or cannot be reviewed against the declared profile, is a quality failure. Cost and the unratified experiment notes stay in [product-reality economics](../../../research/product-reality/ECONOMICS-2026-11-18.md). That page authorizes no spend.

The Storyteller is not limited to extrapolating the last player action. When the campaign's selected direction permits proactive authorship, it may maintain a private, revisable narrative intent and introduce developments that would not arise from the character's immediate routine. A player may spend the day fishing while a war begins or a meteor approaches. The Storyteller may make that pressure real through admitted world changes. The limits in [What it must not do](#what-it-must-not-do) apply unchanged, including that it cannot decide the character heroically stops the threat.

A grand narrative is optional. A campaign may explicitly prefer observation, ordinary life or a farming rhythm with no cultivated arc. In that case the Storyteller must not invent a looming destiny merely to create momentum.

Narrative direction is distinct from a Storyteller profile. The profile supplies voice, taste, rhythm and initiative defaults. The campaign owns any current direction, open threads and scheduled developments. Changing profile mid-story does not erase established threats or turn possibilities into facts.

Private narrative intent is not executable future state. A possible invasion remains noncanonical guidance until introduced. A development that must become due at a supported time uses an admitted world obligation. Evocative planning prose cannot schedule itself.

Player availability, notifications and spending limits are operational constraints, not measures of how much misfortune a character deserves.

## Developments, choices and endings

The Storyteller can compress an uneventful afternoon or stay close to a conversation that needs immediate responses. Choices express different intentions, not different wording for a predetermined outcome. An inactive response such as waiting is still a meaningful choice. It does not require a special waiting subsystem.

A choice may privately identify a few exact sources the next turn will need. Those hints are invisible, bounded, and used only when that choice is selected. They are not a reason to invent a calendar or dramatic machinery for a world that has none.

Invention authority is captured by domain. Permission to invent descriptive lore never grants permission to invent executable rules or bypass state admission. In shared play, do not let the fastest player dictate every other character's behavior. The method of collecting conflicting intentions is **not accepted** until an owner chooses it; see open designs rather than this page.

Continuing life is a target experience: a character can remain in the same world for real months, with minimal dramatic intervention and no compulsory ending. Individual adventures can resolve without ending the playable life. A deliberately finished story remains readable and does not keep scheduling new developments. The creation controls that choose finite adventures versus continuing life are still to be designed.

## Selected solo proof boundary

This rehearsal boundary is the selected Storyteller proof for one profiled story. It does not ratify the small-group live acceptance, which stays Proposed/Blocked in [First playable target](../foundations/vision.md#first-playable-target). The approved solo proof selects a versioned profile before opening generation. Opening and continuation are separate bounded tasks. The narrative rehearsal uses 2–5 distinct offered intentions. Mechanical openings and consequences use zero to four admitted plans: zero is a visible held state, one is valid when the situation is genuinely constrained, and larger sets must differ in intended approach rather than wording. Public plans communicate the attempted commitment and apparent risk without revealing DCs or unused outcomes. There are no free-text gameplay actions and no automatic life endings. Inactivity admits no new generation. An accepted prepared interval may finish once.

Multiple autonomous agents are a possible implementation technique, not a product requirement and not a reason to add extra model calls.

**Negative:** a conditional encounter planned further along an abandoned path must be reconsidered if that route is abandoned. It is not presented as if nothing changed. Model prose cannot spend the same coins twice or apply an already resolved action again.
