# Storyteller memory and continuity

**Owns:** what the Storyteller must remember, retrieve and promote so a life stays coherent.  
**Accepted product expectation** for current-versus-history and sparse promotion. Retrieval routes and creative exploration below are the **selected Storyteller target** (2026-11-14), not a claim that every route is implemented.  
**Does not own:** mechanical receipts and time ([gameplay continuity](../gameplay/continuity.md), [rules](../gameplay/rules.md)).  
**Technical companion:** [context and memory](../../technical/storyteller/context-and-memory.md).

## Current truth versus inspectable history

A useful detail must survive more than the immediately following choice. When later play changes that detail, the Storyteller receives the **current admitted version**, not an obsolete description and its correction as equally live facts. Earlier passages and document versions remain inspectable history. The current canonical record answers what is true now. This applies to a collapsed road, a changed relationship, a repaired wall or a revised private possibility. It is not a quest-specific memory system.

**Negative:** a paraphrase that resembles a familiar name is not a remembered fact. Search candidates are leads, not facts. A no-match does not prove the history contains nothing relevant.

## Promotion and active-scene attachment

When play introduces a person, place or other descriptive subject with durable continuity value, the Storyteller may promote it into a concise canonical identity or lore record in the same turn. Passing scenery and disposable names do not require permanent files. Promotion preserves the published passage as evidence and gives later turns a stable identity. It does not automatically create mechanical statistics, inventory, agency or simulation.

Promoted records that remain part of the newly current situation may be attached to its active-scene anchor as an identity, place or thread. Ordinary continuations then receive their current canonical versions. Attachment is bounded and deliberate: it is not inferred from names, paths or prose. Restarting the active situation replaces its focused cues rather than accumulating them forever.

A restarted situation may attach an already-existing record from the exact campaign catalogue. The Storyteller refers only to the task-local handle and declares whether it is acting as an identity, place or thread. Publication validates the role and resolves the handle. The record need not be rewritten merely to become relevant again.

## Selected retrieval routes

Unanticipated returns require discovery rather than permanent prompt inclusion. The Storyteller may ask campaign memory one or two ordinary-language questions and optionally open exact returned handles. It should not need to understand indexes or ranking controls. The application translates that need into permitted retrieval over the captured story's current canonical records.

Different questions deserve different work, sharing canon and provenance but not necessarily cost:

| Need | Starts from |
| --- | --- |
| Current possession or health | Exact state, not a text search |
| A returning person or place | Admitted identity and relationship links |
| Remembered wording | The exact source |
| A paraphrased callback | Hybrid search, then the loaded document |
| A broad regional question | A separately budgeted synthesis, not a free dump |

When the application already knows a current character, place or thread is relevant, it supplies that admitted stable identity directly. The current canonical version is a deterministic candidate before any text search. Unknown wording remains an explicit search. A stale identity cannot silently select a similarly named record.

A richer tier may investigate more leads. It cannot receive a more favorable or different truth.

## Creative exploration is not retrieval

For a turn that merits deeper authorship, the Storyteller may search through bounded lenses—echo, contrast, consequence, relationship, dormant thread, setting affordance or controlled serendipity—then inspect exact sources and compare a few private directions before composing one result. Evidence remains evidence. An inferred connection remains interpretation. A proposed direction remains noncanonical until ordinary publication admits what the turn establishes.

This work is optional and costed. Disabling it must still produce a grounded turn. An explicit no-grand-narrative campaign cannot acquire a secret arc merely because a surprising candidate exists. The application stores inspectable queries, sources and concise direction artifacts, not private chain-of-thought.

**Negative:** paying for a stronger model improves interpretation. It does not purchase a better chance of success, a secret future disclosed as fact, or permission to treat model memory of a franchise setting as supplied canon. A multi-day reading of this retrieval is the [Proposed/Blocked evaluation family](behavior-and-quality.md#multi-day-evaluation-family), not ratified acceptance. A discarded narrative plan is not committed truth. Idle minutes do not authorize a provider charge.
