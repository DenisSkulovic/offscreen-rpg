# Organizations, factions and local economic context

The first generated-world direction includes some organizations/factions and both deliberately specified and procedural NPCs. This topic owns group identity, affiliation and its effects. It does not commit the MVP to strategic faction AI or a simulated regional economy.

## Proposed initial group model

An organization has an identity, relevant purpose, locations/resources where needed and explicit member/role relationships. A faction is an organization participating in aligned or conflicting interests; membership need not be exclusive. Membership may affect a supported job, access permission or relationship, but a faction label has no automatic mechanical effect without a defined rule.

For the miner fixture, an employer/group can be associated with the mine, a named contact and available work. A merchant can hold stock independently of that group. These are reference roles, not prescribed Morrowind lore or a global class hierarchy.

Generate useful group lore at the chosen breadth, but instantiate detailed memberships, resources and behavior only where the experience needs them (D047). Preserve their identities and affiliations across sessions. Leave distant institutions coarse until a meaningful connection requires detail. Territorial wars, diplomacy, mass recruitment, production chains and dynamic market equilibrium are later or parked scope.

## NPC population

Distinguish player-specified or source-grounded people from procedural residents, but apply the same persistence and action rules after creation. Procedural NPCs may receive reproducible variation in supported traits and schedules; they do not require individually generated biographies or independent LLM calls. Named NPCs are not automatically omniscient or more mechanically privileged.

An NPC may remain in a place, follow a supported routine or travel through available connections. Location occupancy reflects its actual location, rather than copying the NPC into each visited square. Keep incidental population abstract unless interaction or a consequential event requires individual identity. Regenerating a crowd must not regenerate a previously encountered individual, their inventory or obligations.

Initial affiliation and known facts suffice. Automatic worldwide reputation, all-to-all rumors, sophisticated allegiance changes and per-NPC off-screen conversations remain deferred. DOC-KNOWLEDGE-BELIEFS governs what group members actually know; membership does not imply instant information transfer.

## Established does not mean continuously active

D047 supersedes any inference that all generated NPCs need schedules or position updates. Keep incidental residents aggregate or unspecified. A named person can persist as identity and relevant facts while off-screen behavior remains abstract. A faction can persist as lore, relationships and a few relevant members without a leadership simulator. Add individuals and affiliations when they become relevant, preserving prior statements and distinguishing rumor from fact. Supported routes/routines remain available for actors whose movement actually matters; they are not a population-wide obligation.
