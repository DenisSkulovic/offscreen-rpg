# D-0001 | Modular engine, not an LLM-led chat

**Date:** 2026-11-16 record of the November 2026 working selection.  
**Acceptance:** selected working target. Not a code authorization. Open items in the architecture page remain unratified.  
**Norm:** [selected engine architecture](../technical/engine/selected-architecture.md).

## Choice

Keep a modular monolith: PostgreSQL story authority, pure rule capabilities, boundary-driven time, immutable narrative objects, and a bounded Storyteller that proposes rather than commits.

## Rejected for now

An LLM-led chat as the source of mechanical truth. The rationale is [Alternatives that lose for now](../technical/engine/selected-architecture.md#alternatives-that-lose-for-now). A universal world simulation. Event-sourced microservices before domain seams are stable. A turn-based CRPG engine as the whole product.

## Revisit

Only if a specific shared-world simulation, or measured independent hot domains with stable event semantics, makes the rejected shape necessary. Never revisit LLM-led chat as the sole mechanical authority.

## Continuity on 2026-11-19

The choice, the rejections, and the revisit rule above stay in force. They are the working engine target, and they match the current modular-monolith implementation.

The only scope that moves is the earlier deferral of a learning environment. On 2026-11-19 the owner selected an educational platform laboratory in addition to this decision. Its contract is [Distributed learning target](../technical/platform/topology.md#distributed-learning-target). The laboratory may practice Kubernetes, a message broker, and separately deployable processes on disposable local resources, before a measured production need exists.

That selection does not supersede PostgreSQL story authority, pure rule capabilities, boundary-driven time, immutable narrative objects, or the bounded Storyteller. It does not accept event-sourced campaign state. It does not accept an LLM-led chat as mechanical truth. Splitting a process does not move a rule. Nothing named in the laboratory is deployed by this record.
