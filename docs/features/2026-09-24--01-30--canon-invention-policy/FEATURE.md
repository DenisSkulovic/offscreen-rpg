# Configurable canon invention policy

Status: Agreed and deliberately dormant
Approval: On 2026-09-24 the owner asked to preserve the multi-axis world/lore creativity idea as a prepared feature dossier, leave it dormant and continue the currently higher-priority implementation route.

## Intended outcome

A world author can state how much new fictional material may be added beyond supplied files, and a story owner can choose an equal or stricter preference. The Storyteller receives the resulting per-domain authority on every task, so a strict reconstruction, a grounded expansion and an improvisational sandbox can use the same engine and creative profile without relying on model confidence or temperature as hidden policy.

The feature prevents two opposite long-run failures: a sparse private world becoming random because the model silently fills important gaps, and a playable world becoming inert because every incidental person or place had to be pre-authored. It preserves creative neutrality: the engine enforces the selected permission but does not prefer strictness or invention.

## Representative flow

A prepared familiar-world campaign caps history and cosmology at **Reference only**, permits **Compatible additions** for characters and places, and starts with the **Grounded expansion** preset. The Storyteller may introduce an incidental dockworker or a small marsh camp when useful, then promote one into campaign canon with passage provenance if later continuity depends on it. It may not rely on pretrained setting memory to invent a missing historical event, faction or deity. If that fact matters, it retrieves supplied evidence, avoids the claim or holds honestly.

In a private space setting, the world grants **Broad invention** for characters, places, groups and technology but keeps its authored metaphysics reference-only. The Storyteller can establish a minor asteroid station and its operator, or later a campaign-local institution, while preserving the supplied faster-than-light constraints. Descriptive technology does not acquire executable mechanics unless a supported rule admits them.

An abstract-organism world can map the same domains to relevant acting subjects, regions, collectives, environmental elements and origins without acquiring humanoids, settlements or economies. A setting change affects only tasks prepared afterward. An already captured result retains its exact policy, and no revision rewrites facts that earlier play established.

If a result declares a durable addition above the captured permission, validation rejects it before publication. If prose appears to violate a strict policy without a corresponding structured declaration, the system preserves the exact trace for evaluation rather than pretending string parsing can prove fictional truth.

## Scope and boundaries

Included:

- five descriptive domains: characters and agents, places and routes, groups and institutions, world elements, and history and cosmology;
- three ordered permissions: `reference-only`, `compatible-additions` and `broad-invention`;
- world-owned maximums, optional start-package narrowing and a story-owned equal-or-stricter choice;
- canon-bound, grounded-expansion and open-world presets plus advanced per-domain overrides;
- immutable settings revision capture and task-visible effective policy;
- structured classification and admission of newly created descriptive documents;
- provenance that distinguishes source promotion from campaign invention;
- provider-free strict/private/abstract contrasts and later bounded live evaluation.

Deferred:

- arbitrary user-defined policy domains;
- automatic semantic fact checking, prose parsing or a claim-extraction model;
- verification of third-party fictional canon against the public internet;
- public lore editing, retcon workflows and cross-campaign world-library mutation;
- any mechanical capability inferred from descriptive permission;
- automatic model, temperature, exploration or spending changes based on invention level.

The fixed domains classify authorship permission; they are not a universal ontology. A fact spanning domains must satisfy every applicable restriction. Private possibilities remain noncanonical regardless of invention level. Established source and campaign canon always constrain later additions.

## Acceptance

- Every world foundation has an explicit five-domain ceiling; package-free custom foundations do not inherit an engine default.
- A prepared start can narrow but never widen its pinned world's ceiling, and a story can select only equal or stricter values.
- Creation exposes the three presets and an understandable effective summary; advanced editing shows why a world cap prevents a wider choice.
- A captured Storyteller task contains the exact effective matrix and source revisions. Changing settings afterward does not alter that task or its retry.
- New descriptive-document proposals declare domain and origin class. The provider-facing schema omits permission combinations that captured policy forbids, and semantic admission independently rejects forged or excessive declarations.
- Source-backed promotion of an already established subject remains possible under `reference-only`; unsupported new durable facts do not masquerade as promotions.
- Campaign inventions retain originating passage, domain and permission provenance without fabricated world-library citations.
- The strict famous-world fixture tempts the Storyteller with unloaded recognizable lore and admits no unsupported durable claim. The private-world fixture can add a useful local subject. The abstract fixture remains free of human-world defaults.
- No policy level can create typed effects, rules, inventory, travel access or player decisions through prose.
- Before the first 30–50-turn evaluation, exact held packets prove the policy is present. Any live Sol run remains separately authorized and cost bounded.

## Decisions still needed

No product decision blocks the first implementation slice. Exact player-facing explanatory copy can be refined with the creation UI. Additional domains require evidence from real worlds rather than speculative taxonomy expansion.
