### 5. Extremely economical Storyteller evals; one inference can justify an hour of analysis

**Owner direction:** eventual tuning could begin as soon as tomorrow when the game actually produces a scene. Model may be too weak, too capable/expensive, poor at narration or missing context; tuning is empirical and not only prompt wording. **No corporate evaluation budget.** User is unemployed and treats every dollar as household money. Never automatically spend $3, $5, $10 or even $0.75 for evaluations, and do not assume that a ceiling is an expenditure target. One carefully framed live model call and prolonged human inspection may be the correct experiment. Want the best cost-aware approach informed by standard practice, without adopting a heavy hosted evaluation vendor by default.

**Proposed evaluation hierarchy:** (0) $0 deterministic correctness/trace/context/authorization/canon/format and budget checks; (1) $0 replay/re-score/archive old already-paid outputs under new rubrics; (2) exactly ONE preflighted, explicitly authorized paid inference with a tiny verified worst-case spend and full trace; (3) paired models/recipes only when a prior result justifies another call. Never auto-run a paid judge, retries, background evals or broad benchmark sweeps. Separate measured human narrative quality (coherence, specificity, restrained invention, pacing, continuity, desire to continue) from hard objective validity, context retrieval, tool behavior, tokens and price. An absent provider receipt/unknown charge is not zero.

**Existing source to reuse:** `packages/lab-support/src/{live-evaluation,memory-evaluator,creative-exploration-evaluator,evidence-packing-evaluator,retrieval-oracle}.ts`, `packages/contracts/src/live-evaluation.ts`, current unit-price/reservation and preflight. Existing LLM eval support is narrower than a general Writer-tuning workbench; do not create another duplicate harness or assert production proof.

### 6. Strongly configurable runtime policies, not hard-coded subscription logic

**Owner direction:** long-term users will have different plan/tier capabilities and budgets; product/business staff must ultimately control allowed models, sampling (temperature etc where supported), context length/packing, max rounds/tool calls, model/query/output token sizes, soft warnings, hard stops, research/repairs, timeouts and currency limits. Admin configuration persists as versioned data, and Chamber can trial hypothetical policies against an isolated story. Product defaults/seed rows are acceptable, but don't hard-code commercial plans in business logic. "Infinitely configurable" means extensible bounded typed dimensions and flexible values, **not arbitrary executable config or bypass of safety/security/transactional invariants**.

**Existing architecture already specifies:** entitlement profile ≠ funding allowance ≠ task recipe ≠ creative profile; versioned platform caps and account grants intersect; soft conserve targets ≠ hard money/dispatch ceiling; effective policy is frozen with contributing version pins for one operation, so a later Admin edit does not rewrite an ongoing billable attempt. Missing configuration fails closed, money is strictly reserved then settled in existing SQL custody. Subscription tiers must be data mapping to grants, never `if (plan==='premium')` scattered across Writer. No silent commercial fallback or auto top-up. Pricing/route capabilities must be verified for each selected model. Provider credentials remain separately protected.

**Concrete drift found:** `packages/story-engine/src/storyteller/writer-continuation.ts` currently hard-codes `maxModelRounds:4`, `maxInputTokens:8000`, `maxGeneratedTokens:2000`, `maxMicrousd:'250000'`, `deadlineMs:120000`.
