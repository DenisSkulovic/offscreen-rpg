# Designs | lifecycle catalog

**Lifecycle and retirement reference:** [Repository structure](../../REPOSITORY-STRUCTURE.md#4-work-is-temporary-coordination-not-product-truth). Every dossier in `open/` must have an unresolved decision and next review; accepted or historical designs leave the active tree only after canon/ledger/link reconciliation. No age-only deletion.

Active investigations belong in `open/`; deferred worthwhile investigations belong in [backlog](backlog/README.md); terminal dossiers belong in [archive](archive/README.md). Neither backlog nor archive grants implementation authority. [Work taxonomy](../WORK-TAXONOMY.md) · [Current work selection](../QUEUE.md).

**Review command:** `pnpm repo:retirement` inventories working designs, their approximate age and Markdown backlinks, and aged archived Changes. No automatic deletion. 

**Retirement:** [Design ledger](ledger/2026-11.md) indexes dispositions with immutable provenance. **Design lifecycle (owner decision 2026-11-27):** `work/designs/open/` holds active investigations with a named owner, unresolved decision and review trigger; `work/designs/backlog/` holds deferred worthwhile investigations with a reason and reactivation trigger; `work/designs/archive/` holds terminal accepted, completed, rejected, cancelled or superseded dossiers as a browsable review cache. Placement never authorizes implementation, live mutation or spending. Accepted decisions must be promoted to canonical owners; implementation and acceptance remain with their Changes. Record disposition, exact original full SHA/path, canonical successor, unique evidence and repaired references in the design ledger. Review archive retention after seven days by default; preserve unique evidence and require explicit authorized pruning, never age-only removal. Git remains permanent recovery; archive is not canon or a shadow queue.

**Design lifecycle:** follow [design-led renewal](../../.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md): invest in research, target architecture, concrete specimens, repeated critique and authorized experiments here, before implementation planning. A recorded [formal architecture defense](../../.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#mandatory-formal-architecture-defense-and-convergence) is mandatory before promotion; material objections require revision and re-defense, not a cosmetic sign-off. After self-defense, the [mandatory independent committee](../../.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#mandatory-independent-design-committee) in a fresh ChatGPT conversation must commit an affirmative verdict for the selected candidate and scope before promotion. Every committee outcome (including rejection, evidence-gating and early reviews) must be committed to the reviewed branch and verified; chat-only reviews are incomplete. Select clean-slate or evolutionary posture explicitly; legacy internals are not the target. Investigate and compare alternatives here → explicitly review/accept product outcomes → reconcile accepted requirements into the existing `docs/product/` and `docs/technical/` owners → only then choose direct implementation, bounded `work/changes/` packets when delegation is useful, or a hybrid. An open design is not a selected Change, and a committed proposal is not an approved spec. **Each remaining folder requires a named next decision and review date; entries that are merely historical will be retired in subsequent evidence-backed passes, not by blind deletion.** The [deferred player experience renewal](backlog/player-experience-renewal/README.md) records the reactivation gate for remaining creative design.

**Design scale:** use flexible profiles; reconsider beyond 15 current documents or 100 KiB. One Design may produce multiple bounded Changes. **Formal submission quality:** apply [the shared artifact standard](../../.agents/references/planning-artifact-quality.md) before defense. Exploration may have arbitrary useful research/specimens; formal submissions need a coherent target and clear current/evidence routes. Commit a rejecting verdict for unreadable or contradictory folders rather than reviewing isolated amendments.
