# Designs | lifecycle catalog

**Lifecycle and retirement reference:** [Repository structure](../../REPOSITORY-STRUCTURE.md#4-work-is-temporary-coordination-not-product-truth). Every dossier in `open/` must have an unresolved decision and next review; accepted or historical designs leave the active tree only after canon/ledger/link reconciliation. No age-only deletion.

Active investigations belong in `open/`; deferred worthwhile investigations belong in [backlog](backlog/README.md); terminal dossiers belong in [archive](archive/README.md). Neither backlog nor archive grants implementation authority. [Work taxonomy](../WORK-TAXONOMY.md) · [Current work selection](../QUEUE.md).

**Review command:** `pnpm repo:retirement` inventories working designs, their approximate age and Markdown backlinks, and aged archived Changes. No automatic deletion. 

**Retirement:** [Design ledger](ledger/2026-11.md) indexes dispositions with immutable provenance. **Design lifecycle (owner decision 2026-11-27):** `work/designs/open/` holds active investigations with a named owner, unresolved decision and review trigger; `work/designs/backlog/` holds deferred worthwhile investigations with a reason and reactivation trigger; `work/designs/archive/` holds terminal accepted, completed, rejected, cancelled or superseded dossiers as a browsable review cache. Placement never authorizes implementation, live mutation or spending. Accepted decisions must be promoted to canonical owners; implementation and acceptance remain with their Changes. Record disposition, exact original full SHA/path, canonical successor, unique evidence and repaired references in the design ledger. Review archive retention after seven days by default; preserve unique evidence and require explicit authorized pruning, never age-only removal. Git remains permanent recovery; archive is not canon or a shadow queue.

**Design lifecycle:** follow [design-led renewal](../../.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md) at the [depth the risk requires](../../AGENTS.md#investigation-depth). Focused work under a settled contract does not open a dossier. Evolutionary subsystem work uses a compact artifact and proportionate review. Material new architecture invests in research, specimens, experiments and critique here, before implementation planning of the selected evolution. A dossier records that evolution and, compactly, later candidates. It leaves the active tree when the evolution is accepted and promoted. The wider subsystem may continue in a later design. [Progressive resolution](../../.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#progressive-resolution-owner-decision-2026-11-27) owns the boundary, the stopping rule and what reviewers judge. A recorded [formal architecture defense](../../.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#mandatory-formal-architecture-defense-and-convergence) and an [independent review](../../.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#mandatory-independent-design-committee) are required for that material scope before promotion. A fresh chat is one valid review context. Only unresolved Critical and High findings block the affected scope. The reviewer delivers a SHA-bound verdict. An authorized reviewer persists it, or a read-only reviewer delivers it for the custodian. Promotion uses the persisted verdict matched to the reviewed candidate. A delivered verdict awaiting persistence is not an incomplete analysis. Select clean-slate or evolutionary posture explicitly. Legacy internals are not the target. Investigate and compare alternatives here, review and accept product outcomes, reconcile accepted requirements into the existing `docs/product/` and `docs/technical/` owners, and only then choose direct implementation, bounded `work/changes/` packets, or a hybrid. An open design is not a selected Change, and a committed proposal is not an approved spec. **Each remaining folder requires a named next decision and review date; entries that are merely historical will be retired in subsequent evidence-backed passes, not by blind deletion.** The [deferred player experience renewal](backlog/player-experience-renewal/README.md) records the reactivation gate for remaining creative design.

**Design scale:** use flexible profiles. Reconsider a dossier beyond 15 current documents or 100 KiB when that size prevents a coherent review. One Design may produce multiple bounded Changes. **Submission quality:** apply [the shared artifact standard](../../.agents/references/planning-artifact-quality.md) and the severity policy before defense. Exploration may keep the research and specimens it needs. A formal submission needs a coherent target and a clear current route. A fundamentally incoherent folder stops review because further review would be unreliable. A correctable defect does not, by itself, end the rest of the review.
