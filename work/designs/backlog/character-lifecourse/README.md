## Reactivation

Reopen when character-state and Game Authority boundaries are sufficiently established and a concrete scenario requires growth, lasting conditions, transformation or irreversible consequences. Settle admission, authority, continuity, risk, replay and acceptance before selecting mechanics or schemas. Owner acceptance and applicable design defense precede promotion.

## Existing guarantees

- [Earned life](../../../../docs/product/foundations/vision.md#earned-life): earned completion and campaign-permitted loss remain real; reading or waiting for a model does not age the world.
- [World-independent foundation](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/rules.md#world-independent-foundation) and [protagonists](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/worlds-and-characters.md#flexible-settings-and-protagonists): capability applicability depends on current form; absence is neither zero nor unlimited capability. A transformation may add abilities when admitted.
- [Continuity](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md#people-objects-and-capabilities): established lasting changes constrain later choices; atmosphere need not become tracked state.
- [Storyteller boundaries](../../../../docs/product/storyteller/behavior-and-quality.md#selected-solo-proof-boundary): no automatic life ending; a deliberately concluded story remains readable without new scheduled developments.
- [Return and journal](../../../../docs/product/experience/return-and-journal.md#after-absence): recap reports admitted history and current decision without rewriting it.

## Seven unresolved lifecycle decisions

| Event | Decision to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Growth and skills | How capabilities are earned; whether a shared skill-tree vocabulary is useful. |
| Injury and recovery | Typed lasting conditions versus continuity constraints; admission and recovery. |
| Death or irreversible loss | Who admits fatal outcomes, whether they end a story, and whether reversal is possible. |
| Aging | Whether a world admits age and what elapsed fictional time changes. |
| Form transitions | Admission, capability changes and stale prepared-action invalidation without rewriting prior receipts. |
| Player return | How admitted consequential changes are communicated; whether proposed bodily changes belong in return accounts. |
| Story ending | Distinguish finished adventure, fatal loss and continuing life; whether separate commits and who chooses. |

## Five unresolved authority decisions

| Authority | Decision to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Fatal outcomes | Commit authority, ruleset admission and whether narration may precede a committed effect. |
| Fairness and legibility | Whether irreversible or fatal stakes require advance disclosure beyond existing apparent-risk guarantees. |
| Irreversible changes | Representation and allowed reversal of lasting constraints. |
| Account and story fork | Whether fatal loss forks account or story; how survivors continue; locked settings are not permadeath and forks do not rewrite history. |
| Audit | Who can inspect irreversible commits; player chronology versus separate staff forensic record. |

## Historical evidence and exclusions

The original dossier records applicable-ability checks, optional paired health fields, fact and quantity effects, world-obligation conditions and locked campaign settings at `b3113814`. These are not a general transformation, wound, death or permadeath implementation. It preserves the humanoid, healthless microbe and storyteller fixture contrasts, exact source anchors and negative evidence. A stored score does not imply applicability; absent health is not HP zero or infinite health.

No universal HP, fatigue, age, level, skill-tree or disease schema, D&D full combat, mortality policy or new product page is selected. The [world-neutral capability kernel](../../../../docs/technical/engine/selected-architecture.md#world-neutral-capability-kernel) remains conceptual, not a schema.
