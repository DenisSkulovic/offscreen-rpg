## Consent and resolution matrix

All group-specific selections remain unratified. For each decision settle actors, permitted action, deadline, competing response, commit authority and stale/late/withdrawn consent.

| Domain | Open decision and constraints |
| --- | --- |
| Invitation | Private invite/join/withdrawal, expiry, readiness, who may send/accept/start; invitation is not Start, stale or incompatible preview cannot start. |
| Participant control | Grant/transfer/revoke character control; host versus collective pause, pace and Storyteller authority; one vote per person not selected; cannot silently shorten published opportunity. |
| Action arbitration | Collect and resolve incompatible intentions, shared revision and operation identity, transaction authority, stale/revised/late responses; no conflicting double commit or last-write-wins. |
| Deadline and absence | Wait, eligible fallback, temporary consent, publication and expiry authority, manual-versus-expiry arbitration and revocation; no automatic group timeout selected; absent eligible consent hold. |
| Character agency | Effects on other characters and allowed delegation of combat, relationships or trade; absence, browser closure and host title grant no new character authority. |
| Funding | Payer/sponsor admission, refusal, competing funding and withdrawal; keep customer allowance, company spend and fictional resources distinct; no split-pay or wallet switching assumed; in-flight charge pins original payer, future dispatch rechecks. |
| Leave and rejoin | Who may remove/rejoin, access revocation, pending intents and remaining character control; no silent death/deletion/transfer; agreed continuation or hold; no replay of settled outcome; access-version fence and current-state notifications. |

## Alternatives for investigation

- Whole-party lockstep: strong shared coherence, slow participant may stall everyone; investigate narrow first proof with individual intent ownership.
- Per-character proposals in shared scene: preferred historical candidate, requires arbitration, revision and deadline design.
- Host-controlled party: simple but contradicts independent character agency unless distinct explicit consent is designed.
- Independent simultaneous subworlds: rich flexibility, higher consistency/context/orchestration cost; separate later scope.

No candidate is selected. Public matchmaking, PvP, large worlds, independent scenes, voice/video and live cursors are outside narrow group research.

## Six offline experiment scenarios

1. Both agree to approach a bridge; one inspects while another prepares rope.
2. Player disconnects after submitting an intention.
3. Both attempt incompatible uses of one item.
4. Player returns after a settled group decision.
5. One requests pause while another wants to continue.
6. One attempts to command the other's character through custom text.

Assess agency, visible required consent, coherent outcomes, stale/duplicate/revoked commands, privacy, funding and absence behavior. These are research specimens, not approved acceptance fixtures.
