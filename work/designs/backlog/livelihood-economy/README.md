**State:** Deferred, owner decision 2026-11-27. **Owner:** product owner with Game Authority and Storyteller design owners. No selected Change or implementation permission.

**Reason:** economy capabilities should follow the chosen mechanical authority and a concrete world need, not force an early universal simulator. DOC32 completed documentation/decision ownership, not economy mechanics.

**Reactivate when:** relevant mechanical authority and accepted activity/effect contracts are stable enough for design, and a selected playable world needs livelihood or exchange, or the owner explicitly commissions independent investigation. Refresh current HEAD and canonical contracts before design.

## Selected constraints (unchanged)

[Rules](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/rules.md), [possessions and exchanges](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md#possessions-and-exchanges), [actions and commitments](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/actions-and-commitments.md), [earned life](../../../../docs/product/foundations/vision.md#earned-life) and [time and autonomy](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/time-and-autonomy.md) own selected meaning. No reward before committed earned effect; maintain exact ownership/counts and remainder, reject stale or duplicate effects, preserve consent and interruption boundaries. An offered activity is not accepted work. A contextual price is not a simulated market. Gifts and promises need no universal price. A nonmonetary world is valid.

## Unresolved decisions

| Question | Alternatives / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Economic units | Shared resource vocabulary versus world-defined quantities; no universal currency. |
| Pricing | Contextual valuation versus persistent price/wage rules. |
| NPC inventory | Durable individual possession versus simulated merchant stock. |
| Employer authority | Who may offer, refuse, establish or bind obligations; protect player consent. |
| Exploitation | Whether and how a world depicts coercive labor and intervention; no selected labor law, abuse mechanic or tax. |
| Scale | Individual transactions versus population/market simulation; dedicated trade systems not first-solo requirements. |
| Absent labor | Accepted work completion, whether it is employment and what reward applies; worlds without labor valid. |
| Recurring work | Compare existing accepted-plan mechanisms before inventing another engine; bounded authorization, iteration identity, stop at new required decision/hold, no repeated unearned reward. |

Compare a wage/barter world with a nonmonetary abstract or microscopic world. Discriminating cases: stale holder, duplicate purchase, interrupted work, estimated but unearned reward, no declared currency, and a recurring commitment reaching a new decision. Do not infer economic mechanics from prose or require an economic agent per NPC. [Cross-domain research](../../../../research/product-reality/DOMAIN-COVERAGE-2026-11-18.md#autonomy-and-agency) retains broader autonomy questions.
