## Important no-false-positive decisions

- `find work/changes/archive` returning none **is expected in the lean export**, not proof Cursor deleted hundreds of Done packets.
- The archive contains **zero** `node_modules`. Missing `unified` while invoking `wiki-health`/maintenance in the export is expected and not evidence those tools fail on developers' installed Node 24 workspace.
- The isolated RabbitMQ test proves durable redelivery on one replica, **not** business-event integration or exactly-once delivery.
- The SeaweedFS restart test proves Pod restart on the existing claim, **not** backup across cluster deletion, production durability or application S3 routing.
- The 98/98 canonical navigation score measures reachability, **not** policy consistency, correct link anchors or Storyteller recall quality.
- `STATUS.md` is relatively cautious and explicitly states the game is not deployed on Kubernetes, checkpoint history is missing and the DM context pack is not yet assembled. Do **not** rewrite those factual negatives as bugs merely because roadmap promises future work.
