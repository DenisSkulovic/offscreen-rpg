## Proposed canonical additions versus proposals versus noncanonical guides

**Likely good additions, pending owner test:** content-package authoring guide; small cross-boundary HTTP contract; bounded local operational recovery and release-readiness how-tos; decision locator rather than giant decision archive.

**Design-only until ratification:** player data rights, shared play membership/consent, gameplay long-life domain semantics (economy, bodily progression, spatiality and socially consequential relationships), abuse/policy controls. Their absence from *selected* docs is not proof a universal mechanism should exist.

**Likely restructure not new page:** acceptance scenarios into separately classified benchmark sections; substantial H2s in context/memory and data-and-persistence only if independent owners and update cadence differ; local-development runbook into onboarding vs advanced troubleshooting if copied commands/links can be migrated without loss.

## Coverage is a matrix, not a count of pages

For each important domain question, record:
- product promise owner or **not yet decided**;
- technical target owner or **not yet selected**;
- realized source and test evidence or **not implemented/unknown**;
- user/operator/author task guide or **not yet safe to execute**;
- explicit negative case and dependent claims;
- whether there is **reason not to document more yet**.

A gap is closed when a reader can find the exact authority and know the remaining decision/implementation uncertainty, not when we created another document by name.
