## Change kinds: what Cursor may do

- **Implementation:** only the named existing wiki/search/maintainer scripts and focused provider-free fixtures. No gameplay source changes, paid models, destructive operations or product ratification.
- **Documentation:** canonical existing pages or *planned additions*, with actual inbound/fragment checks and seven-column ledger. Every new page must beat the alternative of one precise section in an existing owner.
- **Proposed policy:** create or refine work/designs decision material and link existing selected rules; do not add new normative product/gameplay/legal rules. Owner approval is a blocker, not a task to guess through.
- **Structural migration:** record old path#heading → new path#heading, preserve every unique claim and critical negative, repair all inbound links. A split that increases search burden should be cancelled.
