## Decision

The earlier wiki work solved **basic routes, Markdown reachability, source-scoped retrieval and close-time impact accounting**. It did **not** yet solve all four independent problems:

1. **Authority-safe retrieval:** section-level proposed clauses inside otherwise selected documents can be misclassified as normative.
2. **Dense, useful knowledge relationships:** ordinary links are far ahead of meaningful contract dependencies. A reachable article is not necessarily discoverable by the question that needs it.
3. **Coverage of distinct reader jobs:** specifications exist for many internals, but authoring, integration, operations, recovery and unselected product-domain policy may lack their own coherent owner or practical task route.
4. **Actual end-to-end comprehension:** the editorial program's held-out cross-corpus examination was explicitly not executed. A search/index with all pages reachable can still answer the wrong question.

The goal is not to reward document or link counts. It is to lower wrong-authority answers, missed mandatory dependencies, time-to-safe-first-edit and recoverability of important knowledge, at controlled reviewer and maintenance cost.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- A proposal cannot turn into a ratified product rule merely by being written in \`docs/product\`.
- A technical target is not evidence of implemented code, nor is an actual code symbol authority to revise product purpose.
- Historical/omitted files in a lean snapshot are **not missing repository documents**. Archive, old evidence and legacy research were intentionally excluded.
- No global requirement for mandatory links, minimum pages per folder, frontmatter everywhere, new vector databases, paid model calls, automatic canon promotion or unreviewed large rewrites.

## Distinction between types of candidate

**Confirmed structural defect:** observed by local source/reproducible static extraction (e.g. page-level authority leakage; links and missing task routes).

**Coverage gap:** a genuine reader question lacks a focused documented owner or explicit decision status, even if fragments exist in multiple files.

**Potential product-design gap:** a domain (economy, life progression, spatial play, participant consent) is important to the vision but needs human ratification before a new selected contract. The Change may produce an evidence-backed *proposal*, not silently accept gameplay rules.

**No need for new page:** current owner already answers the question cleanly with a good link. Just improve the route or record reviewed unchanged.

## Work package integrity

A move or split needs exact inbound path and anchor migration, unique-claim preservation and selected/proposed classification. Never delete evidence merely to improve graph health.
