## Composition takeaways, not a moodboard pile

- **A promise must stay legible without art.** Photo, portrait and generated illustration are optional surfaces, not the semantic content layer.
- **Documentary browsing is not live gameplay.** Journal can borrow source-index and museum-catalogue ideas, but must not turn historical reading into a fake interactive replay.
- **Distinct type identities should map to actual jobs.** At least a platform/system face, an immersive prose face and a source/index voice; no more font families without demonstrable need.
- **Visual density varies by task.** The ambient stage, rapid decision, long-form reader and 2,000-passage overview cannot be one identical composition.
- **The current trend toward large editorial typography is not a license to abandon copy fitting.** Use real full-length passages, Cyrillic tests and 320px layouts, not fashionable one-sentence mockups.
- **Motion should disclose semantic change, not fabricate it.** A passage transition occurs after publication, not merely after client clock expiry.

## Research limitations and future studies

We have not run competitor accessibility/contrast tests or studied complete user journeys in their products. Future rounds should add original annotated 2026 browser screenshots or licensed references and measure navigation/prose readability, rather than inferring entire UI systems from promotional key art. The source URLs above are references for **design critique**, not dependencies and not copies to place inside the product.
