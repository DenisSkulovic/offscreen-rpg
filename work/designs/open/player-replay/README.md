# Player replay | open design

**Status: proposed. Not an accepted player feature.**

A player may eventually want to reread published history without developer artifacts. The proposed shape is [player replay v1](player-replay-experience-v1.md). That experience is not specified as a shippable journal mode, and it is not the staff forensic Turn Capsule.

Staff reconstruction, raw prompts and evidence tiers stay in [observability](../../../../docs/technical/observability/identities-and-evidence.md) and [admin authority](../../../../docs/product/admin/authority-and-governance.md). Do not merge investigation payloads into the player journal by citing this draft.
