# Product renewal | E1 acceptance

## Selected boundary

This is a documentation-only evolution. Acceptance demonstrates a better product specification, not gameplay, UX usability, game quality or runtime certification. Every one of the 31 existing pages has a disposition in [TARGET](TARGET.md); no page count is a proxy for semantic success.

| Required property | Positive check | Discriminating negative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intended first solo experience | Reader locates prepared/custom entry, admitted scene, time rule and solo oracle | Mandatory character wizard, mandatory preview or latency-driven fictional time appears as accepted requirement |
| Meaning survives | Compare changed claims and exceptions against baseline canonical owners; trace deliberate moves | A rule is removed because its implementation is weak, or an unselected mechanic is treated as canon |
| POC separation | Reader distinguishes fixture facts and current UI/narrator from intended behavior | Existing narrow wait, fork or scripted scenario is called general product acceptance |
| Player and staff authority | Check action consent, time, historical isolation, privacy, content rights and proposed staff grants | Free clock advance, unauthorized dice/effects, parent mutation, player exposure to staff payload, implied role or distribution grant |
| Transfer integrity | Unique implementation facts have a surviving owner and evidence/provenance; link resolves on final main candidate | Source-only fact disappears or link depends on unmerged technical branch |
| Retrieval and readability | Independent reader reaches one governing heading per domain via the product index; prose is cohesive | Must reconstruct the product rule from archive or a wall of contradictory amendment text |

## Proof and review

For the eventual Change batch, compare claim/heading before and after, preserve negative constraints and typed dependencies, use existing DOC semantic review and planned-impact requirements. Record page-level dispositions including justified no-ops; use existing shape-ops/receipt when an actual owner moves/splits. Do not manufacture a separate permanent ledger. At join run `python scripts/check_docs.py`, `node scripts/repo-tools/docs-health.mjs` (pnpm repo:docs-health equivalent) and `node scripts/repo-tools/wiki-health.mjs` (pnpm wiki:health equivalent). Compare against same full-checkout baseline. No new affected broken links/anchors or orphan routes; inherited unrelated errors are not automatically blockers. In lean snapshot many archive routes are absent; distinguish unavailable from broken in full checkout. Semantic review, not link count, owns acceptance. No runtime tests or live infrastructure needed. Evidence state: NOT RUN for the future rewritten docs.

The reviewer should sample high-risk claims and reject an apparent improvement that is only a status banner. The planner must map exact changed claims and protected cross-Design handoffs before packet readiness, not delegate decisions about mechanics or policy.

## Shared delivery boundary

These are Designs only. Independent committee review of the selected E1 documentation architecture comes first, followed by separate owner acceptance. No Change packet, Ready status, queue entry, implementation commission, branch creation, code edit, live operation, migration, permission, paid call or new proof credit is authorized by this dossier. After acceptance the planner prepares the bounded Change batch against refreshed main. At commission use `implementation/<design-slug>` per the current standard; each constituent branch joins its own design integration branch, and Done packets retire there before reporting. Main receives the Design only after joint acceptance.

Independent preparation and disjoint domain edits are allowed after commission, subject to actual ownership. The technical Design is the first publication dependency: product realization facts that leave product must already survive in a technical/source owner on main before product integration removes them. Both branches may be prepared concurrently. Do not merge a product branch that points at unmerged headings or loses unique facts; a temporary retained paragraph is an acceptable intermediate state, not an E1 end state. Freeze the transfer set during planning, including non-obvious transfers from UI, staff and scenarios, not just gameplay and Storyteller. If an active technical owner is protected, keep the fact in place with its current scope until the owner has accepted and integrated its successor; do not force a cross-branch write. Product integration rechecks technical owner routes on actual main. No circular dependency: technical renewal may link stable existing product headings, never require the product rewrite to land first.

The exact current integration SHA, concurrent ownership and changed files must be rechecked before packet planning and again before integration. The frozen snapshot proves neither the present live HEAD nor an active branch's uncommitted state. Medium and Low findings are retained in durable owners; only material Critical/High issues block affected work.
