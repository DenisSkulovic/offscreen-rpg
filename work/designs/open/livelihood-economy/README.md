# Livelihood and economy | unratified proposal

**Status: Proposed. Not selected.** This page is an open design. It is not a product rule, not a technical contract, and not permission to implement.

**Reader question:** Which livelihood and exchange facts are already selected, and which choices about units, prices, employers, inventories, and absent labor stay inside one world?

This page is the unratified map for that question. The [historical domain research](../../../../research/product-reality/DOMAIN-COVERAGE-2026-11-18.md#domain-questions) retains the coverage classification. This investigation owns the unanswered shared-resource-name question. Selected promises stay on the headings named below. The shared-name choice stays unanswered here. `docs/product/gameplay/livelihood-and-exchange.md` is not created. Selected possession continuity stays on [Possessions and exchanges](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md#possessions-and-exchanges).

A wage example stays an example. A microorganism story with no employer and no coins still fits the foundation.

## Two worlds

These worlds show the same selected causality under different rulesets. Neither world is a currency table.

### A village that admits wages and barter

A campaign may admit a counted resource and a shift. Five coins and a payment of three leave two when that quantity is declared and a committed effect spends it. A spear can change holders when `item.transfer.v1` names the current holder. A gift or a promise can matter with no price. The storyteller may judge one contextual price for that scene. Starting a shift does not pay the shift. An estimate of the pay is not owned. Narration that says the character was paid does not create the coins.

That village may speak of an employer, a wage, and a barter. Those words belong to that ruleset.

### A world where money and work do not apply

A microorganism story, or an abstract pattern, need not have money, employment, or a profession. Two organisms can exchange a chemical signal. The exchange updates what each holds. It does not open a coin ledger. A contribution process exists in that world only when its ruleset admits the activity. Contribution points are progress toward that activity's captured requirement. They are not wages. The missing currency row is the world.

**Normal:** a ruleset that admits a counted resource spends three and keeps two, through a committed effect. **Negative:** the village wage becomes a universal currency table, a microbe must earn coins to advance, every farm needs a resident economic agent, or prose about wages grants money without a committed effect.

## Open choices

Each row is unratified. Naming it here does not select it.

| Open choice | What stays unchosen |
| --- | --- |
| Economic units | Whether any resource name is shared across worlds, beyond a quantity one ruleset already declared. The catalogue leaves this unanswered. |
| Pricing | A scene may use one contextual price. No price list, market, or wage schedule is selected. |
| NPC inventory | A story item has one holder key. That key is not a shop's stock. Simulated merchant inventories stay outside the initial product. |
| Employer authority | Who may offer work, refuse it, or bind another actor is unchosen. An admitted activity is a player-selected intention. |
| Exploitation and abuse | Whether a world depicts coercive labor, and who may stop it, is unchosen. This page selects no labor law, abuse mechanic, or tax. |
| Simulation scale | The first playable target withholds dedicated trade systems and a simulated population. A later market's size is unchosen. |
| Labor finishing while absent | Accepted work can finish while the player is away. Whether that work is employment, whether it pays a wage, and whether a world with no work has labor to finish, stay unchosen. |

## Inspected contracts

Technical game effects are the only executable boundary. This inspection does not add an effect. Source was read at `b3113814` and was not executed again.

| Contract | Selected in source | Still open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `item.create.v1` | A narrative continuation introduces one keyed item with a label and a holder. Keys are unique. The registry rejects a duplicate and growth past 50 items. The passage and the effects commit together, or not at all. | The label is not a price. The holder key is not an inventory system. |
| `item.transfer.v1` | The move applies only when the declared prior holder still matches, and the new holder differs. A stale holder rejects the whole transaction. An immediate outcome may carry at most one such transfer. It does not create an item. | A successful transfer is not a sale, a wage, or a tax. |
| `quantity.change.v1` | A character may carry optional declared quantities. A committed delta changes one declared id. An effect that names a missing quantity is rejected. Immediate-outcome admission also rejects a zero delta. Contribution completion checks that the quantity was declared. | No quantity id is a universal coin. An empty quantity list is valid. |
| `contribution.v1` | At each captured cadence the engine rolls the captured check and adds that attempt's success or failure contribution. Elapsed time alone does not complete the activity. The displayed estimate does not award progress. Completion applies only that action's captured effect list, which may be empty. | Contribution points are not a profession, a wage unit, or proof that every world has work. |
| Time while absent | An admitted activity settles due boundaries without a model call, including while the browser is closed, until its accepted limit or a stop. Pause, a required decision, and a mere offer do not advance that work. | A quiet completion may apply a captured quantity delta. That delta is not a pay schedule for worlds that never declared the quantity. |

The same mechanical path can omit quantities, currency, and a human calendar. [Opportunity boundary](../../../../docs/technical/engine/mechanics-and-capabilities.md#opportunity-boundary) records that those omissions are authored content. They do not require a second engine.

## Refused ratification

This proposal does not publish `docs/product/gameplay/livelihood-and-exchange.md`. It does not add a universal currency, a wage table, a profession, a tax, or a new effect kind. A later owner may ratify a distinct player promise and then choose an owner. Until that acceptance, the selected headings above remain the rules.
