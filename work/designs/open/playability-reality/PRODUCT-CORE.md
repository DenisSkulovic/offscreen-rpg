# REAL product target | The smallest convincing life

**Epistemic status:** targeted product hypothesis based on selected solo-first direction; not ratified as a new exclusive campaign/creation policy, not demonstrated gameplay. Canonical owners remain docs/product/foundations/vision.md, docs/product/gameplay/acceptance-scenarios.md, docs/product/gameplay/actions-and-commitments.md, docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md, and docs/product/gameplay/time-and-autonomy.md.

## Player promise, phrased as a falsifiable experience

I can take a modest role in a constrained world; the Storyteller gives me an interesting situation and honestly bounded options; my choice is mine; effort consumes fictional time only when I admit it; a consequence persists across interruptions and reloads; people remember relevant things **if the game actually recorded them**; returning after being away gives something meaningful to attend to, not an invented autonomous decision or a paid spinner.

A good play session is not proven by valid JSON or a correct timer. Human value requires at least one genuine tradeoff, a consequence that shapes later options, source-backed social continuity, and some degree of surprise within the game's consent envelope.

## Recommended single-session slice

Use a rights-clean, small fictional **harbor/beacon** setting as the design fixture, drawing from research/authored-scenarios/harbor-session.md (Mara, work A/B/W, a stranger, a badge, 4 credits). Those are target facts from **authored research**, not a claim the full scenario already runs in production. Avoid copying brand-specific Vvardenfell lore into a publicly hosted demo.

1. The player sees a practical problem and two or three meaningful offered intentions. One is a quiet activity; one exposes a different tradeoff. The engine authors none of those options by rummaging through inventory.
2. The player accepts repair or equivalent work. It holds a real progress clock, survives browser close and can pause/interrupt at a declared boundary; inference is not used every tick.
3. A supported development interrupts work. The player may converse, inspect or withdraw; the original job does not silently resume and the Storyteller does not force a canned dialogue path.
4. A consequential outcome commits once. The narration must obey receipts and provide only still-valid offers. The offered resumption must be explicitly reauthorized.
5. A person or object becomes socially significant. A tiny **optional social-continuity extension** tests a named gift and explicit promise; do not claim it is part of the existing GS-01…GS-07 fixture until independently composed and admitted.
6. Several unrelated actions and a restart later, the return can use a source-backed detail correctly, or transparently hold/abstain when required evidence is absent.
7. The player sees one understandable account of what progressed in absence. An unchanged quiet world is permitted; never invent an event merely to show movement.

## Core invariants (not reopened by this proposal)

- Only an accepted intention moves fictional time or authorizes a consequential action; browser close is not permission to choose.
- A model-written transfer or promise is not a ledger effect. Item-holder identity and quantity come from committed truth.
- Uncertain provider result, exhausted budget and stale source do not trigger hidden dispatches or retroactively retcon the scene.
- Every newly required person/place/relationship record has source identity, version/authority/visibility and an explicit publication gate.
- A private possibility, rumor, reported testimony and confirmed canon remain distinguishable.
- No need to render a 3D map, simulate a town, invent 200 NPC histories or process an unoffered natural-language action to validate V0.

## What success is, and is not

**Mechanically passable:** accepted activity resolves precisely once; interrupted work retains progress; passage is published once; the latest offer is current; a canonical document can be read after restart; no extra model calls during quiet work.

**Human-play passable (not yet observed):** the owner voluntarily chooses to continue; choices feel meaningfully different; two important recalled details are accurate and source-linked; the player can explain why a consequence mattered; subjective boredom is low enough that a repeat is attractive. These are experiment ratings, not assertions.

**Fail despite green tests:** consecutive menus repeat the same choice in disguise; gift is treated as accepted despite holder transfer failure; a stranger's unverified assertion becomes world truth; a record exists in storage but never reaches the model; a quiet shift is merely an expensive progress bar; model prose changes a committed effect.

## The constrained-agency question

Selected V0 options are authored, not freely typed intentions. That is a deliberate reliability and spend limit. Evaluate when the player thinks of a reasonable unoffered action. Record *which* intention was missing, whether an offered alternative met the underlying goal, and whether the failure spoiled play. Do not solve this prospectively by building a general intent compiler. A separately priced "ask about another approach" interaction is a candidate only after evidence and owner choice.

## Deferred choices

- Is the harbor scenario the published, branded first-playable world, or merely our experimental fixture? Proposal: fixture first; select original release content separately.
- How much authoring freedom does "Create your own story" guarantee at release? Existing UI route is source-observed, but reliable unlimited freeform play is not proven. Preserve the existing route while clarifying its maturity; defer removal or changed product promise.
- Are relationship records automatically required after every significant social exchange? Proposal: **no**; prove specific supported cue types first. The model may omit a proposal, but that is a measurable failure when later correctness needs it.
