# E1 | Product nucleus and knowledge architecture

**Decision draft, not canon.** Owner direction is recorded separately from designer recommendations. Current accepted contracts remain in their existing owners until a selected change is ratified and promoted.

## 1. Product direction

Owner direction, 2026-11-28: the game is a Storyteller agent with a Markdown writing workbench and Library, assisted and constrained by real D&D-style mechanics. The Storyteller handles creative interpretation and world development; application code performs mechanical calculations and protected state changes. Inventory exchange must be a structured proposal executed by game authority, not agent edits to two inventory documents. The resulting state should be represented coherently in canonical Markdown. D&D mechanics must fit the actual fictional world and protagonist; an intergalactic blob need not have HP or Agility. Worlds may span arbitrary locations, scale, realms and time, including several fictional timelines. No universal 2D grid. The owner wants to revisit ideas before removing or rewriting them, not discard all existing thought indiscriminately.

Recommended synthesis: Offscreen RPG is a persistent, D&D-inspired roleplaying story. The player inhabits a perspective and makes consequential decisions; a Storyteller investigates, invents, writes and revises from durable knowledge; supported mechanics settle exact consequences under game authority; an authoritative publication makes the next story state inspectable and recoverable. No universal medieval-life, humanoid, world map, physics or economic simulation is required. A rich story may use these as relevant content or optional capabilities.

## 4. Decision method and page architecture

Choose the hybrid posture over (a) preserving every previous semantic choice and heading, which cannot address the owner's concern, or (b) deleting all documentation and re-deriving truth from code, which would lose rights and mistake disposable POCs for intended behavior. Write from the newly accepted target while retaining important guarantees, source provenance and explicit unknowns.

For each affected page, the planner first reads its unique claims, incoming/outgoing dependencies, related selected decisions and relevant code/tests if realization is discussed. Map the meaningful units to their exact destination and decision: retain, rewrite without meaning change, explicitly replace selected meaning, merge/move, defer a useful idea, or retire with rationale. Do not require a row for every sentence; use claim-sized units where loss or meaning changes would matter. Deletion of abandoned material is preferred over a permanent disclaimer or archive copy where no unique evidence or binding guarantee needs preservation. Retain licensed content provenance, security/financial constraints, historical evidence and necessary recovery links; use Git for ordinary historical prose. The owner/planner settles contested decisions, not a rewriting agent.

Start E1 with the product index, foundations (`vision`, `concepts`) and authority-bearing sections of `gameplay/rules`, `gameplay/continuity`, `storyteller/behavior-and-quality`, plus immediate technical crosslinks only where needed to avoid contradiction. This is a candidate write set; freeze exact headings and ownership after the claim map. Later page organization should answer reader questions, not mirror runtime packages or historical initiatives. Product describes intended experience; technical describes owned contracts; source maps describe real code; STATUS/evidence show demonstrated maturity; runbooks describe operations; decisions/research preserve useful rationale. Avoid duplicated authority, encyclopedic front doors, old diaries, and mandatory template paragraphs.

The planner supplies actual canonical decisions, claim disposition, write set, source anchors and acceptance examples before commission. Cursor implements bounded full-section/full-page rewrites with editorial freedom, not architectural freedom. Independent owners can proceed in parallel after vocabulary and shared claims freeze; one integration custodian serializes shared index/link edits. Use standard Design and Change lifecycle, not special token-burning rules. Preserve active Durable Execution, Observability E2, Agent Core, security, platform and data contracts; technical changes in a later wave reconcile against the then-current source and in-flight write sets.

## 5. Counterexamples that must survive the synthesis

- A disembodied consciousness explores several realms: no mandatory HP, inventory, coordinates or physical body; meaningful established facts still constrain later scenes.
- A human attempts a risky climb: writer judges narrative situation and proposes a supported check; game computes from admitted inputs, stores the draw and outcome, refuses an unavailable ability, and retries do not reroll.
- A sword is purchased: a proposed exchange must validate ownership, currency/resources, rule applicability and consent; both sides settle together or neither does; duplicate request cannot charge twice. Canonical Markdown reflects admitted truth, not independent model edits. This is a target scenario, not a claim of current generalized trade support.
- A journey crosses a portal or branches across fictional eras: no global coordinate system required; do not confuse in-fiction time travel with a player-created historical fork. Preserve meaningful origin/destination, interrupted state, knowledge and causal constraints where applicable; do not design every time-travel paradox in E1.
- The writer drafts a false rumor or future event: staged or attributed text is not an authoritative fact merely because it exists in the workbench. Publication must not expose hidden knowledge or make a planned event historical prematurely.
- The player forks scene 10 after reaching scene 30: the branch inherits only the exact admitted historical state and relevant versioned material at scene 10, never later possession, private developments or current model settings by accident.
