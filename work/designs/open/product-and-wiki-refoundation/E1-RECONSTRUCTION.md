## Discriminating acceptance specimens

A reviewer reads the rewritten seven-page set and locates one controlling claim for each question. These are semantic tests of documentation, not requests to run the game or promise its implementation.

| Question / scenario | Required answer | Reject if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An original narrative with no body, dice or precise clock | Legitimate intended experience, preserves agency/history/visibility | Compulsory sheet, clock or fantasy schema appears by implication |
| D&D immediate play vs two-day 1:1 journey | Optional rules and time are independent; clock-enabled settlement preserves outcomes; uninterrupted 1:1 travel waits two days | A pace change rerolls, grants arrival early, or gives blanket autonomy |
| Companion-like life after the journey completes | Further life requires an admitted commitment or future explicitly selected standing policy | Closing browser silently authorizes choice, time or spend |
| Merchant exchange interrupted or repeated | Protected settlement is atomic/idempotent; no direct inventory edits or duplicate money | Readable Markdown becomes a second writer or narration settles transfer |
| Nonmetric realm / past era / fork from old scene | No mandatory grid or linear fictional universe; publication and branch history distinct; exact historical state | A historical read replays mechanics, a branch imports later facts, or grid is mandatory |
| Writer remembers a rumor or abandoned arrival | Attributed claim and private possibility differ from committed fact; bounded source retrieval | Summary or draft becomes truth merely by existing |
| New player starts with one prompt | Preserve prepared and freeform creation, no mandatory wizard, premise is intent | UI POC described as product prerequisite or arbitrary worlds claimed proven |
| Player returns after a quiet interval or failed generation | Accepted work may settle once; no mandatory model call; known hold/default rules and current truth | Forced drama, invented progress, duplicated charge, missed decision due to explicit pause |
| Engineer asks if full D&D or broad autonomy is implemented | Reach exact current STATUS/technical owner without reading a historical design | Product aspiration treated as current implementation |
