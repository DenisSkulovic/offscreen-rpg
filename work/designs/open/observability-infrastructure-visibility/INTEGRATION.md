## Artifact routing and exact history contract

One canonical artifact resolver has four named immutable bundles. The historical `loadObservabilityVariants(dev)` remains `{desired: dev-e1-developer, predecessor: dev-e1-base}`; release returns only `release-e1`. `loadArtifacts` must take an explicit variant at internal call sites (no accidental default in a mutator or proof); public render/lint without an evolution flag targets the approved `dev-e2` or unchanged `release-e1`. A separate normal operational selector returns the admissible set and desired bundle, not an alias for historical `desired`. Use one bounded observation of the union of known exact resource identities, including E2 RBAC collision reads even when the namespace is absent; compare against each applicable exact bundle/history row. Do not delete, ignore or union-accept unexpected objects. Selection requires exactly one matching artifact/history, stable cluster/namespace identity and appropriate pending/custody state. Distinguish an installed artifact that is structurally ready from a desired-artifact convergence result. Carry the selected artifact ID, immutable descriptor and history row through forwarding, probes and owned verification; do not resolve a new default mid-operation. An unrecognized, ambiguous, foreign, unreadable or pending installation is not publicly admitted.
