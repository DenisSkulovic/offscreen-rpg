# Consent and resolution matrix

**Status: Proposed. Not selected.** This page is an open design beside the [shared-play index](README.md). It is not a product rule, not a technical contract, and not permission to implement. Owner ratification is still required. Shared play stays unratified. One vote per person stays unratified. An automatic timeout stays unratified. [GM07](../../../changes/archive/2026-11/experience/2026-11-13--gm-delegated-decision/CHANGE.md) is archived for one harmless invitation and is not selected from here.

**Reader question:** When two or more people might share one story, who may commit which action, and what happens if consent is stale, late, or withdrawn?

**Unique owner of that question:** this matrix. The directory stays the proposed home. The longer unsettled study is [shared-story collaboration](shared-story-collaboration.md). Its lockstep research, per-character proposal, host pause, and absence options are not adopted here. A canonical page may point here. It must not copy an Unratified cell into a selected rule.

A cell is Unratified when no owner decision exists. A limit quoted from a selected page stays that page's limit. Listing it here does not turn a solo rule into a group rule.

## Selected promises this page does not rewrite

These promises stay on their own pages. The small-party ambition stays an ambition.

- No accepted execution means no fictional time. An unanswered decision stays open until the player returns. [First playable scope](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/time-and-autonomy.md#first-playable-scope).
- Leaving the page does not authorize a new choice. A general delegated choice is not accepted. [What this page selects](../../../../docs/product/foundations/vision.md#what-this-page-selects).
- A goblin deadline is illustration. The near-term playthrough still routes to the [long-life atlas](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/acceptance-scenarios.md#long-life-benchmark-playthroughs). [Long-life benchmark playthroughs](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/long-life-benchmark-playthroughs.md).
- A solo story can grant pause to its player. Group pause authority is the open choice above.
- The pinned payer of an in-flight charge stays that payer.

## Refused readings

A fictional group scene must not become selected policy. The pass, the goblin, the apple, and the companion rescue stay illustration inside the small-party ambition.

An unanswered decision stays open. This page does not grant Player A the choice of Player B's action. It does not let one device commit both incompatible outcomes. It does not treat absence as general delegated combat choice. The group mechanism that would enforce those withholdings is Unratified.
