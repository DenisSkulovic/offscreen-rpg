# Shared play | open design

**Status: proposed. Not accepted product mechanics.**

Shared campaigns are part of the long-term vision, and Admin reasoning must not assume one account is one story owner. The method of collecting conflicting intentions, invitations, readiness, who may start, and pause rules during another player's turn are **not chosen**.

Do not implement shared decision resolution from this note. A canonical page may point here. It must not paraphrase these gaps as accepted rules.

Related stress tests that stay proposed: [Player 360](../../../../docs/product/admin/player-360-and-support.md), [LiveOps shared-world compatibility](../../../../docs/product/admin/gameplay-and-liveops.md).
