# Decision | golden campaign uses a quiescent fork point; active mechanics need v2

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Basis:** current `main` at `32d9b6a498ad` and BOOT03 Cursor receipt `b68f0b40` (13/13 imported-start tests). **Authority:** first distributed POC fixture/acceptance scope, not a change to the selected long-term any-scene product promise. **Execution state:** PLANNED fixture repair, NOT IMPLEMENTED/NOT RUN here.

## Answer to the BOOT03 question

**NO:** the first golden TURN03 campaign is **not required to fork a mechanical `microbe.v3` opening or a pending executable world obligation.** Its required historical fork must start from an explicitly **quiescent scene-1 checkpoint**: non-mechanical Storyteller-kind scripted opening, pinned source/rules, an offered narrative choice, no accepted activity, no hold/active offer, and **no instantiated `world_obligation` rows**. Complete scene 2 by the real distributed Storyteller path, then historically read/fork **scene 1** without using current/live tables. This satisfies the already selected *POC subset* in [technical lifecycle](../../../../docs/technical/engine/lifecycle-and-compatibility.md#forked-continuities) and [data-and-persistence](../../../../docs/technical/platform/data-and-persistence.md#story-snapshot-and-chronology).

**YES, later:** the selected **product end-state** must permit historical fork from *any committed scene*, including active mechanics, offers, holds, clocks, obligations and resources. Checkpoint v1 deliberately does not model their safe historical identity or replay. A future separate checkpoint-v2 design/Change must inventory all relevant authoritative SQL state, capture it at a publication fence, preserve unresolved work as unresolved, remap every story-scoped ID, rebase wall-clock anchors without inheriting the parent's timer, and prove no duplicate effects/payments or parent-future leaks. Do not falsely label this as supported, silently promote v1, or load latest mutable rows at fork time. **Do not make this redesign a BOOT03 dependency or spontaneously create/select a new Change.**

## Source-discovered fixture incompatibility: fix this *before* TURN03 fork proof

Current `tools/api-integration/test/helpers/turn03-distributed-poc-fixture.ts:provisionTurn03DistributedPocFixture` writes `obligations/harbor-watch.json` and lists it in `start-package.json` as `activation: executable-obligation`, `schemaId: world-obligation.v1`. `tools/api-integration/test/turn03-distributed-poc-fixture.integration.ts:campaignForPins` and `story03-causal-multiprocess.integration.ts:campaignForPins` specify `mechanics: false` and `worldObligations: []`, **but that only means no explicit request-level obligations**.

`packages/application/src/stories/start.ts:createStoryStart` reads every `executable-obligation` entry from the pinned start manifest, remaps its ID, and combines it into `campaignOptions.worldObligations`. A non-mechanical opening stages a v1 campaign checkpoint only when that combined list is empty. Therefore **the existing golden Harbor-watch fixture does not guarantee a scene-1 selector**. Story sequence `[1,2]` and a green T09 HTTP test are valuable, but are **not a historical-fork PASS**.
