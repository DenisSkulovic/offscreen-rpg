# Gameplay domain coverage | unratified decision catalogue

**Status: Proposed. Not selected.** This page is an open design. It is not a product rule, not a technical contract, and not permission to implement.

**Reader question:** For economy, progression and fate, spatiality, social relations, collaboration, and content policy, what is already selected, what belongs to one world or ruleset, what is only an illustration, what is still unratified, and what is intentionally absent?

**Unique owner of that question:** this catalogue. Selected promises stay on the headings named below. Decision questions stay here. No new page under `docs/product/` is created.

A wage example stays an example. A character with no applicable Strength is a valid form. Lack of a Strength stat for a microbe is not an engineering bug.

## How to read a row

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Selected | The named heading already governs this limit. |
| World or ruleset | The named heading allows it only when that world or rules sheet admits it. |
| Illustration only | The named heading uses a picture. The picture does not bind every world. |
| Unratified | The named heading leaves the choice open, or points at an existing proposal. |
| Intentionally absent | The named heading withholds a universal mechanic on purpose. |

Each row names one existing heading and one nonhuman or world-neutral counterexample. Conditional packets DOC32–DOC36 stay unselected. This catalogue does not start them.

## Economy

**Reader question:** Which exchanges must the product remember, and which livelihood pictures stay inside one world?

**Normative status:** Selected possession continuity and the refusal of a mandatory currency. Wages and coins on [Earned life](../../../../docs/product/foundations/vision.md#earned-life) are illustrations. Any further livelihood vocabulary is unratified.

**Owner:** [Possessions and exchanges](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md#possessions-and-exchanges) and [World-independent foundation](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/rules.md#world-independent-foundation). The open question lives in this catalogue.

**Normal:** A campaign whose rules admit a counted resource spends three and keeps two. **Negative:** The vision's wage picture becomes a universal currency table, or a microorganism is assigned an employer so the table has a row.

**Open question, unanswered:** Whether any resource name should be shared across worlds beyond an admitted ruleset quantity. [DOC32](https://github.com/DenisSkulovic/offscreen-rpg/blob/34e2b4fd8d9d9967e13311da3a938460b1a02244/work/changes/archive/2026-11/documentation/2026-11-18--doc32-livelihood-and-economy-scope/CHANGE.md) is a conditional later packet. It is not selected by this page.

## Progression and fate

**Reader question:** What lasting change is already required, and which advancement, injury, fate, and combat systems are withheld?

**Normative status:** Selected: earned completion, campaign-permitted loss, form-scoped abilities, and lasting constraints that the story has established. World or ruleset: the POC ability and hit-point subset. Illustration: combat named beside work and travel. Intentionally absent: level progression, a disease track, and universal combat. Unratified: the control that chooses a finite adventure or a continuing life.

**Owner:** [World-independent foundation](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/rules.md#world-independent-foundation), [Foundation](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/rules.md#foundation), and [People, objects and capabilities](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md#people-objects-and-capabilities). The open question lives here.

**Normal:** A form that declares Strength can be checked with Strength. A form that does not declare it cannot invoke it. **Negative:** A missing Strength score for a microbe is filed as an engine defect, or inapplicability is treated as an automatic success.

Inspected source at `b3113814`, read-only: `packages/game/src/checks.ts` rejects a check whose ability is outside `applicableAbilities`. `packages/game/src/state.ts` records that absent health is not HP 0. That source agrees with the selected ability rule. It does not add a level ladder or a disease table.

**Open question, unanswered:** Whether a lasting bodily change should become a typed condition beyond the continuity constraint. [DOC33](https://github.com/DenisSkulovic/offscreen-rpg/blob/34e2b4fd8d9d9967e13311da3a938460b1a02244/work/changes/archive/2026-11/documentation/2026-11-18--doc33-character-lifecourse-policy/CHANGE.md) is conditional and unselected.

## Spatiality

**Reader question:** Which location facts must stay consistent, and which map geometry is optional?

**Normative status:** Selected: authoritative whereabouts, scene frames, and world-neutral connections. Illustration: the cube-map candidate. World or ruleset: cells, sectors, and scale. Intentionally absent: a universal movement formula and mandatory coordinates. Unratified: the representation of space.

**Owner:** [Space, movement and maps](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md#space-movement-and-maps). [Travel duration and progress](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/time-and-autonomy.md#travel-duration-and-progress) keeps the movement rule undesigned. The open question lives here.

**Normal:** An interrupted journey remains between departure and arrival. **Negative:** Every actor receives map coordinates, or a diagram's cell size becomes a distance.

**Open question, unanswered:** What smallest representation can show an interrupted journey without a universal map. [DOC34](https://github.com/DenisSkulovic/offscreen-rpg/blob/34e2b4fd8d9d9967e13311da3a938460b1a02244/work/changes/archive/2026-11/documentation/2026-11-18--doc34-space-travel-and-location/CHANGE.md) is conditional and unselected. This catalogue does not create `work/designs/open/spatial-play/`.

## Social relations

**Reader question:** When does another being become durable, and what must not be stored merely because a relationship exists?

**Normative status:** Selected identity and relationship facts. Illustration: the familiar NPC. Intentionally absent: a population simulation. Unratified: any numeric trust model.

**Owner:** [Six kinds of record](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md#six-kinds-of-record) and [People, objects and capabilities](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md#people-objects-and-capabilities). The open question lives here.

**Normal:** The player engages one organism. That organism becomes a canonical actor. **Negative:** The engagement writes a relationship meter, or every neighbor in a field becomes an actor.

**Open question, unanswered:** Whether "trust" is anything beyond continuity facts such as a debt or a repeated encounter. No meter is proposed as a rule.

## Collaboration

**Reader question:** What shared-play promise is already an ambition, and which consent rules remain unchosen?

**Normative status:** The small-group ambition is selected as an ambition. Live acceptance is Proposed/Blocked. Solo pause belongs to the player of a solo story. Group pause, conflicting intentions, and shared funding are unratified.

**Owner:** [First playable target](../../../../docs/product/foundations/vision.md#first-playable-target), [Shared play (product target, unsettled mechanics)](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/worlds-and-characters.md#shared-play-product-target-unsettled-mechanics), and the deferred proposal [shared play](../../backlog/shared-play/README.md). This catalogue does not open a second collaboration design.

**Normal:** A solo player can pause that solo story. **Negative:** A player-count setting is treated as a consent, pause, or funding rule.

**Open question, unanswered:** Those questions stay in the shared-play design. [DOC35](https://github.com/DenisSkulovic/offscreen-rpg/blob/34e2b4fd8d9d9967e13311da3a938460b1a02244/work/changes/archive/2026-11/documentation/2026-11-18--doc35-shared-play-consent-matrix/CHANGE.md) is conditional and unselected.

## Content policy

**Reader question:** Which fiction limits are already selected, and which safety or rating rules are still only drafts?

**Normative status:** Selected: franchise memory is not canon, and unusual bodies are valid protagonists. The staff trust page is Proposed. A player-facing content rating, ban list, or age rule is intentionally absent from the selected gameplay pages and remains unratified.

**Owner:** [What it must not do](../../../../docs/product/storyteller/behavior-and-quality.md#what-it-must-not-do) for canon. [Commercial operations and trust](../../../../docs/product/admin/commercial-and-trust.md) for the proposed staff draft, still awaiting owner and legal review. The open gameplay question lives here.

**Normal:** An abstract or tragic nonhuman story is playable under the selected world-neutral rules. **Negative:** This catalogue is read as a content code, or the proposed staff page is quoted as an accepted gameplay ban.

**Open question, unanswered:** Who would ratify a player-facing fiction limit, and which existing owner would receive it after ratification. It is not a content code, and it is not selected here.

## No product page created

A new canonical product owner is unjustified. Each player-facing promise already has a heading, and each missing universal mechanic is an open choice or an intentional absence.

| Proposed split | Why no product page was created |
| --- | --- |
| Economy / livelihood | Possession continuity and the selected absence of a mandatory currency already have owners. A new page would become the universal wage table this catalogue leaves unselected. |
| Progression, injury, fate, combat | Applicability, separate combat scope, and lasting constraints already have owners. A new page would ratify levels, disease, or universal combat. |
| Spatiality | [Space, movement and maps](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/continuity.md#space-movement-and-maps) already owns the selected limit and records that the movement rule is undesigned. |
| Social relations | Identity and relationship facts already live under continuity. A new page would invite a relationship meter. |
| Collaboration | Vision, worlds and characters, and [shared play](../../backlog/shared-play/README.md) already separate the ambition from unsettled mechanics. |
| Content policy | Ratifying a rating or ban would be a new policy. The staff draft stays Proposed on its own page. |

## Claims left unselected

This catalogue does not select global employment, a money table, disease, map coordinates, relationship meters, experience leveling, or universal combat. It does not select shared-decision, group-pause, or shared-funding rules. It does not select a content rating. It does not change the world-neutral ability rule on [World-independent foundation](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/rules.md#world-independent-foundation) or [Flexible settings and protagonists](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/worlds-and-characters.md#flexible-settings-and-protagonists).

## Retained routine and delegated-response questions (2026-11-27)

The historical convergence experiments GM03 and GM07 have narrow Done receipts in the [Change ledger](../../../changes/ledger/2026-11.md); neither selects general standing life nor universal timed decisions. When revisiting autonomy, compare existing accepted-plan machinery before inventing a repeated-activity engine. Preserve bounded authorization, per-iteration identity, stop at a new required decision/hold, no repeatable reward without admitted earned effects. For optional timed decisions, investigate per-published-decision explicit consent, exact allowed option, versioned revocation, manual response versus expiry under the same authoritative transaction, and invalid/stale-option hold. No fictional time just for waiting for consent. Historical three 600-fictional-second waits and 20-real-minute invitation deadline are fixture parameters, not general defaults. [Time and autonomy](../../../../docs/product/gameplay/time-and-autonomy.md) owns selected meaning. [Research provenance](../../../../research/agent-practice/BACKLOG-CONVERGENCE-2026-11-18.md) preserves original evidence. No new implementation authorization.


## Retained product-coherence questions

[November product audits](../../../../research/product-reality/README.md) compare world-neutral forms versus narrow authored rules, meaningful player agency versus bounded choices, accepted unattended progression versus autonomy, and spatial representation versus actual movement authority. Compare the smallest supported representation against a discriminating failure case; avoid making all worlds share one ontology or first-solo proof depend on group mechanics. Preserve accepted consent and state/time authority. No historical candidate map, humanoid fixture or experimental threshold is ratified here.


[Retained playability experiments](../../../../research/product-reality/PLAYABILITY-EXPERIMENTS-2026-11-18.md) ask whether bounded choices provide meaningful agency and accepted offscreen work adds value rather than a waiting tax. Record missing reasonable intentions, actual consequences, return quality and attributable cost; compare alternatives before adding free-intent or autonomy mechanisms. No historical duration, rubric or fixture is selected as a universal rule.
