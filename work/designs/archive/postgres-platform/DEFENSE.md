## Revised target and staging
Retain Helm renderer and pins. Shared Cluster operations for install/resize/experiments, not generic workflow DSL. Strongest existing topology and Temporal access mechanisms become reference implementations; preserve existing public interfaces and identities. No blanket rewrite of Temporal or tenant lifecycle. Explicit operation-level concurrency design required before commissioning structural slice, including lock ownership and order, fresh re-observation and unknown mutation outcomes. Keep baseline ACL migration and role cutover separate, not necessary for structural slice; do not change existing data or credentials to make new design look neat. See revised SPECIMENS.md.
