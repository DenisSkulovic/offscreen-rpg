## What would justify Grok?

Not “SQL”, “distributed”, “resilience”, “I want a second opinion”, or “we haven't inspected the source.” Only an **actual, source-documented failure** where ChatGPT has already reduced the issue to a small bounded decision, there is no safe deterministic proof or fully specified implementation with Composer, and the owner **explicitly authorizes** that individual Grok task. The parent must first ask whether another ChatGPT investigation would remove the unknown. Most such failures should return to this planner, not spawn another paid investigator.
