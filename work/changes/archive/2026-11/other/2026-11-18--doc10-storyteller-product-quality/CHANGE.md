## Exact intended editorial delta

1. Preserve the adopted falsifiability matrix while adding a small multi-day/multi-scene evaluation family with exact source-backed continuity, quiet work and player redirection.
2. Separate creative temperament, limited initiative, private possibilities, canon invention and funding or tool permissions. Link their governing technical boundaries without turning future controller design into a live feature.
3. Mark each illustrative evaluation scenario as proposed acceptance where no user approval exists. Never invent a percentage or universal drama frequency.

No bulk formatting, no blanket shortening, no new product policy, no executable edits, no paid provider calls, no new data migration. Move or split only when one canonical responsibility and all inbound anchors can be preserved. Mark a genuinely good subsection reviewed unchanged instead of forcing edits.

## Success and discriminating negative

**Normal:** a reader can locate the selected claim, its governing rationale, its precise status and its negative branch with a bounded route through these owners. **Negative:** An agent forces a dramatic incident every quiet day, treats a discarded narrative plan as committed truth, or charges a provider for every idle minute.

**Escalation:** New mandatory Storyteller acceptance thresholds or new permission to write canon are product decisions; keep Proposed until ratified. Keep unresolved normative subsection Proposed/Blocked, while permitting only meaning-preserving edits elsewhere if reviewable independently.

**Proof required:** before/after claim inventory and unchanged-contract list, exact affected path/heading/links, real source evidence for any status correction, negative scenario result, `pnpm wiki:health`, `pnpm wiki:change-guard --mode plan` and `--mode close` when available, plus actual link checker. Mark Done only with an independent adverse-reading review and no unresolved material contradiction. Do not claim semantic correctness from a green structural checker.
