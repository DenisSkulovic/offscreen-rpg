# GM07 | Decision: one durable, consented withdrawal from a published campaign offer

**Decision date:** 2026-11-20. **Inspected:** attached source tree \`64f3de7\`, connected GitHub main through \`c93cbc12\`, exact implementation anchors below. **Status:** source-backed planner decision for bounded implementation, NOT code, migration, active production feature, or live SQL/Temporal acceptance. **Current work:** TURN02 is selected in \`work/QUEUE.md\`; GM07 is **unselected**.

## Judgment: the original blocker was real; what solves it

There are already **three different things** which must not be conflated:

1. The *campaign's actual action command*: \`packages/application/src/campaign/actions.ts:createCampaignActions\` receives \`actionCommandSchema\` with \`expectedRevision,offerId,path\`, locks the **story** with \`lockOwnedStory\`, validates \`campaign.offer\`, \`situationAuthorization.offerId\`, \`game_offer\` plan, eligibility and holds, consumes the exact offer, creates an action/execution and an idempotent command under SQL. \`game_action_receipt\` and \`game_action_execution\` each enforce uniqueness \`(story_id,offer_id)\`.
2. The old *narrative* timer: \`packages/application/src/stories/plans.ts:decisionPlanSchema\`, \`story_passage.decision_plan/response_due_at\`, \`timing.ts:resolveDecision\`, and \`story.decision.v1\` Temporal workflow. Its default can be applied without **GM07 consent** and \`story_passage.response_source='default'\` does not encode preapproved withdrawal. It writes a continuation, **not** a campaign action. This mechanism must **not** be reused as GM07 permission or expanded into the game’s mechanical authority.
3. GM07's \`campaign/delegated-decision.ts:admitDelegatedDecision\` is a **pure, memory-only six-test shape** for two choices, grant and revocation, 20-minute deadline and one settlement. It has **no DB, authenticated grant, captured source offer, Temporal wake, action transaction, pause, or durable history**. The next caller must obtain facts from SQL; pure tests alone cannot close this Change.

**Chosen implementation:** a **separate, opt-in campaign-offer delegation** with one explicit first-party withdrawal eligibility, story-level versioned owner consent, an immutable per-offer publication capture, a separate versioned deadline notice and a delegated action admitted through the **existing campaign action transaction**. Other offers remain indefinite. No broad AI delegation, narrative script-default expansion, or provider calls.

## Ratified first-slice product and safety contract

- **Actor:** current authenticated **solo story owner** only. Enable via owner story setting *before* a future offer is published. Policy v1 = \`decline-harmless-invitation.v1\` (fixed 20-minute real response window); disabled/absent is the default. No shared-world choice delegation or inferred personality toggles.
- **Offer:** two **actually playable terminal actions** in one \`gameOffer\`, including one explicit, domain-admitted fixed \`withdraw\` action in a known first-party rights-cleared fixture. Do **not** infer a safe withdrawal from free-text label, risk blurb or model prose. There is no \`withdrawal\` kind in current \`immediateActionPlanSchema\`; that exact missing typed eligibility must be added or derived from a source-authorized immutable fixture manifest with explicit action key + hash. The application validates the candidate's admissibility from current plan, actor, facts, holds and effects at publication and **again** at expiry. For first slice limit fallback to non-combat, no-provider, finite automatic resolution with no transfer, important resource debit, permanent social/relationship commitment, or ambiguous consequences. If the exact fixture has no safely admissible withdrawal, **do not arm a timer**.
- **Publication anchor:** publication is the SQL transaction that commits the offered \`game_offer\` and \`campaign.offer\` and transfers the Storyteller hold to a decision hold (\`storyteller/publication.ts\`). Record the offer ID, narrative revision, exact fallback **path/actionKey**, captured policy version, owner, exact trusted source fingerprint, DB publication timestamp and dueAt (+20 real minutes) in that **same** transaction. No clock before committed publication, and no retroactive window for an already offered decision when a story setting changes later.
- **Manual choice:** continues through the ordinary \`campaignAction\` path and can win the same story lock. Expiry must not circumvent \`actionCommandSchema\` or duplicate mechanics. A chosen action is not itself a completed resolution. Ordinary settlement still applies duration, dice (only if admissible), effects, history, and narration fences.
- **No consent/expired grant:** never select fallback. Leave the currently offered choice and campaign decision hold intact. A failed worker/Temporal delivery does not reauthorize choice, create a new offer or call a model.
- **Consent edit/revoke:** under the story lock, version increments. Revocation invalidates pending captures (no retroactive undo of already committed actions), independently of story revision. Changing an unrelated policy cannot silently relabel an old grant as approved.
- **Pause:** GM07's explicit owner *decision-window pause* is the first-slice manual pause control, not a claim of general game pause. Under the story lock before expiry, save \`remainingMs\`, put the captured decision in \`paused\`, invalidate the old wake **epoch**, and keep fictional time held. Resume only by owner, preserving exactly the saved remaining real milliseconds (never reset to 20 minutes), incrementing wake epoch and atomically enqueuing a new notice. Expiry after a pause must hold; a pause arriving after the DB deadline is not guaranteed to overtake an already eligible timeout.
- **Clock:** all deadlines, pause cutoffs, revocations and timeout admission use \`readDatabaseClockMs\` while holding the story row, never browser time, wall-time of a provider or fictional clock. Waiting earns **zero** fictional seconds. Action's own accepted duration is still charged by its ordinary engine path.
- **Provenance:** a successful delegated **admission** records an immutable \`preapproved-withdrawal\` source with offer ID, policy version, selected action key, operation ID and captured event timestamp. Project one player-readable factual event (not as a personal click), with source identity tied to that one command; no raw hidden offer, secret policy, or future result. Distinguish action *initiated* from action *settled* and don't fabricate the latter at admission.
- **No global default:** the existing \`decisionPlanSchema\` v1 narrative default is a separate isolated capability. GM07 never arms \`story_passage.responseDueAt\` or calls \`resolveDecision\` with a fake consent. Reject a proposed GM07 plan that only supplies that old \`defaultOptionId\`.

## Persistence and concurrency: one minimal capability, not a second engine

**A. \`story_delegation_policy\`** (new narrow DB table, owner story key): \`storyId PK/FK\`, \`ownerId\` (or owner verified through locked story), \`version\` positive, \`allowedClass\` literal first-slice class, \`enabled\` boolean, \`updatedAt\`; explicitly validated owner-intent operations and stored policy receipt/idempotency. Owner control uses the same story lock for all writes; never infer granted policy from Storyteller profile. For disabled/absent record, no future captured timer. Non-owner denied without a policy leak. Dedicated grant-revoked source event only if the public design requires it, otherwise keep audit in private policy/receipt.

**B. \`campaign_delegated_offer\`** (new table, \`offerId\` PK/FK to \`game_offer\`, \`storyId\`, \`narrativeRevision\`, \`ownerId\`, \`fallbackActionKey\`, captured *exact path*, \`sourceFingerprint\`, \`capturedPolicyVersion\`, \`publishedAt\`, \`dueAt\`, \`status\` = \`armed|paused|revoked|held|admitted|consumed\`, \`controlRevision\` nonnegative, \`remainingMs\` nullable, \`wakeEpoch\`, stable \`actionOperationId\`, \`selectedAt\` nullable, \`selectionSource\` nullable. Constrain eligible statuses/dueAt/remainingMs pairing, unique action ID and one record per offer. Database migration must be forward-only/reversible for clean disposable DB; no destructive backfill. **Do not duplicate authoritative game offer/facts/actor into JSON**; source fingerprint binds immutable plan and selected path, then actual current offer/plan is rechecked under the same lock.

**C. \`campaign.delegated-decision.v1\`** (new outbox/Temporal notice family distinct from \`story.decision.v1\`). Enqueue on captured publication/resume in the exact SQL transaction. Workflow ID includes \`offerId\` **and wakeEpoch**, so a paused/old workflow cannot accidentally eat the next resume notice. Activity sleeps only until dueAt and calls a worker-only operation that re-locks story and compares wake epoch, policy version, offer ID, narrative revision, current source fingerprint, decision hold, paused/other hold states, eligible exact fallback, and DB time. If not yet due, return remaining milliseconds. If revoked, stale, paused, no longer safe or missing, mark appropriate held state and **do not make any action**. Duplicate deliveries always see prior command or no-op.

**D. One action writer.** Today \`createCampaignActions(...).act\` takes its **own** SQL transaction. Do **not** call it from inside another locked SQL transaction and assume an atomic consent fence. Extract a **single** narrow \`admitCampaignActionInTransaction(tx, lockedStory, validatedRequest, trustedSource)\` from the existing implementation. The public player adapter and worker GM07 expiry both call this shared inner admission under one story lock. GM07's trusted source is derived from persisted delegation and the authenticated owner in the locked story, never supplied by an external request body. Preserve all existing offer, plan, command, activity, receipt, authority and world-obligation guards; an expiry does **not** bypass risk/effects/Storyteller custody. If code structure cannot admit extraction without a broad domain refactor, STOP with the exact source obstruction and return for planner re-slicing, not a second action implementation.

**E. Version/lock order.** Every owner grant/revoke/pause/resume, ordinary player action and worker expiry acquires \`lockOwnedStory\`/\`lockStoryById\` **before** dependent policy/campaign/offer reads. The first transaction committing an accepted action consumes the single current offer; the loser finds stale source and does no work. Stable per-offer \`actionOperationId\` plus existing \`game_action_execution_offer\`, \`game_action_receipt_offer\` and command uniqueness make retries converge. No need to invent a second roll. An ordinary player click at/after deadline can still win if it commits first and the GM07 fallback has not committed; document this same-lock first-commit policy rather than an imaginary client-time priority.

## Concrete acceptance, not green-by-configuration

1. **Pure:** preserve/refine \`delegated-decision.test.mjs\` for refusal decisions. Include absence of consent, wrong class, post-deadline grant, stale policy/offer, grant revocation, actual DB-time early expiry, extra/hidden choices, combat/model candidate, pause and replay.
2. **Disposable SQL:** publish actual source offer with grant, exactly one fallback row and outbox; no grant -> zero delegate rows/no wake, no fictional time spent. Player-before-worker and worker-before-player run as **concurrent separate SQL connections** with one exact winning action/offer, one command/event/receipt or a single in-progress execution, no duplicate effects. Check stale revocation, changed offer, changed plan/source fingerprint, other holds, paused/resumed remaining time and provider-disabled path. Rollback a forced failure and verify **zero partial state**.
3. **Worker:** replay/double delivery and old epoch after pause/resume are no-ops. Worker restart resumes a due event; no browser tab, provider call, manual script or extra game clock tick required.
4. **Public projection:** show *approved fallback armed/paused/held/selected* only for owner and exact currently published decision, hide unsupported/secret options. On admitted action show provenance; upon actual mechanical settlement show actual factual outcome. External nonowner cannot set policy, pause, revoke, or select another player's offer.
5. **Code ownership:** background workers must invoke the legitimate application command/GA writer for the deployed architecture. Do not add an unreviewed second Nest-side gameplay writer while TURN02/GA transition is ongoing. Implement only after current TURN02/BOOT03 shared app writer files are released.
6. **Closure:** run relevant unit, disposable SQL, workflow and operator integration tests with exact counts; update \`docs/product/gameplay/time-and-autonomy.md\` and \`docs/technical/engine/chronology-and-activities.md\` only once actually supported, plus Change evidence ledger and wiki link guards. Then archive GM07. No live provider calls, financial grants, production rollout or clusters deleted.
