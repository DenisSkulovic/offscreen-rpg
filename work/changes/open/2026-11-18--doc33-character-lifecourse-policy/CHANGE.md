**Reader job now insufficiently supported:** The project promises earned lives, risk and potentially severe loss, while current D&D subset does not claim full advancement, health simulation, aging or permadeath. These concepts have no single scope/ownership and could be accidentally hardwired across worlds.

## Planned editorial change

1. Develop a proposed boundary map for character identity and lifecycle events: growth/skills, injury/recovery, death or irreversible loss, aging, form transitions, player return and story ending; map each to selected existing text and actual code status.
2. Use two world-neutral scenarios (humanoid adventurer and disembodied/microbial existence) to distinguish universal 'consequences remain real' from ruleset-specific HP, fatigue, aging, skill trees and disease.
3. Document unresolved authority for fatal outcomes, fairness/legibility, irreversible changes, account/story fork and audit; do not claim D&D 5e full combat is already selected/implemented.
4. If a distinct durable product promise is ratified, propose eventual docs/product/gameplay/character-life-and-progression.md with a product/technical Change, not a hidden story-quality edit.

Before editing, compare actual source tree, the selected meaning of exact headings, all incoming links and current dirty state. If the reader question is already answered at one precise stable owner, **record reviewed no-op** and improve the route instead of adding a redundant document.
