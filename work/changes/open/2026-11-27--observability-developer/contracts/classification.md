### Pure classification and custody adapter
Separate pure classifyInventory(snapshot,desired,{predecessorArtifacts}) from mutation-only verifyOwnedFinalShape(snapshot,desired,{identity,custody,predecessorArtifacts,expectedOperation,expectedRevision}). expectedOperation is required enum ensure/upgrade/persistence/outage, never inferred from record; expectedRevision is required 1 or 2 and must equal observed Collector revision (ensure=1, transition upgrade=2, disruption=actual admitted baseline revision). No ownedPending boolean or public classify options bypass. Internal helper assessShape(snapshot,artifacts,variant) returns structural state/detail/configRevision independently of pending; private to readiness.mjs. Public classify validates pending fields: only pending===false AND pendingValid===true means clear; invalid/missing pending flags refuse unreadable/pending, never absent/ready. Preserve legacy/foreign details where independently proven; complete shape plus pending -> not-ready/pending. Public no-op ready only desired + clear. Clean absent requires absent namespace, no target PV claimRefs, no legacy, no releases/objects and clear pending. Namespace present empty is not-ready. Require exact namespace UID and owner label from profile, nonterminating. Never reinterpret unknown or partial as clean absent.

Owned finalization adapter calls custody.assertOwnPending() (actual exclusive capability from T04) and compares full tuple to frozen identity and record.operation to explicit expectedOperation; require exact expectedRevision; require snapshot namespace.uid equal identity tuple target UID, pending===true/pendingValid===true; compare owned record again immediately after shape assessment. Return {ok,configRevision,detail}, not public state ready; no snapshot mutation, no pending=false clone. No shape-only success when snapshot missing. Ensure uses operation ensure and requires desired; upgrade uses operation upgrade and requires desired rev2 when transition attempted; disruption finalization uses own persistence/outage intent and requires full desired ready shape after restoration. Intentionally down observation uses the separate narrow adapter below; never call finalization with a relaxed flag. Only caller clears record after full final identity recheck, exact completed phase prefix or transition proof, and closed owned children. Re-read pending after clear before success. T04 owns actual record validator and compare methods; fixture fake capability must maintain independent on-disk-equivalent record and reject wrong operation/tuple/replacement, not unconditional true.
