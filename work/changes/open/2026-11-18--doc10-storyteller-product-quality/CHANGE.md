## Exact intended editorial delta

1. Preserve the adopted falsifiability matrix while adding a small multi-day/multi-scene evaluation family with exact source-backed continuity, quiet work and player redirection.
2. Separate creative temperament, limited initiative, private possibilities, canon invention and funding or tool permissions. Link their governing technical boundaries without turning future controller design into a live feature.
3. Mark each illustrative evaluation scenario as proposed acceptance where no user approval exists. Never invent a percentage or universal drama frequency.

No bulk formatting, no blanket shortening, no new product policy, no executable edits, no paid provider calls, no new data migration. Move or split only when one canonical responsibility and all inbound anchors can be preserved. Mark a genuinely good subsection reviewed unchanged instead of forcing edits.
