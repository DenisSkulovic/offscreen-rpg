**Budget discipline selected:** child caps are **suballocations**, not extra reservations against account/run. Under the **same `storytellerOperation` row lock** as `reserveWriterMissionProviderAttempt`, ensure sum of all outstanding child allowance + already consumed/reserved parent spend ≤ frozen mission cap. Child attempt reservation verifies charged/reserved within its own cap and shares the *existing* `storytellerOperation` and `storytellerFunding/storytellerRun` counters, with `storytellerAttempt.attribution.contextId=childId`; parent default attribution maps to `contextId=missionId`. Never count a child cap as real account spending and then charge again. Every dispatched or uncertain attempt remains reserved/consumed in parent operation; only **unused** child allocation returns when terminal. If a parent tries to spend money earmarked for an outstanding child, deny under operation lock. Include oversubscription races in DB proof. Maintain global uncertain guard and don't reprice/retry dispatched unknowns.
