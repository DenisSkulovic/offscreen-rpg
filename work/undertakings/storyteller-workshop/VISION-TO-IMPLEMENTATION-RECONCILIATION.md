## The correct picture of the product

**Not a chat app, not a D&D sheet, not simply an AI-generated opening.** The intended experience is a person, organism, community, place or stranger perspective living a consequential fiction over many human visits. Time is earned by admitted work; quiet is allowed. The Writer can investigate pinned Library evidence and craft scenes, decisions and meaningful developments with creative neutrality, independent of whether typed mechanics exist. The engine preserves one admitted world truth and protects player agency. Every publication is an inspectable, forkable historical frontier; a branch never acquires later parent facts. The Writer is a **repeatable, bounded operation**, not a permanent chat session or fixed ritual of tool calls.

**An end-to-end experience** therefore requires three cooperating ongoing loops:
1. **Player / game loop:** read present; see authored options; choose or accept time-bound work; deterministic authority commits results; reenter later. No forced action during absence, no mechanical effect from prose.
2. **Writer loop:** capture the exact story+scene+Library+profile+mechanical frontier; decide whether to research; use Library/tools/Workdesk optionally; propose next passage, choices, relevant source edits; Game Authority validates and commits as one scene boundary; repeat for meaningful continuations, not only scene 1.
3. **Historical loop:** immutable journal and evidence; fork any eligible scene with its active unresolved commitments, resume child without replaying parent work or paid attempts.

These loops explain why “launch worked” is *necessary but insufficient*. A scripted opening that cannot advance through multiple Writer-mediated player decisions is a demo, not the Storyteller refoundation.

## Five corrective architecture/product decisions

**D1. One Writer mission framework, two story-entry modes.** An initial mission has `sceneScope.kind='opening'`, reserved story/passage, no fictional past. A continuation mission has `sceneScope.kind='continuation'`, with an **exact current checkpoint/source root, selected controlling effect/intent or current situational need, narrative revision and campaign state fence**. Add a new explicit versioned continuation capture; preserve v1 and v2-opening immutable replay. The Writer's tool/workdesk/provider round control remains shared; opening/continuation are mission *inputs*, not different agent species. No new “Storyteller sequel package.”

**D2. Publication is one acceptance protocol for Writer scenes.** A proposed opening or continuation carries validated prose, choices and **deliberately selected staged-document changes**, source provenance and frozen version/pin identity. Game Authority independently validates semantics and publishes only the admitted delta plus passage, root, after-state checkpoint and applicable mechanical outcome under one transaction. A private possibility may remain private or be rejected; a draft not listed as an admitted change stays in Workdesk. Legacy narrow reports may remain specialized because reports are not current choices.

**D3. Separate Writer decision from source discovery, spending and ticking.** The controller should choose *when* a Writer mission is needed from meaningful causal boundaries. Quiet accepted routines can settle without an LLM. One Writer mission may use zero/one/many bounded tool rounds as its recorded policy permits. An uncertain paid attempt is not retried automatically; a captured source root is never replaced with mutable latest state.

**D4. Evidence-quality tests must discriminate narrative correctness.** A 200-scene corpus that only opens Library handles is not sufficient. Exercise an old exchanged item, a long-running promise, a revised relationship, a private possibility, a source correction and a divergent fork; require actual cite-backed retrieval and *observed* current-scene decisions consistent with those facts, not only store integrity. Compare short and difficult tasks for appropriate resource use; no fixed minimum tool-call count.

**D5. Preserve selected POC scope.** Solo-first, no multiplayer or mandatory free-text gameplay during play, no omniscient sim, no every-tick model calls, no per-NPC agents, no universal D&D sheet, no new event-sourced microservice topology. The engine's existing typed capabilities and time ledger remain the authority. Nothing here mandates a broad creative-director/relationship simulator before the solo Writer loop works.

## Target acceptance journeys (all required for FULL undertaking Done)

1. **Create and continue:** User starts from one word, receives a first scene, chooses an admissible meaningful option, sees a *new Writer-produced scene* with coherent fictional-time/after-state and a second meaningful choice; browser reload/reconnect and retries do not duplicate result.
2. **Ordinary life:** Accept an hours-long routine, leave and return, receive exact earned progress/interruptions without a model call per tick and no invented incident. After an admitted noteworthy event, the Writer investigates source evidence and narrates precisely that event.
3. **World and tone neutrality:** Run nonhuman/abstract world without human anatomy/gear; run a quiet no-grand-plot profile without forced dramatic escalation; run a contrasting permissive adventurous profile without unearned mechanics.
4. **Canon and memory:** Writer materializes a consequential person/place only when needed; records one specific object exchange and promise; later reopens actual rooted sources and continues correctly while a private future possibility stays private.
5. **Fork under pressure:** At scene 37 with open work, parent advances to 80, fork 37, reentry starts with earlier clock/ownership/options/notes and source lineage; parent scene 80 future is not in child context. Child makes its own different decision; no doubled paid attempt.
6. **Bounded multi-round research:** One difficult scene uses the Writer Workdesk and (when granted) independent read-only child evidence; parent result shows correct citations/uncertainty; ungranted tool, revoked funding, budget exhaustion and uncertain provider delivery cannot leak facts/spend or write canon.
7. **Architecture and product landing:** Package graph and real app builds, auth/finance/owner negatives, no old wizard compulsory, no false ready, clean docs/branch/Change disposition; merge undertaking to main.
