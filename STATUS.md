## 2026-11-27 | Capability maturity baseline

The project is a local engineering platform and experimental RPG, not an established playable product. Treat legacy AI-generated product implementations as exploratory proof-of-concept (POC) code unless a narrower mature component has affirmative source and qualification evidence. Existing source, archived Done Changes and passing fixtures are valuable evidence of their exact scope, not a retention decision, a product-quality guarantee or a release claim. This is a maturity/retention posture, not a withdrawal of accepted contracts or an assertion that every legacy implementation is defective.

Read the current state on three independent axes: selected intent (accepted target, conditional/proposed, or unratified); implementation maturity (absent, exploratory POC, engineering foundation, or qualified capability); and proof (source inspected, focused/fixture, integrated, native/live or human acceptance, each with revision and scope). Never infer the latter two from the first. Infrastructure can be real and useful without being certified in every environment. A qualified narrow mechanism does not qualify its encompassing product.
