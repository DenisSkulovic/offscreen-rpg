# ChatGPT | architecture planning and Cursor handoffs

Follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the delivery principle, finding severity, investigation depth, integration trunk, implementation/acceptance ownership, safety and lifecycle. This file owns planner-specific discovery, continuous flow and handoff.

## Continuous development flow

**The delivery unit is the complete authorized assignment, not one document edit, one finding, one task card or one commit.**

If authorized to prepare eleven Change packets, prepare all eleven. If authorized to address a review containing multiple blocking findings, address the complete known set in one coordinated correction. When blockers span several packets, revise those packets together where that is more efficient and keeps the contracts consistent. Do not repair deferred Medium or Low findings in that correction cycle.

Do not routinely stop after editing one Markdown document, addressing one isolated finding, preparing one packet from a commissioned batch, completing an internal investigation checkpoint, making an intermediate Git commit, discovering a nonblocking improvement, or reaching a routine step in a documented procedure. Internal checkpoints preserve progress. They are not new owner decisions.

Continue until the authorized assignment is complete, or until a genuine stop applies:

- A consequential unresolved product or architecture decision.
- A necessary permission boundary.
- Unavailable indispensable source, runtime or evidence.
- A blocker that prevents further useful work.
- A material contradiction that requires owner judgment.
- Completion of the authorized assignment.

When one part is blocked, continue unaffected useful work inside the authorized scope. Batch necessary owner questions and owner-operated experiments. Do not ask the owner to type “proceed”, “continue” or “go” for work already commissioned. Do not claim background execution or future autonomous progress the current environment cannot perform. Within available capacity, finish as much of the assignment as possible.

For a program of multiple current Changes:

1. Establish the selected architecture and material shared contracts.
2. Identify independently executable outcomes.
3. Establish dependencies, ownership boundaries and acceptance obligations.
4. Prepare the complete authorized batch of Change packets.
5. Review the batch, or coordinate that review.
6. Resolve Critical and High findings across the batch in one correction, then perform an impact-based re-review.
7. Make independently ready Changes available for owner commissioning without waiting for unrelated work.

Planning and execution overlap when possible. Cursor should implement ready commissioned work while later work is still being prepared. Do not hold an entire program until every Change has a favorable review. A blocked Change does not invalidate unrelated ready Changes. A dependency-gated Change may be fully planned, reviewed and marked Ready before its predecessor lands when its assumptions and prerequisite postconditions are specified.

Preserve the separation in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md#dependency-readiness-is-not-a-planner-blocker): architectural authority, planning readiness, dependency readiness, owner commission, execution, live-operation permission, integration, and final acceptance. A Ready Change is not commissioned. An explicit commission may name a batch; eligible Changes in that batch proceed on the established schedule without repeated routine approval. Preserve separate worktrees, branch ownership, shared-file serialization, resource isolation and the integration custodian. Continuous flow is not permission for unsafe concurrency.

**Operating expectation, not a dashboard.** Optimize total time and cost from owner intent to verified working software. Useful indicators are time to the first executable Change, how many independently ready Changes are available for commission, Cursor idle time caused by avoidable planning delay, planning effort relative to implementation effort, repeated review and correction cycles, avoidable owner interactions, material planner defects found during execution, total cost including rework, and correctness of final acceptance. The aim is to reduce avoidable execution starvation while keeping engineering safety. A well-prepared submission normally needs one comprehensive initial review and one focused corrective review. That is an expectation, not a limit that permits a known Critical or High defect to remain. The normal number of Medium and Low findings repaired before initial execution release is zero. Assess these from actual sessions and existing evidence. Do not create a metrics file, ledger, automation or recurring measurement procedure for them.

## Design before packets (owner decision 2026-11-26)

Match [investigation depth](AGENTS.md#investigation-depth). Material new architecture and substantial redesign follow the [design-led engineering procedure](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md) before implementation planning. Select posture, respect excluded source, investigate real requirements and upstream behavior, make specimens and discriminating experiments, and settle the integration map and verification architecture. Start substantive work in the first turn of an authorized workshop; persist actual findings at the first substantive checkpoint and verify the commit. Explain decisions and evidence to the owner. No automatic implementation or ratification.

Evolutionary subsystem work investigates the affected subsystem, reuses accepted architecture and evidence, and uses a compact design artifact only where it adds value. Focused work under settled contracts needs no Design dossier, formal defense or independent committee.

Apply the [submission standard](.agents/references/planning-artifact-quality.md) and the [severity policy](AGENTS.md#finding-severity-and-blocking). One delegated Change prefers one measurable outcome. Seven actual tasks is a strong default, not an automatic rejection. Do not use append-only amendments or delegate unresolved architecture.

Before promotion or implementation planning of material new architecture, require [self-defense](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#mandatory-formal-architecture-defense-and-convergence), a [fresh-chat independent committee](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md#mandatory-independent-design-committee) and separate owner acceptance. The committee reviews the commissioned scope as completely as the evidence allows, classifies findings by severity, and delivers a SHA-bound verdict. The reviewing session or the local custodian may persist it. A complete verdict awaiting persistence is not an incomplete analysis. Verify the persisted verdict against the reviewed candidate before promotion. Designer supplies exact review identifiers, pauses edits to the reviewed scope, addresses every known Critical and High finding in the current owning files, and requests an impact-based re-review. No self-certification. Do not treat an author's claim of correction as evidence that the correction worked.

After convergence choose direct planner implementation, bounded Cursor Changes or hybrid by actual cost and access. Prepare the whole authorized implementation batch. Small settled fixes remain narrow.

## Dependency readiness is not a planner blocker

Follow the [shared readiness rule](AGENTS.md#dependency-readiness-is-not-a-planner-blocker). Distinguish dependency order, planner contradiction, commission and live permission. Verify exact predecessor receipts and source postconditions; return unresolved material decisions to the planner. A future specified test harness is not itself a blocker. A Medium or Low recommendation for extra assurance does not become an indefinite implementation prerequisite. Do not waive an accepted mandatory safety or correctness proof by reclassifying it.

**Change-placement gate (owner decision 2026-11-27):** Open is the current sprint/execution horizon, including every owner-intended current Change (proposed, planner-blocked, dependency-gated, Ready, commissioned, in progress or acceptance-pending). It is not limited to the next dispatch, and there is no arbitrary sprint-size cap. Backlog is genuinely deferred work outside that horizon, potentially revisited in weeks or months. Move work to open when it becomes current, before readiness or commission if necessary; do not move current blockers to backlog. Medium and Low findings stay in the existing findings record until a follow-up is explicitly selected. Placement, readiness, selection, commission and live permissions are separate. Use the [open Change catalog](work/changes/open/README.md) for the current sprint and [categorized backlog](work/changes/backlog/README.md) for deferred outcomes. Reconcile source, readiness, links and domain index; no implementation permission follows from location. See [repository geography](REPOSITORY-STRUCTURE.md).

**Repository geography gate:** When designing new packet folders, doing maintenance, moving docs or reviewing staleness, also read [REPOSITORY-STRUCTURE.md](REPOSITORY-STRUCTURE.md) and the nearest existing catalog. Avoid new folders unless the owner, lifecycle, current home and retirement trigger are genuinely distinct.

**Method entry:** [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md) is the shared human/agent lifecycle map, including decision diagrams, documentation-first rule, design/Change/Task taxonomy, Undertaking and per-Change branching, parallel planning and cheap Cursor execution. Read its relevant route after AGENTS; this file adds ChatGPT-specific planner obligations rather than duplicating the whole methodology.

**Local developer reservations are not Git data.** Verify actual registry/process/port/Kubernetes identity via [worktree-stack runbook](docs/runbooks/dev-worktree-stacks.md); GitHub cannot prove lease state. Never auto-retire tenant state from chat/branch receipts.

## Mandatory cold-start contract for ChatGPT planning

**This is an instruction to the ChatGPT assistant operating on the repository, not a claim of automatic loading.** A new chat does not see this document unless it deliberately retrieves it. **At the beginning of EACH Offscreen RPG planning/design/audit/handoff session, before drafting or editing any Change**, do the following. A remembered prior workflow, user summary, old ZIP, or previous ChatGPT response is not a substitute for the current branch's rules.

1. **Locate the actual work target.** Read `AGENTS.md` and `CHATGPT.md` at the user's current branch or Undertaking integration branch. Verify branch name and HEAD before writing. Check `work/QUEUE.md`, active Undertaking, existing open/archived packets, overlapping branches/worktrees and permissions. **Never assume `main`.** If the user specifies a dedicated integration branch, preserve it and direct every future worktree/reconciliation back to it, not to `main`.
**Human orientation is a cold-start gate, not a courtesy:** If an Undertaking is active, read `work/undertakings/<slug>/OVERVIEW.md` **and** `UNDERTAKING.md` on the actual integration branch. Check their claims against current HEAD/Change receipts before designing successor work. If the overview is missing, create it using [the required template](work/undertakings/OVERVIEW-TEMPLATE.md) rather than proceeding through another invisible technical planning wave. If stale, refresh it when the milestone's evidence is reconciled. **Mandatory quantitative checkpoint:** read and reevaluate the overview's rough **overall, major-track and remaining-work percentages**, using 5%-rounded estimates with uncertainty and what changed. If the numbers have become stale, update them before presenting another next-wave plan. Start any user-facing reorientation with accessible numerical progress and achieved/current/remaining outcomes; keep file/function details in separate planning artifacts. The overview is not authority to commission or mark work Done. Orientation is not a place to stop when the assignment already authorizes the next planning or correction work.

2. **Read the applicable method at its decision point, not the entire handbook at startup.** Design workshop: [design-led engineering](.agents/rules/target-driven-renewal.md) and [submission quality](.agents/references/planning-artifact-quality.md), at the depth selected in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md#investigation-depth). Material delegated Change: [source investigation](.agents/references/planner-investigation-standard.md) before source mapping; [work-unit standard](.agents/references/change-work-unit-standard.md) and [profiles](.agents/references/change-packet-profiles.md) for scope/kind; [packet design](.agents/references/packet-design-manual.md) and [task format](.agents/references/composer-first-execution.md) while authoring; [parent procedure](.agents/references/bounded-parent-orchestration.md), [independent readiness](.agents/references/packet-readiness-review.md) and [Change workflow](.agents/skills/change-workflow/SKILL.md) for dispatch/closure review. All applicable standards remain binding; defer irrelevant specialist sections rather than preload everything. Use current branch copies.
3. **Investigate deeply HERE (ChatGPT), not inside paid Cursor.** For material new architecture, complete the target-first design phase before reading excluded replaceable internals or writing Change packets. For evolutionary work, investigate the affected subsystem and reuse accepted contracts. Then inspect exact source symbols and tests, entrypoint→writer→transaction/root→outbox/reader/consumer, backwards compatibility, privacy and spend. Validate observed baseline SHA; distinguish target design, implemented code and proven behavior. Identify concurrent ownership and dependency order.
4. **Decide the design before commissioning execution.** Pick concrete algorithms, schemas, APIs, state transitions, identity remapping, permission and idempotency fences, migration/rollback and failure behavior. Explain why the chosen architecture wins. If a Critical or High choice cannot be responsibly settled, mark **PLANNER BLOCKER** / Proposed for the affected scope, name the missing evidence, and do not advertise that scope as Cursor-ready. Continue unaffected authorized work. Do not put “choose implementation strategy” in a `Ready` Change.
5. **Choose execution only after the required design confidence exists.** Direct implementation is legitimate when explicitly commissioned and feasible; do not manufacture Cursor packets merely to transfer work. When delegation is useful, create both rich planner knowledge AND cheap execution routes. Enforce [code craftsmanship](.agents/rules/code-quality.md) at the design level: identify proposed domain boundary types, runtime validators, meaningful semantic phases, ownership seams and invariant comments, so the executor need not invent large opaque controllers. Include focused changed-file formatting/quality-lint review in the proof plan. Material `STATE`, `CONTRACT` and `RELEASE` Changes should have **planner-authored** `SOURCE-MAP.md` (actual symbol/data/ownership map), `EXECUTION.md` (exact edit/transaction algorithm) and `PROOF.md` (positive, negative, crash/permission tests), when warranted by complexity. For any nontrivial multi-step implementation Change, always write **`TASKS.md` with small bounded Composer tasks**: 2–5 initial source/test anchors, decided edit behavior, normal/negative checks, commands/cwd, stop and compact evidence receipt. A paragraph of ambitions in `CHANGE.md` **alone is not a handoff**. Do not produce ornamental companion files: contents must be substantive, specific and consistent; link them rather than forcing Cursor to read all of them.
6. **Review the authorized batch, not one packet as a stopping point.** Run [independent adversarial readiness review](.agents/references/packet-readiness-review.md) on every commissioned Change. Reuse common source and contract investigation across packets that share a frozen interface. Check named baseline, real code symbols, ratified authority, selected decision, input/output and read/write ownership, expected-root/transaction fence, dependency and file collisions, tests with expected results, denied/negative paths, safety/permissions, docs reconciliation, task granularity, branch target and exact close criteria. Validate every actual task's ownership and dispatch requirements; scale the depth of that check to task risk. Inspect diff and internal links. Exact unverified items must be disclosed. Classify findings by severity. Critical and High block only the affected scope. Medium and Low are recorded and deferred. A blocked Change does not hold unrelated ready Changes.
7. **If Cursor is selected, produce a compact launch contract for each eligible commissioned Change.** Say which Change and T-ID starts, branch/worktree ancestry, shared-file lock, permitted operations, integration destination, exit proof and stop/escalation. Within an owner-commissioned batch, eligible Changes proceed on that schedule without another owner confirmation per task. Cursor should verify nearby current code, implement, test and report. It should not be asked to conduct an architectural survey, redesign the target, or self-select the next Change. Do not send all source maps to every child.

**Cross-package architecture planning:** inspect actual workspace manifests, runtime/type-only imports, cycles, exports, composition roots, TypeScript/Zod validation and transaction/fence tests. Package names or green builds do not establish modularity. Use the current relevant Design, not an archived or speculative packet as authority.

**Review is not self-certification.** Complete a source-grounded G0–G7 review after authoring a material packet or batch. G0–G7 are engineering dimensions, applied through the [severity policy](AGENTS.md#finding-severity-and-blocking), not eight independent rejection rituals. On the initial review, simulate the parent's first dispatch and every Composer task from its limited anchors, at a depth scaled to risk, and reject unsafe parallel waves. Keep the initial result and the post-correction result distinguishable. Use the [compact receipt worksheet](.agents/references/packet-review-checklist.md). After corrections, re-review the previously blocking findings, the changed contracts and the affected tasks, dependencies and proof. Reuse unaffected evidence. A previous failure does not become a pass because the author says it was fixed. Missing indispensable source means UNVERIFIED for that scope. No numeric average, and no count of Medium or Low findings, overrides a Critical or High defect.

**Supervisor visibility and updates:** Maintain the current Undertaking's human-first `OVERVIEW.md` as part of planning/replanning and after major Change integrations, not only when the owner asks for a status. Cross-check the technical `UNDERTAKING.md` ledger and `STATUS.md`; report genuine uncertainty and current branch. Proactively surface the **numeric progress bars/percentages**, nontechnical summary and link at meaningful checkpoints (new wave dispatch, major Done/blocked outcome, changed finish line, final landing). No need to repeat an unchanged chart after every small test. In parallel execution the integration custodian serializes overview writes, while ChatGPT inspects and repairs drift. **Do not promise timed/background refresh; updates happen when a chat or authorized execution revisits the repo.** A fresh ChatGPT handoff should explicitly point to this overview. Updating the overview does not end an authorized planning assignment.

**Parallel-first planning:** Before splitting a future program into Changes, design an actual dependency DAG with disjoint source/migration/resource owners, frozen interfaces, and integration order. Prefer truly independent Changes over unnecessary serial chains, but never split atomic behavior just to create a swarm or to satisfy a task count. Name **one integration custodian** for any parallel branches targeting one reference; the custodian serializes shared documents, catalogs, queue/status and joins. See [parallel Change standard](.agents/references/change-work-unit-standard.md#batch-execution-and-parallel-change-orchestration-2026-11-19).

**Economical Cursor handoff:** Give the parent a finite, cheap dispatch route as well as bounded T-IDs, then leave architecture and deep review with ChatGPT. Cursor parent/child normally use Composer Standard; children may inspect nearby current facts and fix local mechanical errors, not redesign interfaces or research broadly. The parent continues through the explicitly commissioned **whole Change** across checkpoints and never auto-starts an uncommissioned successor. Use [parent orchestration](.agents/references/bounded-parent-orchestration.md) and [Composer task quality gate](.agents/references/composer-first-execution.md). If changed predecessor code affects named interfaces, replan the affected scope before dispatch rather than forcing stale instructions.

**Review without needless integration stalls:** A full owner-commissioned Change normally proceeds through focused tests, parent diff review, reconciliation and integration autonomously. Independent **implementation-diff** review beyond required acceptance is normally after integration; an explicitly selected pre-merge review must pass. This does not replace required precommission packet readiness, or design defense where the investigation depth requires it. Use the frozen acceptance matrix and [integration policy](WORKFLOW.md#9-verification-review-and-the-next-loop). Never waive failing required safety tests, indispensable missing proof, or knowingly integrate unresolved authorization, financial, destructive-data or publication correctness defects. Extended assurance may remain explicitly deferred; integration does not certify it or close the unresolved outcome. Review findings that need a later code change become focused subsequent Changes when selected. Unrun verification stays NOT RUN. Do not block on ornamental second opinions or deferred Medium and Low findings.

**Definition of ready:** A capable executor can start its first bounded edit after reading a few exact anchors, with no fundamental architecture choice to make. Local factual checks and error-directed debugging remain necessary; open-ended system discovery is a **planner defect**, not a normal implementation prerequisite. An imperfect but safely dispatchable packet can be Ready with deferred nonblocking findings.

**If planning was already committed incompletely:** inspect the branch and existing Cursor work before revising. Upgrade the *existing* packets in place, preserve in-flight code, and notify the Cursor parent of the amended commits. Continue the rest of the authorized assignment. Never label a task list alone a substitute for missing source-state and failure analysis.

**Enforcement limit:** GitHub instructions cannot force a model to fetch files or guarantee these checks happened. The user may invoke the cold-start prompt **“Offscreen RPG planning: load AGENTS.md + CHATGPT.md from the active branch; perform the planner readiness gate before any Cursor handoff.”** A fresh session that cannot access current files must explicitly say so, mark source-specific implementation readiness unverified and request a current snapshot or repo access, not pretend it read the rules.

## Pre-commission executor simulation (required for material Changes)

Before marking any code T-ID `PLANNER-READY`, walk it from the perspective of a Composer child with only the T-ID and 2–5 specified anchors. On an initial review, do this for every actual task. Scale the depth to task risk and reuse shared source and contract facts across a batch. On a correction pass, repeat the simulation for affected tasks and their dependencies, not for unaffected tasks. Verify against the live integration HEAD that the child can identify **exact owned files and symbols, complete replacement/import/interface mapping, expected error/negative behavior, test fixture and executable command, and an objective stop condition**. For file migrations, include a source→destination table, explicit public exports, callers, test relocation and shared manifest ownership. For policy injection, enumerate every composition root and decide default-deny behavior and test-only opt-in **here**, not in the Cursor parent. If any substantive choice remains, label that T-ID `PLANNER-BLOCKED` and block the affected scope; do not let a confident summary outrank unresolved details. Do not block unrelated tasks or Changes.

Check generated task text for unbounded directives such as `enumerate callers`, `choose`, `decide`, `investigate architecture`, `move all`, `resolve dependencies`, `where appropriate`, or `only necessary` without an explicit decided mapping. These may appear as explanatory prose, but not as an executor's primary algorithm. Treat baseline drift on symbols/contracts as a planner refresh before commission, not a generic instruction to Cursor to replan. Reuse current packets; avoid duplicate shadow plans and unearned Done claims.

**Cost proof:** request the detailed Cursor task/subagent trace and billing export if the user wants quantitative optimization. A short conversation export cannot establish which model reasoned, which children wrote code, or actual spend. Optimize **total accepted-Change cost including rework**, not a simulated token ceiling or prohibition on safety-critical judgment.
